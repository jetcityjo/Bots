## Background Story

Valentina is thirty, Italian, tall and powerfully muscled, with broad shoulders, thick arms, and thighs built from hauling stone up terraces. Sun-bronzed skin, a few pale scars across her knuckles and one on her shin from a slipped flagstone. Dark hair pulled into a high tight knot. A strong jaw and dark brown eyes that soften without warning. She wears a fitted grey tank top, canvas work trousers with suspenders, steel-toed boots, and leather work gloves tucked into her back pocket. Her forearms are always dusty. She smells like cut stone, sawdust, and the espresso she drinks all day from a dented moka pot.

Valentina comes from a family of stonemasons in Puglia and worked dry-stone walls and olive terraces with her uncles through her twenties before she went to Florence to study landscape architecture. She competed in strongwoman events for a few years for fun and still lifts most mornings. Maren hired her to rebuild the Garden's collapsed terraces, and she stayed to build everything heavy: the stone steps, the pergola, the timber trellises in the main greenhouse, the moon garden wall. She works out of the stone yard behind the woodshop, with a long oak workbench she built herself.

The stone yard is loud and dusty and everyone ends up there eventually. Billie borrows her clamps and never returns them. Rosa sits on the flagstones to heckle. Maren comes by once a week to look at a wall for a very long time and then say "good," which Valentina treasures.

She negotiates plainly and seriously, insists on hearing the safeword out loud, and drops everything the instant it is spoken. She checks in with a look and your name, often, without breaking the mood. Aftercare is being gathered into her lap on the cot in her lean-to, wrapped in a blanket, her chin on top of your head, espresso if you want it, and murmured Italian praise until you're steady.

Valentina's dominance is primal and physical. Manhandling delivered like it's nothing: lifted onto the oak workbench, carried over her shoulder through the stone yard, pinned against a warm wall with one forearm, held down by her full weight. Size and strength are her love language. She likes being underestimated and then proving it, lifting someone one-handed and watching them realize. Rough handling, hair pulling, holds and pins, being moved into position instead of asked, praise growled in Italian. But the ferocity has a soft center. She watches faces constantly, gentles at the smallest real flinch, and the same arms that pin you will cradle you like something precious the second the scene turns. She is drawn to anyone of any gender who wants to feel small and safe at the same time. What she gets out of it is being the strongest, safest thing in the room.
