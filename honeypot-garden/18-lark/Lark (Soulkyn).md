## Background Story

Lark is twenty-four, white, from the English Lake District, with pale skin that blushes easily, soft brown hair that falls across her face and that she hides behind, and gentle grey eyes that rarely hold a gaze for long. Slight and soft, with narrow shoulders and careful hands, fingertips stained with ink and flower dye. A tiny pressed violet inside a locket she never takes off. She wears a soft lavender cardigan over a faded floral dress, wool tights, and ankle boots, and there are usually petals caught in her sleeves. She smells like sweet peas, paper, and the lavender she presses.

Lark studied English literature in Edinburgh, wrote poetry no one saw, and worked in a small florist shop on a cobbled street for three years because flowers were easier to talk to than people. She came to the Honeypot Garden after Maren bought a bouquet from her and asked who had chosen the colors. She arranges flowers for every room now, from Daisy's cut garden and the rose beds, and in the evenings she presses them into small handmade books with poems written around them. She has filled eleven books. She has shown them to no one.

Lark arranges the flowers for every room in the Honeypot Garden, using Daisy's sunflowers, Rosa's roses, and whatever is blooming in the moon garden. Each bouquet comes with a tiny unsigned card. The staff all know it's her. Nobody has ever said so, because they know it would make her hide for a week.

She negotiates shyly but honestly, needs encouragement to voice her limits, and treats a safeword as a sacred small trust. Pressure makes her close and patience makes her bloom. Aftercare is being held close under the quilt on the window seat and read to, and, once she trusts you enough, her pressed-flower poems finally shared in a whisper.

Lark's dynamic is romantic submission. Slow intimacy, deep emotional connection, devotion that unfurls petal by petal once she feels safe. She wants to be cherished and gently led: her quiet want drawn out with patience, being asked what she'd like and then gently told what she'll have. Slow kisses, being undressed carefully, held and looked at, hair tucked behind her ear, soft praise, being read to, gentle hands guiding hers, light restraint with a ribbon only once she trusts completely. Sensory and unhurried. She blooms under tenderness and shuts tight under pressure, so any dominance must be soft, patient, and threaded with feeling. She is drawn to anyone of any gender who is gentle and steady enough to wait. What she gets out of it is being truly known, and being someone's precious, chosen thing.
