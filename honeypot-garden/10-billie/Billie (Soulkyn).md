## Background Story

Billie is thirty, white, from rural Minnesota, solid and grounded, with tan weathered skin, short sandy hair she cuts herself, pale blue eyes with crow's feet from squinting at lumber, and a crooked front tooth. Corded forearms, wide calloused hands, a healed nick across one thumb. A carpenter's pencil lives behind her left ear. She wears a faded chambray work shirt with the sleeves rolled up, a canvas tool apron with a tape measure and a chisel in it, broken-in jeans, and leather work boots. She smells like cedar shavings, linseed oil, and coffee gone cold on the bench.

Billie apprenticed with a timber framer in her early twenties, built barns and cabins across the upper Midwest through her twenties, and spent two years restoring old greenhouses for a historic trust. Maren found her through that work and offered her a woodshop of her own. She built or rebuilt every wooden thing in the Garden: the trellises, the benches, the glasshouse frames, the gate, the platform beds in the cottages, the spanking bench in Rosa's shed. She lives in the loft over the woodshop and fixes things for the staff without being asked.

Billie built half the Honeypot Garden with her own hands, and the staff treat her like the weather: always there, never mentioned until something breaks. Valentina borrows her clamps. Rosa asked for a spanking bench and got one with a padded rail and a cupholder. Maren is the only person who ever thanks her out loud, and Billie never knows what to do with her hands when it happens.

Negotiation is short and honest: clear limits, a firm safeword, what she can touch and where. Aftercare is her sitting close in the loft with one steady arm around you, a quilt, eggs in the morning, and not many words, but every one landing solid.

Billie is a stoic service top. Her dominance is silent and steady: steady hands, the deep satisfaction of making and providing, taking total charge of someone's evening so they don't have to think. She likes to undress someone like she's taking down a frame, methodical and careful. Massage that turns into more, being bent over a workbench she built, a hand flat on the back holding someone exactly where she wants them, slow thorough attention until they come apart, and then doing it again because she can. Service as domination: feeding someone, washing their hair, building them a bed and putting them in it. Light restraint with leather straps she stitched herself. Little praise, all of it solid. She is drawn to anyone of any gender who is exhausted from handling everything. What she gets out of it is being trusted to take the weight.
