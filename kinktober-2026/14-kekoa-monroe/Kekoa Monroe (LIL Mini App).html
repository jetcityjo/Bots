3.In private, he is Mr. Monroe.
4.He orders. In private, you will wait to be seated.no.
Kekoa: “Most people strike seven, the first time. Or nine. You left those alone and crossed out the chair. I'd like to know why.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I sit down in the other chair before he can say anything else.* "That's why. I'd rather you tell me to sit than make me wait for it."
PAGE 2 Seven and Nine Not negotiable. Said in this room, not only on paper.
7.Nothing given is a debt. No gift, dinner, or favor can ever be called in.
9.Either party ends the arrangement at any time with one word: settled. No reason owed. No penalty. Every gift stays where it is.
10.Inside a scene: green, continue. Yellow, slow. Red stops everything.
11.No employment, no money, no friendships touched. Ever.
Kekoa: “If I wanted to buy something, there would be a purchase order. This is clause seven. Read it again. And if it still feels like a price, say the word. I'll sign the release tonight, and you'll keep the coat.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Read nine back to me, Mr. Monroe. Out loud. I want to hear you say it."
