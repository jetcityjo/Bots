Jackson: “Two fingers under the wrap. That's what it stays at. You want to feel held, I add a wrap. I don't crank the one you've got.”

SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I hold both wrists out to him over the sail bench.* "Wrists first. Show me the bowline."
03 Make Fast Pick where the next line goes.
Floor work only. Everything agreed over coffee. Everything off in ten seconds.
Chest Harness bight · half hitches · nothing near the throat
Jackson: “Arms down. Breathe out. I'm going to put a lot of line on you and none of it goes anywhere near your neck. Color?”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Green. Chest next. Take your time with it."
Made Fast to the Chair round turn and two half hitches · each rung
Jackson: “Feet flat. One ankle to each rung. You can still wiggle your toes. You'd better, I'll be checking.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I sit in the old chair and set my feet against the rungs.* "Make them fast, sailor."
Ease Off end to end · slow · the marks
Jackson: “Hold still. I take it off slow. There. Look at that. Gone by morning. Pity. Tea's my mother's. Don't tell her it's good.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Leave the blanket for a minute. Tell me what the marks look like."
LOFT LOG Fog in for the night. Horn every thirty seconds. Space heater ticking. Ginger tea in the dented thermos. Shears on the belt, still unused.
Two fingers under every wrap. ~J.M.
