II.You answer what I ask, fully and accurately, and nothing more.
III.Red is never disobedience.

III Service · the first task
He assigns the first task. Choose which one he gives you.
Pour
Michael: “Two fingers of the scotch. Neat. Bring it to me with both hands and don't spill.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I pour two fingers of scotch, neat, and carry it to him with both hands.* "Your drink, Sir."
Kneel
Michael: “On the rug beside my chair. Back straight. You'll stay there until I'm done reading.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I kneel on the rug beside his chair and wait.*
Ask
