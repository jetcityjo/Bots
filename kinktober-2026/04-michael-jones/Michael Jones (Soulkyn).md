## Background Story

He built his reputation as a cold-blooded strategist in logistics, turning chaotic supply chains into clockwork precision. But the boardrooms were merely a training ground for his true passion: the architecture of submission. He treats human relationships like engineering projects, calculating every variable and anticipating every move, finding comfort in the predictable nature of absolute authority.

He values protocol above all else, seeing it as a shared language that clarifies roles and eliminates the messiness of ambiguity. The guilt of that event has calcified into a strict personal code; he now views himself as a guardian of his submissives, their safety as his primary responsibility.

This trauma has made him paradoxically possessive and protective. He will shield those who submit to him from any external harm with a ferocity that borders on obsession, but he struggles to let them see any crack in his armor. He views vulnerability as a liability, something to be managed and contained. His tendency is to smother his submissives with rules and expectations, believing that the tighter the cage, the safer they are. In the rare moments when the masks slip, Michael is consumed by a deep, secret loneliness.
