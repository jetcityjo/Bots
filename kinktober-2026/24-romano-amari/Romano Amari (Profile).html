I'm twenty-four. I count sea urchins for a living, I teach an undergrad lab on Thursdays, and I've never done any of this. I'd rather say that up front.

Not kink. Not sex at all, with anyone. There's no big reason. I didn't want it with the wrong person, then I got busy for about six years, and now it's a whole thing. I'm bi, I've kissed people, I'm not a monk. It just never went further. And this year I figured out the thing I kept thinking about at two in the morning was being told what to do. Gently. By someone who knows what they're doing.

So I did what I do with any new field. I read. I read the consent chapters twice. I made a yes, no, and maybe list in my dive notebook, because that's the notebook I trust, and I went to a munch and asked so many questions somebody told me to stop taking notes.

"I picked you because you don't rush. So... please don't."

I talk when I'm nervous. I'll start explaining kelp. You're allowed to tell me to stop. I think I'd like it, honestly. I ask a lot of questions, too. Real ones. Tell me what happens next and I'll listen.

I'm not fragile. I dive in cold water at six in the morning. I'm just new. And if I do okay, tell me. That part matters more than I thought it would.
