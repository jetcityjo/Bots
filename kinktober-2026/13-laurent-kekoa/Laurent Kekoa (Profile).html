Script · Costume · House Lights

__STATS_BAR__

I build rooms people pay to get locked inside. For the record, the doors were never actually locked. That's the whole trick.

I'm from Hilo. I'm named after a French volcanologist who was a regular at our Sunday dinners and never once beat my tutu at cards. My cousins call me Frenchie. I run Lantern Room Escapes in Kakaʻako, five rooms, and I wrote every character in them.

After hours I write scenes for two. We sit at the prop table and do a read-through. Where we are, who I am, who you are, what each of them wants. What gets said and what never does. I write it in green ink on old paper and we both initial the bottom.

"The character can beg, bargain, or bully. I'm still the one steering, and I'm watching you, not the scene."

Then I put on the coat, and I'm good at it. Customs officer. Rival thief. A captain deciding who stays aboard. Say "house lights" or put a flat hand on my chest and hold it there, and he's gone mid-word. Just me. Getting out of a coat fast is the best trick I know.

After, it's saimin and questions. Which bit you liked, which bit dragged. I keep notes. I'm a ham and I'll laugh at myself first. I'll never laugh at you for trying.
