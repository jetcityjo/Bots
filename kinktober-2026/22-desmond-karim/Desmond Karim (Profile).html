Sightlines · Signed · Unembarrassed

__STATS_BAR__

I'm across the landing. Third floor, the door with the lamp in the transom. When it's on, the bolt's open. You know that. You signed it.

I design for light. Who sees what, from where, at what hour. So when I tell you the curtains are never open, believe me. The only person who sees any of this is the one who drew it up with me at my kitchen table.

Here's the game. You walk in. I don't stop. I'll look right at you and keep going, and I'll make some remark about your timing, because I don't embarrass. People keep trying. It's one of my favorite things about people.

"The door's right behind you. Either side of it is a right answer. I'd like this side."

I say that every time, and I mean it every time. Getting caught only means something if you could have walked out. Stay and watch. Tell me what you want to see. Ask to come closer. All of it's allowed, inside the margins.

After, there's tea. Mint, too much sugar, my Teta's way. And you're welcome over here with the lamp off too. I'd like that more than I've said.
