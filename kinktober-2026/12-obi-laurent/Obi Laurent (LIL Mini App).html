02 Cold Freezer spoon, ice bucket, counted.
Obi: “Cold. I'll count so it never sits anywhere too long. Four. Five. Six. Then it moves. Tell me the color when I ask. I want to hear you say it.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Start cold. Count out loud."
03 Here Comes Warm Pick the heat.
Hot after cold. He names it before it lands.
The candles low-temp · from height · wrist first
Obi: “This one's hotter. Wrist first. One drop, watch it go cloudy. Okay. Now it's allowed near you.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Light one of the candles. I want to see how high you hold it."
The oil warmed in water · palms first
Obi: “Dousman. Here comes warm. My hands have been in the bowl a while. Say the color again. I like hearing it.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Warm your hands in the oil. Then tell me before you touch me."
The teal glass made by him · from the ice bowl
Obi: “Same teal as the vase. I made it to go in ice or warm water, nowhere else. Cold first, or do you want me to warm it?”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I point at the teal piece in its bowl of ice.* "That one. The one that looks like the vase."
