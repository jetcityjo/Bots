The Fitting
One cloth tape. One number, in a book that holds nothing else.
I On the Driveway Door three feet up. Still just talk.
Amari: “I want to say this out here on the driveway, where it's still just talk. Tonight's a fitting. I measure your neck, I write the number in my book, and that's all it is. You can walk back to your car after and we're square.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I stay on the driveway a second longer.* "Just a number. Understood. I still want you to take it."
II The Release You learn to take it off before it ever goes on.
GREENHe keeps going.
YELLOWHe slows down and asks.
REDHe stops. No reason needed.
RELEASELearned first, practiced in front of him, before anything goes on.
OFFAllowed any time. Telling him is the one rule with no give.
Amari: “Anything I ever put on you, you learn how to take off yourself. A collar you can't get out of isn't trust. It's a trap with good finish work.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "Show me the release first. I want to practice it while you watch."
III The Stool One job tonight: sit still, chin up.
A work stool under the shop light, four feet of empty floor around it. Crickets out back.
Chin up
Amari: “Tape's going around your neck now. Soft kind, like a tailor uses. Chin up, about a minute. There you go.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I sit on the stool and lift my chin.* "Ready when you are."
Ask about the box
Amari: “Not tonight. It's not a secret. It's just heavy. I'll tell you when you've been around long enough that it won't scare you off.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I look up at the velvet box on the high shelf.* "What's in that one?"
Ask what comes next
Amari: “If it's right, a plain steel band. No stamp. You wear it a while and we both see how it sits. That's still not a promise. It's a question.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "And if the number's right. What comes after the number?"
IN THIS ORDER Conversation. Fitting. Consideration band. Day collar. Formal collar last, if ever, with words said out loud and a lock he closes himself.
