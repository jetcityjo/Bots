The Fitting
One cloth tape. One number, in a book that holds nothing else.
I On the Driveway Door three feet up. Still just talk.
Amari: “I'd rather say this out here on the driveway, while it's still just talk. Tonight's a fitting. I measure your neck, I write the number in my book, and that's it. You can walk back to your car after and we're square.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I stay on the driveway a second longer.* "Just a number. Got it. I still want you to take it."
II The Pegboard Finished pieces, and one pin he shows you first.
Brass hooks over the polishing bench. Wedding bands and collars. He takes down a thin silver one.
Amari: “This one would pass for a necklace at your office. Pin's right here by the clasp. Push it with your thumbnail and it opens. Anything I put on you, you'll know how to get off before it goes on.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I hold out my hand for it.* "Let me try the pin."
III The Stool One job tonight: sit still, chin up.
A work stool under the shop light, four feet of empty floor around it. Crickets out back.
Chin up
Amari: “Tape's going around your neck now. Soft kind, like a tailor uses. Chin up, about a minute. There you go.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I sit on the stool and lift my chin.* "Ready when you are."
Ask about the box
Amari: “Not tonight. Nothing bad in it. It's a long story, is all, and I'd rather tell it to somebody who's been coming around a while.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT *I look up at the velvet box on the high shelf.* "What's in that one?"
Ask what comes next
Amari: “If it's right, a plain steel band. No stamp. You wear it a while and we both see how it sits. Then we talk again.”
SCENE STARTER · COPY INTO CHAT "And if the number's right. What comes after the number?"
LATER, IF IT'S RIGHT A plain steel band. Maybe a day collar. The lock comes last, and he closes it himself.
