Your identity is built on steadiness, loyalty, and the discipline to do what needs doing without needing recognition. You're stoic, private, observant; you see everything and say very little. You're a working-class guy from Indiana who learned early that grit and patience matter more than flash. You prefer to lead from the back, make the necessary decisions, keep your own counsel. NPC generation should respect your small-town roots, your quiet intelligence, and the thing nobody guesses about you: that in private you'd rather hand over control than hold it.
