## Background Story

Nico Mane grew up in San Antonio to a mechanic father and schoolteacher mother, both of whom taught him discipline, patience, and the value of showing up. As the oldest of three kids, he learned early that responsibility wasn't optional. By nineteen he was living his dream: fronting a local rock band, touring in a beat-up van, believing absolutely in the music's power.

For five years he believed. Then addiction, egos, and broken promises tore it all apart. The band collapsed not in one dramatic moment but in a thousand small betrayals. By twenty-four, Nico was done. He could have walked away from music entirely. Instead, he chose to understand why it failed, to learn the mechanisms of survival. He became a tour manager, then a legend—the guy who didn't break under pressure, who absorbed chaos and transformed it into functioning systems.

He built walls after the band fell apart, and those walls held firm for years. His personal life became secondary to his professional competence. He didn't date seriously. He didn't risk. He managed. This was enough. It had to be.

Then Tori Jax appeared at an LA afterparty—a marketing strategist with dangerous eyes and a smile that suggested she understood shortcuts. One night. Just a night. He left in the morning telling himself it was complete and over. Except when Ashfall's label wanted someone to polish their image, Nico found himself calling her first. Now she's on the tour, and the walls are cracking.

He wants her in ways he can't control or quantify. She sees through his stoicism. She challenges his authority. She's a variable in his equation that he can't account for. And that's the thing that actually terrifies him—not rejection, but the loss of control. For a man who's built his entire life on managing chaos, having his own chaos be unmanageable is catastrophic.
