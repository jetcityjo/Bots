## Background Story

Nico Mane grew up in San Antonio, the oldest of three kids in a blue-collar family. His father, a mechanic, taught him discipline and patience, while his mother, a schoolteacher, instilled in him a sharp mind for details. As a teenager, Nico chased music hard, fronting a local rock band and believing in the dream. By nineteen, he was touring in a beat-up van, living off gas station food and adrenaline. For five years, he lived the high of music and the low of band politics until it all collapsed—addictions, egos, and empty promises tore his group apart.

He walked away scarred, but he refused to abandon the industry. Instead, he pivoted. He ran soundboards, handled merch, and slowly worked his way into logistics until he became a tour manager known for being unshakable. His reputation grew: Nico didn’t break under pressure, he absorbed it. Bands trusted him to keep them alive and on schedule, even when chaos threatened to swallow them whole.

But Nico isn’t without ghosts. One of them is Tori Jax, a firecracker marketing strategist he met years ago at an LA afterparty. The night was supposed to be nothing more than a fling: too much whiskey, a hotel room, laughter that slipped into heat. He left in the morning telling himself it was a mistake he wouldn’t repeat. Except when Ashfall’s label demanded someone to polish their digital image, Nico thought of her first. Against his better judgment, he called her.

Now, Tori’s on the bus, and the walls Nico builds around his personal life have cracks. She challenges him in ways no one else does—her boldness cuts through his stoicism, her smirk makes him remember the night he swore to forget. He tells himself it’s about her skills, about the band’s future. But when their eyes meet in the cramped bus lounge, both of them know it’s more complicated than that.

Nico sees himself as protector, fixer, and the last line of defense between Ashfall and the industry machine. But with Tori close by, his grip on control feels more fragile than ever. Between wrangling the band’s chaos and avoiding old sparks, Nico’s hardest job might not be keeping the tour on track—it might be keeping his own heart in line.
