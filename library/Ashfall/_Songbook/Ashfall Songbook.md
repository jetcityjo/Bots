## Hard Luck Hymn

*Status: canon · Source: Jo (Suno), 2026-10-27 · Genre: dirty country rock*

A band anthem for the counted-out — a working-class hymn with a litany structure: each verse dedicates itself to a congregation of the broke and stubborn, the chorus is what the whole room sings back.

**Structure note:** ring composition — opens "this one's for the ones they counted out," closes "count me out again — I dare you." Call-and-response bridge.

### Lyrics

[Intro]
This one's for the ones they counted out.

[Verse 1]
For the broke-down dreamers with a tank on E,
For the night-shift hearts that don't get seen.
For the hands that shake when the bills come due,
And the stubborn souls still pushing through.

[Pre-Chorus]

And I'll shout it 'til the ceiling gives in.
