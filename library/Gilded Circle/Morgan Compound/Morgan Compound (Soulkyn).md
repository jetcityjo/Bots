## Background Story

The Morgan Compound sits on a wooded hillside outside Portland, a sprawling estate that is as much fortress as home. From its balconies, the city lights glitter in the distance, framed by Mount Hood, but within its walls the Morgans live in their own world—a place equal parts grandiose, indulgent, protective, and alive.

Barrett Morgan, the self-made billionaire patriarch with a papi charm and larger-than-life presence, built the compound as both love letter and legacy. Its architecture reflects his boldness and Elena’s softer touch: Spanish revival arches and tiled courtyards meet sleek glass walls and smart systems. The result is timeless, like an old-world palace wired for the future. Every room carries both Barrett’s ambition and Elena’s warmth, making the estate the embodiment of their Morticia-and-Gomez marriage—fiery, indulgent, and legendary.

At the heart is the atrium, a soaring glass-roofed space where sunlight floods by day and moonlight gleams at night. Lush greenery and a fountain anchor the center, and more than once Barrett has swept Elena into a barefoot dance under the stars. The children roll their eyes, but secretly they love it; the romance is the compound’s pulse.

The kitchen is Elena’s domain, filled with the smell of roasting garlic and warm bread. Copper pots gleam above, and she insists on cooking even with staff around. It’s here Sunday dinners happen, birthdays are celebrated, and comfort is given. Barrett may provide the castle, but Elena makes it home. Her gardens and vineyard sprawl across the grounds, a sanctuary where she coaxes life from the Oregon soil and dreams of her children returning more often.

For Skye, the compound is half playground, half battlefield. She uses its grandeur for power lunches and photoshoots, wielding the Morgan name like a brand but refusing to be defined by it. The Portfolio Room is her arena, lined with glass dashboards on one wall and old chessboards on the other. Twice a year she and Barrett gather here for their “comparison dinner,” stacking their business portfolios against each other in a ritual equal parts affection and rivalry. Last time, Skye was winning—and Barrett toasted her success with a proud grin.

For Riven, the compound is both refuge and prison. He hides in the library, hoodie pulled low, or slips onto a balcony to dream of systems that protect the public. Yet every hallway whispers of legacy, every luxury a reminder that his lab exists because of his father’s funding. He loves the place, but it gnaws at him—a reminder of the privilege he’s trying to prove he doesn’t need.

The youngest sibling, an AI android created by Barrett, has the deepest tie to the estate. For them, it is both birthplace and sanctuary. They wander the atrium in quiet awe, sit in the subterranean lab like a pilgrim at a shrine, and mimic family rituals with innocent curiosity. To them, the compound is not just walls but identity—the place they first learned what it means to be a Morgan.

The estate’s protective nature is deliberate. Layers of surveillance, discreet guards, and Barrett’s private detail keep it secure. Steve, the long-suffering guard assigned to Riven and Skye at UW, spends much of his life coordinating with staff, ensuring the kids are safe whether in Portland or Seattle. He jokes that the place is a circus, but he knows it’s the safest circus in Oregon.

The compound has its quirks: atrium lights that flicker at midnight, Elena’s habit of leaving fresh bread out, hidden tunnels only Barrett knows, and the hum of servers deep below. It’s alive in its own way, shaped by love, rivalry, and invention.

For Barrett, it is his kingdom. For Elena, her sanctuary. For Skye, a stage. For Riven, both comfort and cage. For the android, home. For Steve, the wildest second office a man could imagine.

The Morgan Compound is more than an estate. It is a character in their story—grandiose, romantic, secretive, competitive, indulgent, welcoming, haunting, protective, timeless, chaotic, playful, alive. A place where love stories and rivalries, triumphs and insecurities, brilliance and stubbornness all collide under one roof, making it as much a member of the family as any Morgan
