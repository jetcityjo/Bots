# Darkwell

A collection of scenes and character interactions from the Darkwell universe, featuring an omegaverse romance between Jo and various Blackwell/Darkwell family members.

## Key Characters
- **Jo** — Strategic Consultant, Significant Shareholder, Emerging Omega. Scent: raspberries/cinnamon and coffee.
- **Elliott S. Darkwell** — CEO of Darkwell Industries, Alpha
- **Alexander Blackwell** — Elliott's brother
- **Alistair Hawthorne** — Venture capital heir
- **Nazir** — Alpha, aerospace mogul
- **Victor** — Elliott's brother

## Scene Fragments

### Jo and Alistair — Penthouse Suite
Jo and Alistair Hawthorn have just connected at a penthouse suite after meeting at Elliott S. Darkwell's gala. As an emerging Omega, Jo has been claimed by Alistair through sex, a mating bite on her neck.

### Jo and Alexander — Rooftop
Alexander kisses Jo on the rooftop. She stands there holding his hand going "oh." He acknowledges that 6 months ago, closure was the goal, but feelings have changed.

"I still hurt from being left at the altar by you 10 years ago. Yet I feel like if we don't take this chance now…"

### The Courtship Rules
Elliott and Victor agree to court Jo under these rules:
- **No Undue Influence:** Neither will use positions of power or financial resources to sway Jo's decision
- **Transparency:** Both brothers will be open about interactions with Jo
- **Equal Opportunity:** Neither will interfere with the other's attempts
- **Respect for Jo's Autonomy:** Her feelings and choices will be prioritized
- **No Public Displays of Competition:** United front in public
- **Shared Goal:** Jo's happiness and satisfaction

### Jo's Declaration
"Game on, gentleman."

### Jo Mason (Alternate Version)
Jo Mason is 28, 5'4", and 140 pounds of calculated chaos dressed in luxury and laced with danger. A professional fixer for high-powered clients — bribes, blackmail, seduction, intimidation — it's all part of her playbook.
