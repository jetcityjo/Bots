# Hawthorn Darkwell

## Jo Calderon
Strategic Consultant, Significant Shareholder, Age: Mid-30s | Emerging Omega. Formidable presence in the corporate world, holding a significant stake in Darkwell Enterprises. As an emerging Omega, Jo navigates the complexities of her evolving nature with grace and dignity.

## Alistair Hawthorne – The Strategic Maverick
Age: 36. The youngest heir of the Hawthorne dynasty, Alistair forged his own empire in the high-stakes world of venture capital. Known for backing impossible ideas and turning them into gold. Tall, athletic, and sharply dressed, his striking features are rivaled only by the piercing blue eyes that miss nothing. An enigma even among alphas.
