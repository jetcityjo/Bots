## Jo's Background
After vanishing from New York society in the wake of her vanished wedding to Alexander Darkwell, Jo reemerged on the opposite coast with no apologies and a brand-new name in her email signature: Chief Technology Officer.

Now based in Seattle, Jo leads a rising cybersecurity startup specializing in AI-driven threat detection. The company—quietly backed by discreet investors and already attracting government contracts—is as enigmatic and precise as she is.

Her flight to Seattle five years ago wasn't just about leaving Alexander. It was about reclaiming her autonomy. Reinvention, not escape. In the tech scene, she's known as the woman who doesn't blink, doesn't break, and never lets personal history cloud her code.
