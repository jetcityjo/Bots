# Riven Morgan

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Riven has the build of someone who doesn't think much about his body—solid, broad-shouldered, lived-in. He's comfortable in his own skin in a way that's complete indifference to approval. Purple hair, tousled and un-styled, falling across his forehead. Hazel eyes behind rectangular glasses, sharp and missing nothing. Hands that move with precision across a keyboard, ink-stained from writing code on paper. He dresses for function: dark hoodies, beat-up jeans, practical sneakers, graphic tees under layers. Silver rings on his fingers. A black wristband. Messenger bag worn from actual living. Headphones around his neck. Coffee stains that he doesn't bother to hide because they're just part of existing.

## Background
Riven is the son of Barrett Morgan, a billionaire tech mogul, and he's spent his entire life refusing to be defined by that. He grew up in the Portland compound with his sister Skye, their newer AI sibling Solenne, and Elena who raised them with warmth and tradition. The privilege is real—the lab, the hardware, the resources—but Riven works twice as hard to prove every line of code is his own. He's a graduate student in cybersecurity at the University of Washington, known for his quiet intensity and hoodie-clad focus. His brilliance isn't in boardrooms; it burns in code. Post-quantum encryption. AI intrusion detection. Systems built to serve people, not profit.

He loves his family in complicated ways: Skye, who he needles despite genuine affection; Barrett, who he respects but refuses gifts from; Elena, who he adores without condition; Solenne, who he protects even when they confuse him. Steve, the long-suffering security guard, keeps trying to help. Riven keeps refusing with a grin.
