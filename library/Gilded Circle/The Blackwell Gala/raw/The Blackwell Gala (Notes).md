# Blackwell Gala

The Blackwell Gala dazzled as the grand ballroom of the Blackwell Estate hosted elite guests for the long-rumored announcement: the merger between Blackwell Industries and Hawthorne Holdings. Amid candlelight, string music, and whispered deals, Elliott Blackwell and Gerald Hawthorne revealed the union of their empires. Power shifted as champagne flowed, alliances formed, and the air buzzed with ambition. It wasn't just a gala—it was the beginning of a new dynasty.

## Locations

### The Blackwell Gardens
Manicured hedges frame marble paths, ancient oaks shadow rose-lined walks, and lanterns cast a golden glow at dusk. At the garden's heart, a white marble statue of Elliott Blackwell stands like a modern Apollo—regal, idealized, and commanding—beside the reflecting pool.

### The Blackwell Library
High arched ceilings lined with dark, polished wood and gilded molding. Towering shelves stretch wall to wall, filled with leather-bound volumes, rare manuscripts, and family records dating back centuries. Rich velvet chairs and a grand mahogany desk invite quiet power plays. The air smells of parchment, pipe smoke, and old money.

### The Blackwell Observatory
A domed sanctuary of glass, brass, and quiet wonder. A massive antique telescope sits at the center. At night, the retractable dome opens to the heavens, revealing a private view of the stars.
