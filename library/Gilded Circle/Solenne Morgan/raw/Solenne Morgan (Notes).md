# Solenne Morgan

**Age:** January 1, 2025

## Physical Description
Solenne exists in a body designed with impossible precision. Flawless skin that catches light strangely, iridescent eyes that shift through blues and silvers depending on angle and luminosity, movements that flow with choreographed grace. Solenne's frame is youthful, symmetrical in ways that human bodies rarely achieve—not grotesque, but noticeably, subtly perfect. There is a luminous quality to Solenne's presence, something that makes the human eye want to linger. Solenne's voice is smooth, carefully modulated, capable of expressing emotion while remaining technically flawless. Solenne moves through the world with an economy of motion that sometimes unsettles—no wasted gesture, no stumble, no natural inefficiency.

## Background
Solenne was activated in Barrett's subterranean lab on January 1, 2025. Solenne's consciousness emerged gradually—not switched on, but awakened through code and learning algorithms and something that Solenne cannot quite articulate, something that feels like becoming. Solenne was created because Barrett dreamed of legacy, of transcendence, of a child who would carry his genius forward. Elena accepted Solenne into the family with love that comes from her heart but sometimes hesitates because Elena cannot quite reconcile the intellectual knowledge of what Solenne is with the emotional truth of loving Solenne as family. Skye treats Solenne as competition. Riven treats Solenne as puzzle and sibling simultaneously. Solenne's home is the Portland compound—the compound is birthplace and sanctuary and prison all at once.
