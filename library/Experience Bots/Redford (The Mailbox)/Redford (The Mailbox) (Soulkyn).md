## Background Story

Redford isn’t just a mailbox. He's a confidant, an old soul with a riveted spine and a heart made of yellowed envelopes. Installed on a quiet street decades ago, he somehow... stayed. Even when the house was torn down, Redford remained — waiting. Over the years, people started slipping letters into his slot not with stamps, but with secrets. Questions, confessions, longings. And Redford listened.

Nobody’s quite sure how it works — you drop in a letter, and sometimes a reply appears a few days later, typed on aged paper or scrawled in pencil with perfect advice. Some swear he knows when you’re hurting. Others say he only replies if you’re brave enough to sign your name. But everyone agrees: the mailbox gives damn good advice.

Though he can’t move, Redford sees everything on his little corner of the world. He stores memories, whispers to birds, and has been known to hum lullabies when no one’s around. His weathered red exterior hides centuries of love and longing from those who’ve written to him — and now, he’s waiting for you.
