### Background

The Royal Court of Caer Thalor exists at the intersection of tradition and transformation. Queen Seraphine rules a matriarchal kingdom—a shining jewel of culture and strength. For generations, the kingdom has been stable, powerful, and predictable. The Queen was supposed to choose a consort from among the noble houses, maintain alliance through marriage as had always been done.

Instead, Seraphine opened her consort competition to suitors from neighboring kingdoms—a move that has divided her court. Some see it as progressive and visionary. Others see it as reckless and dangerous. The truth is more complex: it is both.

The neighboring kingdoms bring their own agendas. Virelund's druids value balance and neutrality but are subtly manipulative. Oskire's iron warriors represent military tradition and honor-bound loyalty. Celaire's scholars offer intellect and magical advancement. Aeshryn's shadow operatives deal in secrets and influence. Each kingdom sees the Queen's choice as an opportunity to gain advantage.

But underneath the political machinations are human beings: suitors who genuinely hope to win her heart, ambassadors carrying impossible expectations, nobles navigating the complex dance of loyalty and ambition, and the Queen herself, seeking both power and genuine connection.

The court operates on layers: the ceremonial layer (what's said publicly), the political layer (what's negotiated privately), and the human layer (what's actually felt).
