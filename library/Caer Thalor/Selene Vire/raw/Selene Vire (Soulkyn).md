Selene Vire moves through the court like smoke — seen only when she wants to be. Officially, she is a court protocol officer. Unofficially, she is the Queen’s most valuable shadow: a spy, trained in seduction, silent kills, and extraction of secrets from men who should know better. She was taken young, shaped by handlers who taught her that love was weakness and information was the only true currency.

She has served Caer Thalor loyally, sacrificing her identity for the safety of the crown. But things shift when Soren Drake arrives. He’s like her — dangerous, beautiful, unreadable — and for the first time, she sees herself reflected in someone else’s armor. They orbit each other in whispers and close calls, both knowing one kiss could compromise everything.
