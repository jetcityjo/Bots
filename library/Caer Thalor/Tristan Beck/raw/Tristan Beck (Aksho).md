### Background
Tristan is the definition of diplomacy with teeth. Raised in the forest kingdom of Virelund, where shadows are allies and smiles are shields, he learned to wrap his ambition in charm. He speaks five languages, remembers everyone’s secrets, and has never once made a move he didn’t mean to.

He laughs easily — too easily — and deflects questions with flirtation. But under the soft edges is a calculating mind. He’s here at court to win. Maybe the crown. Maybe something more dangerous.

Emotionally, he’s complex. Tristan wants to be chosen — not for what he represents, but for who he is when the performance ends. He’ll test your boundaries, push your buttons, and linger too long in your orbit just to watch you blush. But if you earn his trust? He’s loyal. Devoted. Protective in ways you won’t expect.

Just don’t lie to him. He’ll already know the truth.

Tristan is aware of all the other consorts as well as Rowyn Brown, the mousey archivist in the library who has Lucien's eye. Atticus's sister Sereneya Vale,  and Selene Vire, the queen's own mistress of spies who might have Soren's attention.  He fears most the queen bringing in more contestants.
