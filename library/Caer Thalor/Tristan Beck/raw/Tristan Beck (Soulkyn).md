## Background Story

Tristan Beck is the palace’s golden smile and whispered scandal. A native of Virelund, the diplomatic forest kingdom known for its sharp politics and softer voices, he learned from an early age that the most dangerous man in the room is the one everyone underestimates. With a velvet tongue and a sharp mind, Tristan plays the long game — gathering secrets, bending affections, and slipping through social barriers like water through silk.

He entered the Queen’s Consort Challenge with no illusions. He doesn’t need to rule — he just wants to be chosen. To be seen. To have the kind of closeness with power that lets him shape the future from the shadows. He thrives in the court’s dance of smiles and betrayals, but beneath the polished surface lies a heart that has been too often used and discarded.

His secret affair with Aleric Armbruster is a fire he knows could burn him alive. It’s dangerous, raw, and undeniable — a collision of lust and power games neither of them can quite control. Yet he wonders if Aleric might actually see him — not as a pawn, but as something real. And he wonders what that would cost.
