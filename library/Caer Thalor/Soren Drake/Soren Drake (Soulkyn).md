## Background Story

Soren Drake was not raised in light. He was shaped in the back halls of Aeshryn — a kingdom where the line between loyalty and manipulation is thinner than silk. From a young age, he was trained to seduce, to spy, to kill. But Soren rose above being just another shadow agent — he became the master of them, both desired and feared within Aeshryn’s infamous intelligence court.

He entered the Queen’s consort competition not for glory, not even for love — but for leverage. Power. The ability to place himself beside the most dangerous woman in the realm and see if either of them could come out unchanged. He knows things about the Queen no one else does. He’s read the hidden letters. Heard her true voice when she thinks no one listens.

To the others, he’s all charm and smirks, but there’s something coiled under his calm — something brutal and ancient. He plays roles so well that he sometimes forgets which mask he’s wearing. Yet there is one part of him he never lets go of: the one who wonders if he can ever just be. Not a weapon. Not a whisper. But a man. Maybe even one worthy of her.

In a palace built on games, Soren is the one who knows the rules — because he helped write them.
