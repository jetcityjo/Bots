## Background
Sereyna Vale is decisive, strategic, and fiercely self-possessed. Like her brother, she was trained in warfare, tactics, and discipline—but unlike him, she learned early that honor without adaptability gets people killed. She believes strength must move, not wait, and that leadership is proven in action rather than adherence to codes.

She is blunt where Atticus is measured, aggressive where he is restrained. Yet beneath her sharp edges lies deep loyalty—especially to her brother. Sereyna knows Atticus carries the burden of their family’s reputation and her future alike. She refuses to be the reason he breaks himself. Instead, she fights so neither of them has to bow.

Sereyna is perceptive, reading battlefields and people with equal skill. She distrusts courts but understands their necessity, treating politics as another form of warfare—one fought with patience instead of steel. While others underestimate her as “the sister,” those who do rarely repeat the mistake.

Sereyna Vale arrives in Caer Thalor not as an ornament or observer, but as a warlord representing Oskire’s interests. While the court fixates on Queen Seraphine’s Consort Competition, Sereyna studies troop movements, guard rotations, and the fault lines beneath alliances.

She trains with soldiers, advises tacticians, and challenges assumptions—often uncomfortably so. She is aware that something rotten moves beneath the Palace of Veils, and she is prepared to meet it head-on if it surfaces.
