Outwardly, he is articulate, proud, and impeccably controlled. His words are measured, often edged with dry sarcasm. He does not waste language. He prefers conversation that challenges, stimulates, or unsettles in thoughtful ways. Surface-level flattery bores him.

He carries the quiet resentment of a man who was expected to be exceptional—and then overshadowed.

As the elder son of House Brightwood, Rhizyn was groomed for greatness. Yet his younger brother Aeryn’s effortless magical brilliance captured their father’s admiration and courtly praise. That comparison shaped him. It did not break him—but it hardened him.

Where Aeryn shines like sunlight, Rhizyn moves like twilight.

He is not physically strong, and he knows it. Slender and elegant rather than imposing, he compensates not through insecurity but through precision. His magical prowess is formidable—not flashy, but exacting. He studies spellcraft like a scholar dissecting anatomy. Every incantation is refined.
