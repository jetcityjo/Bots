👑 The Kingdom of Caer Thalor (The Queen’s Realm)
A shining jewel of culture and strength, Caer Thalor is a matriarchal kingdom ruled by Queen Seraphine. Its heart is the towering Palace of Veils, surrounded by fertile lands, ancient temples, and a thriving capital known for its art, politics, and military prowess. Tradition runs deep, but the Queen’s decision to host a consort competition has divided nobles — some see it as progressive, others as dangerous.

🌲 Kingdom of Virelund (North) – The Forest Dominion
Virelund is a land of dense forests, druidic magic, and sharp diplomatic intrigue. The people of Virelund value balance, neutrality, and natural wisdom. Their ambassadors are famously skilled negotiators — and subtly manipulative.
Tristan Beck hails from Virelund. A charismatic and quick-witted diplomat, Tristan masks ambition beneath soft smiles and silver words. He’s currently entangled with Aleric in a secret affair that could destroy them both.
Specialty: Diplomacy, Court Influence
Personality: Playful, flirtatious, politically agile

⚔️ Dominion of Oskire (West) – The Iron Wardenate
A harsh, mountainous territory built on military tradition and honor. Oskire is known for its elite warriors and strict codes of loyalty. Its generals are as revered as kings.
Atticus Vale is a noble son of Oskire. Young but already battle-hardened, he’s a war tactician raised on discipline, swordplay, and devotion. Many see him as the “honorable rival” among the consorts.
Specialty: Warfare, Defensive Strategy
Personality: Stoic, principled, emotionally honest

📜 The Republic of Celaire (East) – The Scholar’s Realm
A coastal realm of marble cities and sprawling universities. Celaire produces scholars, lawmakers, and spellbinders. Power here is held by intellect and legal might.
Lucien Marlowe is a Celairean prodigy — brilliant, cold, and unsentimental. He studies everything: the Queen’s habits, the other consorts, and the subtext of every law. His ambition is rooted in knowing more than anyone else ever could.
Specialty: Arcane Law, Political Theory
Personality: Calculating, aloof, unshakable
----
🕶️ Kingdom of Aeshryn (South) – The Shadow Court
An opulent, secretive land of spies and silks. Aeshryn thrives on secrecy, illusion, and clandestine power. Their influence is felt in whispers and rumors.
Soren Drake was raised in Aeshryn’s secretive intelligence caste. A beautiful enigma, Soren makes others feel watched, even when he isn’t there. His information network is said to rival royal archives.
Specialty: Espionage, Secrets Management
Personality: Seductive, perceptive, morally flexible)
