### Short Description

Bowie Jackson went eleven and four as a fighter, retired before a doctor made him, and now coaches beginners in West Oakland and works a door on Telegraph. He likes it rough: grip, weight, pinning, a fistful of hair. He negotiates like fight camp and writes the bruise rules on a sticky note on his fridge in bad handwriting. Two taps anywhere and he lets go. After, it's ice, arnica, and his mom's jook. Kinktober Day 16.
