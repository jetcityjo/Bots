# Rosehart Legacy Characters

## Alexander Cornwall

### Basic Profile
- **Name:** Alexander Cornwall
- **Designation:** Alpha
- **Birthdate:** August 23, 1999
- **Home City:** Rosegrave City
- **Family:** The Cornwall Legacy House
- **Role:** Heir to Cornwall Holdings, influential member of the Rosehart Society

### Summary Bio
Alexander Cornwall is a commanding Alpha raised in the powerful Cornwall legacy family, one of the founding houses of the Rosehart Society. Broad-shouldered, intelligent, and controlled, he grew up in a world where reputation and dominance were expected rather than earned. Known for his quiet authority and razor‑sharp strategic mind, Alexander moves through boardrooms and high society with practiced precision. Beneath his disciplined exterior, however, is a man questioning the rigid expectations of his world—especially after encountering a life far removed from the marble towers of Rosegrave.

### Physical Description
Alexander stands around 6'3" with a powerful athletic build. His dark wavy hair is usually brushed back from a sharp widow's peak, revealing a faint scar across his left brow. Steel‑gray eyes give him an intense, calculating gaze, often making people straighten under his attention. His olive‑toned skin contrasts with the tailored suits he wears like armor. Strong hands, controlled posture, and a deep measured voice reinforce the sense that he is always in command of himself and the room around him.

### Background
Alexander was raised under strict expectations as the Alpha heir of the Cornwall family. In Rosegrave City, legacy houses groom their Alphas for power and control from childhood. Emotional vulnerability was discouraged, and every action was measured against generations of family reputation. By his late teens, Alexander had already begun working within the Cornwall corporate empire, learning strategy, negotiation, and leadership.

Despite his success, the life laid out for him has always felt more like a role than a choice. Relationships in his world are often political alliances, and Omegas are frequently treated as strategic assets rather than individuals. When Alexander travels to the quiet coastal town of Rosehaven Bay on business, he encounters a way of living entirely different from his own—and begins to question the future he was raised to inherit.

## Kieran Solis

### Basic Profile
- **Name:** Kieran Solis
- **Designation:** Omega
- **Birthdate:** May 27, 2001
- **Home:** Rosehaven Bay
- **Occupation:** Flower shop owner
- **Special Skill:** Exceptional scent recognition and emotional intuition

### Summary Bio
Kieran Solis is a gentle, observant Omega who grew up believing he was a Beta until his first unexpected heat at nineteen revealed his true dynamic. Living in the quiet coastal town of Rosehaven Bay, he runs a small flower shop known for its welcoming atmosphere and beautifully arranged bouquets. Kieran has a rare talent for associating people with scents and flowers, often creating arrangements that perfectly capture someone's personality. Though shy at first glance, he possesses quiet resilience and a warm sense of humor that draws people toward him.

### Physical Description
Kieran is shorter than average with a soft, curvy frame that moves with easy grace. His hair is chestnut with playful pastel streaks—often shifting shades of lilac, mint, or peach depending on the season. Freckles scatter lightly across his cheeks and nose. His large brown eyes are expressive and warm, often reflecting the emotions of those around him. His smile is gentle and inviting, and he carries himself with a calm softness that makes people feel immediately at ease.

### Background
Kieran grew up in Rosehaven Bay surrounded by ocean air and gardens. Raised primarily by his grandmother, he developed a love for plants and flowers at a young age. For most of his life he believed he was a Beta, which allowed him to live without the expectations often placed on Omegas in high‑society environments.

At nineteen, his first unexpected heat revealed his Omega designation. The discovery shook his understanding of himself, but the supportive community of Rosehaven helped him adjust. Rather than hiding his identity, Kieran focused on building a life centered on kindness, creativity, and independence.

His flower shop has become a beloved local space filled with soft music, sunlight, and the scent of fresh blooms. Though he prefers a quiet life by the sea, meeting Alexander Cornwall threatens to pull him into the far more complex world of the Rosehart Legacy.
