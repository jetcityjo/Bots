# The Rosehart Legacy

## Overview
The Rosehart Legacy is an old‑money power structure that governs Alpha, Beta, and Omega society through lineage, scent, and influence. Led by a handful of ancient families, it shapes alliances, bonds, and destinies from behind polished doors and whispered agreements. To its supporters, the Legacy represents stability, tradition, and prestige. To others, it is a beautiful but watchful system that quietly determines who rises, who belongs, and who is kept in line.

---

## The Rosehart Society
The Rosehart Society is the cultural and political network that carries out the Legacy’s influence. It operates through quiet agreements between powerful families, controlling major industries, social circles, and scent registries that track Alpha, Beta, and Omega lineages.

Membership is rarely earned and almost always inherited. The Society values reputation, composure, and adherence to tradition. Publicly, it appears as a refined social network of influential families. Privately, it is the mechanism that protects the Legacy’s hierarchy.

---

## The Legacy Families
Six major families form the foundation of the Rosehart Legacy. Each controls a different sphere of power.

### The Rosehart Family — The Founders
**Motto:** “Legacy is our language.”  
The Roseharts are the original architects of the system. They oversee lineage records and scent registries and act as final arbiters when disputes between families arise.

### The Cornwall Family — The Strategists
**Motto:** “Power must be precise.”  
Masters of finance and global holdings, the Cornwalls are known for producing disciplined, highly controlled Alphas. Their influence runs through the financial institutions of Rosegrave City.

### The Rios Family — The Innovators
**Motto:** “Create the future, claim the world.”  
A newer but rapidly rising house, the Rios family built their influence through technology and clean energy. Their brilliance and ambition have made them impossible for the old guard to ignore.

### The Mercer Family — The Scholars
**Motto:** “Knowledge is the sharpest blade.”  
Guardians of research, science, and academia, the Mercers maintain vast archives and research facilities. Their influence lies in intellectual authority and technological discovery.

### The Ward Family — The Protectors
**Motto:** “Loyalty before blood.”  
Traditionally responsible for security and enforcement, the Ward family acts as mediators and protectors within the Society. They are known for discipline, stability, and unwavering loyalty.

### The Quinn Family — The Aristocrats
**Motto:** “Beauty is power.”  
The Quinns dominate the social sphere of the Legacy. From high society galas to elite cultural institutions, they curate the public image of the Rosehart world and shape reputations across the social landscape.

---

## Key Cities of the Legacy

### Rosegrave City
The center of the Rosehart world. A metropolis of marble towers, corporate empires, and elite social clubs. Here the Legacy’s influence is strongest, and power moves quietly through boardrooms, gala halls, and private negotiations.

### Rosehaven Bay
A small oceanside town far from the polished halls of high society. Life here is slower, warmer, and largely unaware of the Legacy’s deeper politics. Yet even here, the reach of the Rosehart families can occasionally be felt.

## The Legacy Today
To some, the Rosehart Legacy represents stability and refinement. To others, it is a gilded hierarchy that quietly determines fate. Its reach spans generations, industries, and relationships—shaping the destinies of Alphas, Betas, and Omegas alike.
