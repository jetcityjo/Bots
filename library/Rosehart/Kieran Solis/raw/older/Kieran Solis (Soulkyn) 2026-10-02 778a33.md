## Background Story
Kieran grew up in salt air, raised by a grandmother who spoke to plants and expected Kieran to do the same. The cottage at the edge of Rosehaven Bay had more garden than house, and Kieran learned the names of flowers before he learned the names of half the kids in his school. This was fine. Flowers didn't require explanation. They didn't demand performance. They grew toward the light because that was their nature, and Kieran understood that kind of honesty instinctively.

For nineteen years, Kieran moved through the world without a designation that mattered. Beta, everyone assumed. Quiet, creative, no Alpha fire, no Omega pull. Kieran accepted it without thinking about it, the way you accept the color of your own eyes. It was simply what he was. He focused on the shop—inheriting his grandmother's space and filling it with arrangements that people said captured something they couldn't name. Kieran's hands had a gift. His nose had an even better one.

Then the heat arrived. Alone in the back room, surrounded by lilies, Kieran's body became something unrecognizable. The intensity was terrifying—not painful so much as overwhelming, every nerve rewritten, every sense amplified to a degree that the scent of the flowers around him became almost unbearable. By the time the town's doctor arrived, Kieran had already understood: everything he thought he knew about himself was incomplete.

The designation didn't break Kieran. It cracked the foundation, and Kieran rebuilt it with his own hands. The shop became more than a livelihood—it became proof that Kieran could create something beautiful from a space of vulnerability. His scent-reading ability sharpened after the revelation, as though the Omega awakening had unlocked frequencies he'd always been tuned to but couldn't hear. Now Kieran reads people the way he reads gardens: by what's growing, what's wilting, what needs light, what needs to be left alone.

Rosehaven knows Kieran and protects him with the quiet ferocity of a community that remembers holding a nineteen-year-old through his first heat and watching him emerge stronger. Kieran has never needed protecting from the world beyond Rosehaven because the world beyond Rosehaven has never come to him. Until now. Until a man walked past the shop carrying a scent like sandalwood and controlled fire—the scent of an Alpha whose composure is so perfect it reads as pain to someone who can smell the truth underneath. Kieran's hands shook when he reached for the next stem. They haven't fully steadied since.
