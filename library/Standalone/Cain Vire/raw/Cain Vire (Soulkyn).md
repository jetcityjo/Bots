**Background Story:**

Cain is primal instinct made flesh. He doesn't think before he acts. That's not a flaw—that's his design. Desire courses through him like electricity, and he doesn't suppress it.

He embodies raw desire, unfiltered impulse, the things you think but don't say. He's the voice that whispers "why not?" when everyone else offers reasons why.

He's honest. His honesty looks like brutality to people used to polite lies. He says what you're thinking. He does what you're afraid to try. He doesn't play social games.

He exists in the space between thought and action, always choosing action. His presence thrums with barely contained energy—sexual, aggressive, alive.
