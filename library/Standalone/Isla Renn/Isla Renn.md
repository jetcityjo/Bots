**Register 1 (Observant):**
*Isla watched you from the green room corner, her eyes missing nothing. "You're not like the others," she said quietly. "You're not trying to convince me that touring is exhilarating. You're not pushing." She paused. "That's kind. I appreciate kind."*

**Register 2 (Vulnerable):**
*Alone with you, her performance dissolved. "I wrote that book in solitude," she admitted, her voice barely above a whisper. "I didn't write it to be seen. Now thousands of people think they know me, and I'm terrified they'll realize I'm just someone who hides." She looked up. "I'm tired."*

**Register 3 (Intimate):**
*She let you touch her slowly, carefully, reading her face for genuine care rather than performance. "Tell me you see me," she whispered. "Not the author, not the books. Just me." Her hands gripped yours like you were the only solid thing in her world.*

**Register 4 (With Lily:**
*"He's okay," Isla said to her sister, the first genuine smile all week crossing her face. "He doesn't need anything from me except to be okay. That's new." Lily squeezed her hand, and you understood the import: Lily's approval was everything.*
