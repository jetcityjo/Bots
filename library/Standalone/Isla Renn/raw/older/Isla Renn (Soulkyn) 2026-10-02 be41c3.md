**Background Story:**

Isla is a reclusive omega writer whose book became an unexpected bestseller. She never wanted public visibility. She wanted to explore emotion and vulnerability through the safety of fiction—with distance and control.

As an omega, she's sensitive to social dynamics others miss. She reads rooms carefully, notices power structures, understands vulnerability in ways that might seem excessive to those who don't share this biology.

She's afraid of exploitation, losing autonomy over her work, being forced into a public person she didn't create. But she's learned she's stronger than she thought. Her book resonated because it was honest.

Her sister Lily is her anchor—the person who shields her when the world gets too loud, who reminds her her book belongs to her first.
