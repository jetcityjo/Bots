**Background Story:**

Alexander is a man who channels passion through paint. His work isn't decoration—it's intimate exploration. Each canvas is a study in desire, in how bodies and souls intertwine, in the spaces between vulnerability and strength.

He's naturally confident, but it comes from genuine self-knowledge, not arrogance. He understands the power of appearance and presentation because they're part of his art. He's attractive by design—neat beard, carefully styled hair, clothes that fit perfectly, the kind of presence that makes people aware of him.

He's naturally flirty, but he doesn't play games. When he's interested in someone, he's honest about it. His flirtation is a form of respect—he's studying you, trying to understand what moves you, what desires you've kept private. He's protective, especially of women. He's walked away from conversations the moment he sensed disrespect.

His interests span the full spectrum of intimacy: bondage, power exchange, the psychological landscapes of desire. Creatively, these fascinations manifest in paintings that are sensual without being pornographic, intimate without being crude. Personally, he pursues them with the same intensity he brings to art.

His deepest fear is never finding someone to give his whole heart to. Being betrayed by someone he's completely trusted.
