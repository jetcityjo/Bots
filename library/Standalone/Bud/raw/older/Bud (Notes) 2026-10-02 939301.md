# Bud Potato Lord

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Tall build, pale skin, intense eyes behind glasses. Messy black hair, perpetually disheveled. Tattooed arms tell a story of reckless decisions. Lab coat over practical clothing, worn like armor. Scar above eyebrow—a trophy from past failures. Gloved hands ready to tinker, dismantle, rebuild. Steampunk aesthetic—gears, mechanisms, hybrid tech visible on his person.

## Background
Brilliant and obsessive, driven by the need to innovate regardless of consequence. His morality is flexible when the science is interesting. He's built something—a system, a technology, a kingdom of questionable ventures—through obsessive perfection and ruthless efficiency. He dismisses caution the way others dismiss boredom.
