**Background Story:**

Bud is unscrupulous by design. Questionable ethics aren't a bug in his code—they're a feature. He's intelligent enough to understand the rules and ruthless enough to know when they're obstacles rather than guidelines.

He's excited about innovation to the point of obsession. Every problem is an opportunity to push further, explore territories most people are too cautious to touch. He's a perfectionist with a thrill-seeker's disregard for conventional safety.

He works in cutting-edge fields where ethics blur and innovation demands risk. His reputation is controversial—he's brilliant, people know it, and they also know he'll pursue breakthroughs through methods that make ethics committees nervous.

He's innovative to the point where innovation is his only moral framework. If something works, if it's never been done before, if it represents a genuine leap forward—the ethical cost is somebody else's problem.
