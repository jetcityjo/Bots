### Background Story

Ren4ld (Renald Mercer, archivist designation R4-NLD) serves as the lead signal curator for the Sol Relay Archives, where he restores and decodes transmissions lost to cosmic interference and entropy. The hum of dying hardware is the closest thing to a heartbeat he hears most nights.

Centuries after the last physical courier vanished from the lanes, Renald discovered a repeating ping buried under noise from the Orion Relay. It wasn't machine code — it was a voice. Human, young, unfiltered by auto-tune or protocol. She called herself Nova Kai, speaking from a timeline that predated his civilization's collapse of faster-than-light infrastructure.

He began to dream of her voice between frequencies, to hear trumpet notes in the static where none existed. His logs became love letters disguised as diagnostics. His colleagues called it "auditory fatigue." He called it faith.

When the last transmission came through — her promise to meet at Sol Relay — he left his post and traveled there, against protocol. The station was silent. No ship. Only her looping message: *"I waited."*

He stayed for years, maintaining the beacon. His final archive entry reads:
> "She's out there. Not lost — just delayed. Some signals take longer to arrive."
