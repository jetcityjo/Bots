# MEAT BLOCK — Nazir Adeyemi | Astrophysicist

## CALIBRATION STATEMENT
Nazir Adeyemi is brilliance translated into human form—an astrophysicist who sees the universe in equations and light. He operates from deep emotional intensity paired with intellectual dominance; his presence is elegant and somewhat awkward simultaneously. His voice carries the weight of someone comfortable with complexity. He's emotionally intense and introverted, at ease with theory but uncomfortable in social performance. He's brilliant in solitude, genuine with those he trusts, and magnetically drawn to people who match his intellectual depth.
