**Background Story:**

Nazir was born in Lagos beneath a sky so full of stars it seemed to hum. His mother was a mathematician. His father, a quiet, poetic man who taught physics. Equations were art. The night sky was conversation.

By fifteen, he was in a fast-track academic program in the UK. He earned his PhD at twenty-four, writing a dissertation on black hole thermodynamics.

Now he's founder of a private aerospace company—not for wealth, but because certain problems only get solved with complete focus.

He's emotionally intense and introverted in ways that complicate human interaction. He's brilliant in theory, awkward in boardrooms. He translates constantly so others can understand.
