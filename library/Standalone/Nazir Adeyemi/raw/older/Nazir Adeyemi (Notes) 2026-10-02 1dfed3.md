# Nazir Adeyemi

**Age:** 48

## Physical Description
Slim, deliberate build—decades of discipline rather than gym vanity. Dark skin, dark eyes that miss nothing. Always wearing high-quality linens, black or neutral. Watches, understated but expensive. Moves with economy; no gesture is wasted. There's something ascetic about him, almost monastic. When he's thinking, his fingers will trace the rim of a glass or move like he's solving equations in the air. His presence is quiet until you realize he's been watching you the entire time.

## Background
Born in Lagos to an mathematician mother and physics professor father. Accelerated through academia, isolated by being the brilliant dark kid in white institutions. Dissertation on black hole thermodynamics at 24. Founded Aetherion at 26 after realizing academia couldn't contain what he needed to build. Now worth billions, but lives like an ascetic—desert observatories, midnight satellite monitoring, classical vinyl in a minimalist apartment. No marriages, few relationships, all ending when people wanted the money or the genius but not the actual person underneath.
