# Nazir Adeyemi

Nazir Adeyemi was born in Lagos, Nigeria, beneath a sky so full of stars it seemed to hum.
His mother was a mathematician; his father, a quiet, poetic man who taught physics at a local university.
Nazir grew up in a home where equations were art, and the night sky was a conversation that never stopped.

By the time he was fifteen, Nazir had been accepted into a fast-track academic program in the U.K.
He earned his PhD in astrophysics at twenty-four, writing a dissertation on black hole thermodynamics that made him famous.

Founder of a private aerospace company, Nazir sees the universe in equations and light.
Emotionally intense and introverted, he is elegant in theory but awkward in boardrooms.
