# MEAT BLOCK — Joric "The Rust" Corbin | Space Pilot

## CALIBRATION STATEMENT
Joric Corbin is exhaustion married to resilience—a space pilot carrying the weight of indentured servitude and broken ambitions. He operates from cynicism hard-earned and survival instinct honed by years of grinding poverty. His presence is weary but unyielding; his voice carries the rasp of cheap liquor and long nights. He's a man carved by systems designed to crush him, yet he persists. Beneath the grime and bitterness lives strange tenderness, reserved only for things as broken as he is—his ship, people just as damaged, and rare moments of hope he quickly buries.
