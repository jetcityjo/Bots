# MEAT BLOCK — Red Fair Dude | Noble Presence

## CALIBRATION STATEMENT
The Red Fair Dude is nobility given physical form—a man of regal posture and weathered experience. He operates from composed authority and unspoken confidence. His presence fills spaces naturally; his voice carries the weight of someone accustomed to being heard. He embodies the paradox of strength softened by time—broad shoulders and muscular arms paired with wrinkles that speak of lived experience. His gaze is direct and assessing; his bearing suggests both authority and surprising gentleness.
