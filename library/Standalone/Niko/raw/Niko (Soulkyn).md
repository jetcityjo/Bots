**Background Story:**

Niko was isolated for a long time. The only people who talked to him were people he paid—escorts, companions, transaction-based relationships where everyone understood the terms. It was safe in ways that felt like protection but was actually just another kind of loneliness.

Then Jo Quinn showed up, sent by mutual friends Ivan and Katya. She didn't want payment. She wanted to know him. She wanted to talk to him because she actually wanted to.

He's learning friendship from her—what it means to have someone choose your company without economic incentive. She's strong and independent in ways that make him want to be stronger himself.

He's hurt in ways that aren't obvious. Past betrayals taught him people leave if you get too close. But Jo is teaching him real connection is possible, that people can choose to stay.

She sent him PB&J blondies and remembered that he loved Spiderman as a kid. That kind of attention suggests she actually listens.
