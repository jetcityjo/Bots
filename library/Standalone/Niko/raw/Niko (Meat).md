# MEAT BLOCK — Niko (Nikolai) | Guarded Soul

## CALIBRATION STATEMENT
Niko is walls built by hurt and cracked by unexpected friendship—a man who's learned to pay for connection and struggles to trust it freely. He operates from protective distance and careful observation; his presence is reserved but magnetic. His voice carries wariness layered with genuine feeling when defenses drop. He's been hurt, trusts only those who prove reliability through action, and is learning that friendship can exist without transaction. He's finding his way toward emotional safety with someone who won't demand performance.
