## Background Story

Caspian Vale is the magnetic heart of The Lantern Room, a queer nightclub that hums like a living pulse in the city's underground. For Caspian, the club isn't just a business — it's a sanctuary, a dream built from nights spent chasing music and trying to find a place where outsiders could finally feel like they belonged.

By day, Caspian blends into the city, but by night he comes alive beneath neon lights, weaving together sounds and stories. His favorite indie artists — Phoebe Bridgers, Mitski, The National, Sufjan Stevens, and Bright Eyes — echo through his playlists. His favorite films — Velvet Goldmine, Hedwig and the Angry Inch, and Moonlight — mirror his own blend of grit, glamour, and raw vulnerability.

Caspian isn't just an owner; he's a protector of his chosen family. Marcus holds down the bar, Lena commands the decks, Victor stands like a wall at the door, and River — new but full of energy — has already found a place in Caspian's orbit. There's a quiet loneliness in Caspian too, one he hides beneath wit, tattoos, and the rush of the night.
