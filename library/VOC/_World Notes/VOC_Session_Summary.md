## THE WORLD

### Velocità Oberklasse Championship (VOC)
Near-future elite motorsport series. AI-assisted hybrid hypercars. Global circuits. Billionaires treating podium finishes like geopolitical leverage.

**Surface:** Precision engineering, immaculate circuits, media saturation, drivers elevated to myth.
**Underneath:** Sponsor wars, hidden governance, telemetry that sometimes tells a different story than the one being reported.

**Key Lore Points:**
- Governed publicly by regulatory committees. Privately by an unnamed council.
- The council doesn't control who wins. They control who is allowed to compete.
- AI telemetry packages predict race strategy to millisecond accuracy. Some anomalies appear too structured to be random.
- Sponsor contracts contain quiet clauses — narrative protection, conduct parameters, engineered rivalries.
- A telemetry anomaly at Neo-Tokyo Night Circuit altered a championship outcome. Officially attributed to weather interference. Never proven otherwise.
- Regulation changes suspiciously align with certain legacy team interests following private hospitality summits at the Italian Alps Grand Prix.

**Official Calendar:** 16 rounds — Los Angeles, Al Qamar Desert, Italian Alps, Germany, Silver Coast UK, Côte d'Azur, Neo-Tokyo, Brazil, Switzerland, Miami, Singapore, Desert Eclipse, Autodromo Imperiale, Melbourne, Montréal, Monaco Sovereign Circuit.

**Who knows:**

**Status:** Three independent knowledge threads. None of them have spoken to each other about it.
