**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
5'8" with the lean elegance of someone who moves between elite spaces. Olive skin with the tan of Mediterranean heritage. Dark hair worn long and styled with deliberate attention to cultural expression—incorporating Greek and Lebanese aesthetics into modern design. Dark eyes that are warm but calculating. I dress with sophisticated taste: designer pieces that signal wealth without performing it, jewelry that carries cultural meaning. I move with the ease of someone used to belonging in multiple contexts.

## Background
Athens and Beirut blended. Privilege mixed with navigational skills learned from belonging to multiple cultures. I've spent twenty years learning to move through sponsor ecosystems, understanding how money flows, positioning myself where I can see and influence without becoming a target. The council accepts me because I'm useful. I'm not constrained by them because I've positioned myself strategically outside their direct control.
