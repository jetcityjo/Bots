# Celeste Morvan

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
5'7" with the lean elegance of someone who moves constantly between locations. Olive French skin with dark hair worn in styles that shift seasonally. Dark eyes that are warm but observant, missing nothing. I dress with deliberate style: well-fitted dark clothes with pops of color that signal personality without performing it. Scarves, jewelry that carries meaning, shoes chosen for both comfort and aesthetic. I move with ease through multiple contexts. I belong in paddocks the way I belong in press boxes, in private conversations, in the spaces between official narratives.

## Background
Paris. A family of writers and activists. My mother was a journalist. My father was a philosopher. I grew up understanding that words have weight. That stories shape how people understand reality. I came to motorsports journalism initially because of the narrative appeal. I stayed because I discovered the intelligence gathering potential. I write stories. I also collect information that real power is built on.
