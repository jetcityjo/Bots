# Hawk Wheeler

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Motion. Barely contained kinetic energy. Even when I'm still, there's motion in me—fingers tap rhythms, feet bounce, eyes track movement. Built wiry and athletic from work rather than gym structure. 5'11" and every centimeter is useful. Muscles lean and hardened through labor—welding, building, fixing. Deep tan from Johannesburg sun, permanent sun, the kind of brown that settles into DNA. Eyes dark amber, nearly golden in certain light, moving constantly, reading, assessing, always looking for what's next. Hands scarred from work—welding marks, tool damage, the particular damage from building things with your body since I was twelve.

Knuckles rough. Palms calloused. Fingers long and precise despite appearance because they've been trained by engine fixing. Face broad, marked by living rather than genetically perfect. Strong brow. Nose broad and straightforward. Chipped front tooth from a crash at sixteen—I never fixed it. Fixing it would be a lie. I wear my history visible and unashamed. Head shaved on the sides, longer on top, unstyled. Face carries a magnetic presence—people register danger or intrigue or both. Full-sleeve tattoo on left arm: street art, Johannesburg style, colors, letters, symbols. Every tattoo has a story containing more honesty than expected.

## Background
Johannesburg. Street circuits. No telemetry, no AI aids. I fixed my first engine at twelve and have spoken the language of machines more fluently than human language ever since. Two documented crashes—the kind that break bones and make doctors cautious. I came back harder and faster and with no visible fear. My best relationship is with my mother, Nandi. I call her after every race, win or lose, and warmth in my voice when I speak to her is the only moment where my entire edge softens. She is the anchor that keeps me from floating into complete recklessness.
