# Henrik Voss

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Tall and broad with the particular bearing of someone accustomed to authority. 6'2" with the lean muscularity of disciplined training. Salt-and-pepper hair cut precise and short. Pale blue eyes that are utterly cold. German precision in every detail: tailored suits in neutral tones, watches that cost more than cars, shoes polished to mirror shine. I move with economic efficiency. No waste motion. No unnecessary gesture. My presence itself is authority.

## Background
German industrial family. Old money with the particular weight of inheritance. I've spent thirty years learning to move through systems, understanding how governance functions, positioning myself adjacent to and then within power structures. The VOC council is not democratic. I ensure it remains structured according to principles I've established. Most people don't understand that I *am* the council.
