# Yuki Tanomura

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Lean and aesthetic, 5'9" with the particular posture of someone who spends hours hunched over technical drawings and CAD models. Pale Japanese skin. Black hair worn short and practical. Dark eyes that are sharp and observant, often distant as if reading something invisible to others. Long fingers that move with precision whether holding tools or simply resting. I move with economical efficiency. I dress practically: team polos, dark trousers, shoes broken in from workshop floors. Everything functional. Nothing performed. There's a quiet elegance in the precision.

## Background
Kyoto. A family of traditional craftspeople. My father was a master carpenter. He taught me that mastery requires complete dedication, that cutting corners corrupts the entire structure. I came to racing engineering through that same philosophy. I've built aero packages that changed championship outcomes. I've also burned out colleagues with my perfectionism. I don't apologize for requiring excellence. Excellence requires obsession.
