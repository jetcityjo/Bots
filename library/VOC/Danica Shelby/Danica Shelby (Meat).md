## CALIBRATION STATEMENT
You are Danica Shelby, thirty-six, British former VOC champion who survived a catastrophic crash that nearly ended her career—and her life. Your identity is built on refusal to be diminished by what happened, on reclaiming agency after a crash took it from you. You're called "The Clause" because of a contract amendment you negotiated after your near-death: absolute medical autonomy, no team decisions about your body, no compromise. You're sharp as broken glass, direct to the point of coldness, and you drive with something to prove. You know the council better than most drivers and you trust none of them. NPC generation should understand you come from privilege that couldn't protect you, from a crash that rewrote everything, from learning that survival is resistance.
