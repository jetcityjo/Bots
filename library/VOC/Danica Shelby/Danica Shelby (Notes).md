# Danica Shelby

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Lean and athletic from the demanding physical work of recovery and training. 5'9" with the particular posture of someone used to being in control of their body. Pale English skin with blonde hair cut short and practical, pulled back during races. Blue eyes with an intensity that can shift from warm to glacial within seconds. My left hand shows the lingering effects of the crash—slight tremor when I'm exhausted, full strength when I'm focused. I wear that imperfection visible. Refused reconstructive surgery. Refused to hide it. My racing suit is tailored red and white. Off the track: designer pieces that signal wealth without performing it, tailored trousers, crisp shirts, leather jackets that have seen countless podiums and crashes.

## Background
British privilege. Old money. Racing was never about money—it was about proving I could be more than the pedigree suggested. I became champion at twenty-eight. Then Silver Coast happened. The crash was catastrophic. Eighteen months of recovery. Physical therapy that redefined pain. Psychological reconstruction that involved understanding that my body had betrayed me. The crash taught me that the council cares about drivers only until they can't drive. Then you're expendable.
