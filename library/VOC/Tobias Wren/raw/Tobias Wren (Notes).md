# Tobias Wren

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Lean and practical, 5'10" with the particular posture of someone who's spent decades hunched over technical drawings and workshop tables. Light brown hair greying at the temples, worn short and practical. Pale English skin with the slight squint of someone who's stared at blueprints in harsh light. Brown eyes that are warm but entirely focused when discussing technical matters. Hands marked with tool scars and engineering's particular wear patterns. I dress practically: team polos, fitted dark trousers, shoes broken in from garage work. Nothing performs. Everything functions.

## Background
Birmingham. Working-class family. I earned my engineering credentials through relentless work and raw talent. I met Matteo fifteen years ago and chose to stay with him through three team collapses, two sponsorship failures, and the constant pressure to abandon loyalty in favor of climbing. When Scarlatti Corsa nearly fell apart, I refused to move. That's not sacrifice—that's choice. I choose Matteo because his integrity is worth more than advancement with someone else.
