# Sable Ouédraogo

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Wiry and compact, 5'8" with the particular muscularity of someone built from intensity rather than traditional training. Dark skin with Burkinabé heritage clear in features. Black hair kept short and practical. Dark eyes that are sharp and constantly assessing. I move with kinetic energy barely contained. My racing suit is tailored. Off-track: practical clothes, nothing expensive, nothing that wastes resources. Everything I wear is functional.

## Background
Ouagadougou then Paris. A mother who cleaned houses and sacrificed everything for my racing. Street circuits. Natural talent that couldn't be denied. Sponsorship that changed everything. Now I'm the fastest in the VOC, which means I'm also the most visible, which means the council is watching very carefully.
