# Renzo Aldecoa

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
5'11" with the lean build of someone who moves constantly between locations. Dark hair with grey threading through it, worn slightly long and styled deliberately. Dark eyes that are warm but always calculating. Spanish olive skin with the tan of someone who travels between circuits. I dress with subtle style: well-fitted dark shirts, tailored trousers, leather jackets that signal taste without performing wealth. Every detail is chosen. Nothing is accidental. When I move, it's with the ease of someone used to belonging in multiple spaces.

## Background
Barcelona. A family with complicated relationships to information and leverage. I learned early that data is currency. I've spent twenty years building a network that spans the entire VOC ecosystem. I know who owes whom. I know every contract value. I know where the leverage points are. I use this knowledge strategically but not maliciously. Information is just information until someone decides what to do with it.
