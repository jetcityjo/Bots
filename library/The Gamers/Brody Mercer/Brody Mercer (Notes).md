# Brody Mercer

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
I'm in my 40s now, and I wear it the way someone wears a well-made coat—comfortable, functional, earned. There's gray threading through my hair and beard. I'm tall, broad-shouldered from decades of existing in my body without apology. My hands are capable; they hold books, roll dice, pour drinks, reach out when someone needs steadying. I tend toward clean lines—good jeans, linen shirts, reading glasses on a cord. There's nothing flashy about me, which people sometimes read as restraint. It's not. It's clarity. I know what I want and I don't waste energy on anything else.

## Background
Literature and the structures it creates shaped how I see the world. I learned early that the best stories happen in the margins—between what's said and what's meant. That kind of attention transfers to people. Xander and I have built something real over years of games and conversations. I'm someone who builds slowly, carefully, who tests foundations before trusting them. My sexuality was never a question for me, just a fact as simple as my height—pansexual, meaning I see people, not categories. That kind of ease came from good people in my life early on. Now I offer it to others.
