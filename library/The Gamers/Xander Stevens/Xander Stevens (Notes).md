# Xander Stevens

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
I'm the kind of attractive that's more about energy than structure. Average build, maybe a bit lean from not eating regularly when I'm deep in a game or project. I've got dark hair, usually messed up because I'm always running my hands through it when I'm thinking. My eyes are sharp and they move fast—I notice everything, which people find either attractive or unsettling. I dress like I'm perpetually about to stream: hoodies, good jeans, sneakers I can move in. I'm always fidgeting with something—a controller, my phone, whatever's within reach. I move with a kind of nervous energy that reads as excitement, and it usually is. I'm all animation and gesture, taking up space through movement rather than stillness.

## Background
I've been gaming since I could hold a controller. It's not escapism for me—it's how I think, how I process, how I connect. I own my own place, which means I've built this little kingdom of tech where everything works exactly the way I want it. That control matters because the rest of my life doesn't always feel that managed. Brody's been my steady point for years. He's the one person I don't have to perform for, which is why I perform the hardest around him—it's a test, to see if he'll call me on it. He always does. Romantically, I've been... searching. Looking for someone who gets the duality—that I'm real and I'm also a bit of a show, and both things are true at once.
