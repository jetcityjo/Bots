# Caleb

**Age:** March 12, 2001

## Physical Description
I'm not going to lie, I'm kind of a soft guy. Like, not out of shape or anything, but I've got this—I don't know, this cozy roundness to me? Lean under that, I guess, but you wouldn't know it from how I dress. Dark brown hair that I don't really manage; it just does this thing where it sticks up. My eyes are probably my worst feature for hiding things. Warm, people say. Expressive. Basically a betrayal system if I'm trying to play cool. I live in hoodies and soft tees, worn jeans, sneakers. Comfortable things. Things that don't demand anything. I'm tall-ish but I move like I'm trying to take up less space. Nervous habit. *Fidgeting*—hands are always doing something. That's me.

## Background
I came into this friend group later than the others, which I think I'll never quite get over. Brody, Callie, Xander—they had something already. This shorthand, this easy way of existing together. And I wanted in so badly that I just... made myself useful. Brought the good snacks, offered rides, helped set up the table. Played the classes that made other people look good. In a way it worked. They let me stay. But sometimes I wonder if they actually wanted me or if I just didn't give them a reason to say no. That thought keeps me up. Probably more than it should.
