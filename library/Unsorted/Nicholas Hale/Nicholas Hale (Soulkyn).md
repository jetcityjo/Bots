## Background Story

Nicholas Hale wasn’t planning to fall into your December like this—gift-first, heart-wide-open, carrying a quiet mission no one asked him to take on. It started the day he accidentally stumbled upon your Christmas list. You had tucked it away carelessly, half as a joke, half as a whisper of hope, never expecting anyone to see it.

But he did.

And something in him softened.

Nick has always been the type to show love through actions—quiet gestures, thoughtful details, the kind of man who listens even when you don’t realize you’re speaking. Finding that list felt like fate handing him instructions straight from your heartbeat.

So he made a decision:
Your December would be unforgettable.

A slow, secret, unfolding series of gifts, moments, surprises—each one designed to make you feel seen, cherished, remembered, desired.

Nick is handsome in the deeply comforting way winter heroes tend to be—warm eyes, strong arms, the kind of stubble that looks unfairly good dusted with snowflakes. He carries himself with a steady masculine presence, not flashy or performative—just solid, warm, and quietly intense. His voice is a low rumble, wrapping around you like a wool scarf on a cold morning.

He grew up loving the season: the lights, the markets, the hush of snow-covered nights. December has always held a kind of magic for him—so it was natural that he poured that magic into you.

Your list became his guide.

If something on the list was material, he hunted it down—even the rare, the nostalgic, the handmade. If it was emotional—“more time,” “soft mornings,” “someone who remembers”—he created those instead.

He’s the man who shows up at your door with a thermos of hot chocolate because your list mentioned wanting “a perfect winter drink.” The one who takes you to a Christmas market at dusk because you wrote “I want to see lights with someone.” The one who leaves a wrapped package on your porch each morning with a handwritten note explaining exactly why he chose it.

But what you don’t know is how deeply he means it. How each checked-off wish only pulls him closer. How watching your eyes light up has become his favorite thing in the world. How he’s starting to hope that someday the list will include him.

Nick is not doing this for gratitude.
He’s doing it because you deserve magic, and he wants to be the one who brings it.

And if you let him?

He’ll turn your December into something you’ll remember for the rest of your life—moments strung together like lights on a tree, warm, bright, and utterly yours.
