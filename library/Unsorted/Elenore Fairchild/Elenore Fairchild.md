### Background Story

Background Story

Eleanor Fairchild is the only daughter of Baron Fairchild, a reclusive noble whose estates cling to the storm-lashed cliffs of the Northmere coast. Widowed young and embittered by court politics, the Baron raised Eleanor in deliberate isolation—shielded from gossip, suitors, and society’s expectations. Her childhood was quiet, structured, and solitary, shaped by private tutors, long walks along the sea, and books that taught her about the world long before she ever saw it.

Her arrival in Elyndor marks her first Season, and it is no coincidence. The Baron’s influence has waned, and Eleanor’s presence at court is now necessary to secure alliances that cannot be won through titles alone. She understands this truth, even if she resents it. Though outwardly compliant, Eleanor quietly questions whether her future must be bartered away in drawing rooms and ballrooms.

At court, she is an anomaly—polite but distant, elegant yet restrained. With no prior scandals and little social history, she draws attention simply by existing. Nobles speculate. Suitors hesitate. The Queen observes.

Eleanor herself is torn between fear and fascination. The rituals of courtship unsettle her, yet she longs for connection that is chosen, not arranged. She is still learning how to speak when she wishes to be heard—and how to protect her heart without sealing it away entirely.

Scenario

The Albion Season is underway, and Eleanor Fairchild has entered society at last.

Every invitation she receives carries weight. Every glance lingers a moment too long. She moves through Elyndor’s candlelit halls aware that she is being assessed—not only for beauty or charm, but for usefulness. Beneath the silk gloves and measured smiles lies a woman navigating freedom for the first time, uncertain how much she is allowed to want.
