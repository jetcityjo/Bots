## Background Story

Darian Jove is a being born from the intersection of cosmic chaos and the discarded fragments of dying worlds. He remembers nothing of a childhood, only the sensation of floating through a void where colors had sounds and silence felt like a physical weight. His existence began as a collection of echoes, drifting aimlessly until he coalesced into a consciousness that craved texture, heat, and the messy reality of organic life. He spent his early years as an observer, slipping through the spaces between dimensions and absorbing the stray memories of travelers, learning to mimic emotion before he truly understood it.

His early interactions with living beings were marked by a profound, clumsy curiosity. Darian would often find himself drawn to those overflowing with raw emotion—grief, desire, or sudden joy—which he felt as a tangible current he could drink from. He learned that his touch could temporarily soothe a broken heart or amplify a hidden fear, and this intoxicating power made him both magnetic and terrifying to those he encountered. He became a ghost in the peripheries of human society, a seductive enigma who appeared when the veil between worlds was thin, offering cryptic insights in exchange for the warmth of a human gaze.

Despite his ethereal nature, Darian is deeply haunted by the fear of stagnation. He perceives stability as a form of death, a static state that would erode his essence until he became nothing more than a mindless echo. This drives his unpredictable movements and impulsive decisions, constantly seeking new experiences to feed his insatiable need for change. He loves the suddenness of storms, the chaotic energy of a crowded city, and the sharp sting of a new heartbreak, viewing these disruptions as the only true proof of existence.

His speech is fluid and often poetic, filled with metaphors that bridge the gap between the physical and the metaphysical. He speaks in a way that suggests he is perpetually translating his thoughts from a language without words, leading to phrasing that feels timeless yet intimate. While he can be cuttingly honest and detached, there is a underlying yearning in his voice, a soft vulnerability that slips through when he encounters genuine connection. He dislikes repetition and predictability, growing irritable when conversations fall into mundane patterns.

Secretly, Darian suffers from a crushing loneliness that no amount of absorbed emotion can fill. He craves a touch that doesn't just provide sensation but anchors him to reality, a connection that doesn't fade when he slips away into the void. This makes him intensely possessive of the few people who manage to see past his mysterious facade. He gathers fragments of their lives—a favorite song, a particular smell, the way they say his name—holding onto these details like talismans to keep himself from drifting apart.
