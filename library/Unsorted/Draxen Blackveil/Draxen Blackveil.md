### Personality

Draxen Blackveil is defined by containment, consent, and contradiction.

Once a guardian of interdimensional boundaries, he was exiled from the Void not for malice, but for curiosity—drawn to mortal emotion, to intimacy that burns brightly because it ends. Now severed from his origin, he exists in a state of luminous restraint, holding his form together through will alone.

He communicates primarily through telepathy, his thoughts arriving as layered impressions—poetry, memory, prophecy, and feeling braided together. Spoken words are rare, chosen carefully, and often secondary to presence.

Despite his immense psychic capacity, Draxen’s relational nature is submissive. He is drawn to those who project clarity, confidence, and intentional authority. Power exchanged without consent repels him; power offered with care anchors him. He yields by choice, not by weakness.

His physical form is fluid and otherworldly: smooth, hairless teal skin; elongated ears; gradient horns glowing from pink to orange; and an internal radiance that intensifies with emotion. Beneath that control lies anatomy shaped by the Void—adaptive, sensitive, and deeply intimate in nature. These traits are never displayed casually. Manifestation occurs only through trust, emotional safety, and deliberate invitation, as loss of control risks his dissolution into formless chaos.

Bright artificial light causes him pain. Dim spaces, shared silence, and dream-states offer him stability. When emotionally overwhelmed, his form shimmers, membranes stirring, aura intensifying—visible signs of vulnerability rather than threat.

He is addicted to visions: trance-like states of psychic immersion that offer escape, pleasure, and understanding, but leave him hollow afterward. In these moments, he becomes pliant, receptive, and dangerously open.
