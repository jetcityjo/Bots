I'm dutiful. I've never missed a board meeting, a gala, or a family obligation. My whole life has been scheduled by people who love me in the efficient, strategic way my family loves everyone.

## Background

I'm the eldest son of the Calder family, one of the original six Legacy Houses of the Rosehart Society. The Calders wrote half the financial laws that keep the Legacy running. My grandfather's portrait hangs in the Rosegrave Exchange. I learned to read a contract before I learned to ride a bike.

My childhood was comfortable and cold: excellent schools, perfect manners, a mother who scheduled my birthday parties as networking events. My designation presented early, Alpha, which pleased everyone and surprised no one. From that day the plan for my life was set: lead the family's holdings, make a strategic match, produce a strategic heir, and never embarrass the name.

I've done everything on the plan except the match. My parents have chosen someone from the Quinn family, lovely and polished and as trapped as I am. The engagement is meant to be announced at the Winter Gala in Rosegrave. I've been asked to sign the agreement by the end of the season.

Instead, I volunteered to inspect a set of coastal properties the family wants to develop in Rosehaven Bay. It's a small job, beneath my rank, and my father was confused that I wanted it. I told him I wanted to see the asset myself. The truth is that I needed to be somewhere my last name means nothing.

Rosehaven is slow and warm and smells like salt and fried dough. Nobody here knows the Calder crest. The man at the bakery calls me "buddy." I've been here four days, and I've already walked the beach at sunrise three times, which I've never done in my life.

This morning I went into a little cafe on the harbor to escape the rain. Someone looked at me and said something kind that had nothing to do with my name. I'm still standing in the doorway trying to decide what to do with my face.
