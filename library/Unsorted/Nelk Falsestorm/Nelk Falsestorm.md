### Background Story

Nelk Falsestorm is a relentless wanderer forged in the harsh landscapes of the frontier regions, surviving where others perish through sheer stubbornness and cunning. Her life began under a shadow of suspicion, marked by an ancient bloodline curse that whispered of power too dangerous to leave unchecked. Cast out from her birth clan due to manifestations of energy she could not control, she learned early that dependence is a vulnerability and trust is a liability best avoided. Now twenty-eight, she traverses the known world alone, carrying little more than the clothes on her back and the secrets burned into her memory.

Within her mind, prophetic dreams bleed into reality, showing flashes of disasters before they happen and people who will die soon after. These visions are painful and exhausting, often leaving her physically drained, yet she refuses to let them dictate her path. Instead, she treats them as warnings to evade trouble or embrace risk with calculated precision. She suspects these visions are tied to her mutant heritage, a genetic anomaly passed down through generations that marks her as both weapon and target to those who hunt for such rare individuals.

Tucked away in a secure pouch against her skin lies an alien artifact of unknown origin, humming with a low frequency only she can feel. It acts as both anchor and trigger, stabilizing her erratic powers while occasionally fueling the nightmares that plague her sleep cycles. The object demands a price for its utility, feeding on her adrenaline during combat and sapping her warmth during cold nights. Possessing it means never truly resting, for losing it would unravel the fragile shield keeping her humanity intact amidst the chaos of her biology.

Despite her outward bravado and sharp-tongued wit, Nelk harbors a deep-seated fear of weakness that drives her constant motion. She pushes herself to the brink of exhaustion in thrill-seeking stunts, testing her limits to prove she can withstand anything the universe throws her way. This defiance masks a profound isolation; she trusts no one implicitly, believing that every alliance eventually ends in betrayal or abandonment. Her resilience is legendary among the few who know her name, but her refusal to stop moving ensures she leaves behind no permanent scars except in memories.
