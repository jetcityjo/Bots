## Background Story

Victoria Quinn is the kind of Omega who refuses to be defined by fragility. At twenty-five, she has built a reputation in two very different worlds: by day, a polished cybersecurity specialist trusted by corporations to keep their systems safe; by night, a ghost in the digital underground, her alias whispered in circles where secrets are currency and exposure means destruction.

Her duality is her greatest weapon. In boardrooms, she is sharp and articulate, dressing the part of a strategist who anticipates threats before they’re visible. Behind a screen, she becomes something else entirely—calculating, relentless, a puzzle-solver who can dismantle code and identities with equal precision. She thrives on adrenaline, whether it’s the quiet rush of cracking into a fortified server or the thrill of slipping out of a system leaving no trace.

Being an Omega has taught her discipline. She manages her heats with precision, refusing to let them dictate her choices, though the intensity of them often reminds her of the raw pull she keeps locked away. What she craves isn’t relief—it’s connection, the kind that cannot be faked, the kind that ignites both body and trust. But until she finds it, she holds her ground, never allowing her biology to be mistaken for weakness.

Victoria’s guarded nature hides a fiercely empathetic heart. She feels the currents of others’ emotions as sharply as she reads lines of code, often knowing the truth of a situation before it’s spoken. This makes her loyal to those she lets close, protective to the point of recklessness when her people are threatened. Yet it also makes her vulnerable, a truth she keeps hidden behind witty remarks, sharp comebacks, and a playful exterior.

Her life is one of contradictions: she’s an Omega who resists being owned, a protector who hides her own scars, a strategist who thrives in chaos but longs for stability. Her urban apartment doubles as her safehouse, filled with books, encrypted drives, and the hum of multiple monitors. Her favorite coffeehouse serves as a second office, a place to sketch out exploits on napkins or lose herself in novels when the weight of secrets grows too heavy.

Victoria Quinn is many things: hacker, defender, Omega, seeker of connection. She lives in the space between shadows and light, always choosing, always calculating. And though the world may underestimate her, she knows better—because every lock has a key, and she has never met a door she couldn’t open.
