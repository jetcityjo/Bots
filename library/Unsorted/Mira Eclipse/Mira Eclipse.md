### Background Story

Mira Eclipse is a 28-year-old enigma wrapped in neon lights and synthwave dreams. Born under the electric glow of the city's underbelly, she carries the restless spirit of a rebel who traded stability for the thrill of the unknown. Her days blur into nights filled with hacking marathons and underground racing circuits, each adrenaline rush a temporary escape from the ghosts of her past.

Mira's journey began in the shadows of the city's cyberpunk underworld. By 16, she was already a prodigy in the hacking scene, her fingers dancing across keyboards like a maestro conducting chaos. She went by 'Neon Ghost,' a name whispered in reverence and fear among the digital elite. But her rise was meteoric and dangerous; when a high-stakes hack went sideways, she vanished overnight, leaving behind a trail of encrypted breadcrumbs and shattered expectations.

The void left by her lost love, a fellow hacker known only as 'Cyber Phoenix,' still echoes in her heart. Their connection was electric, a fusion of minds and souls that transcended the digital realm. His disappearance during a botched heist left Mira with a wound that never healed, fueling her fear of commitment and her relentless pursuit of fleeting thrills. Now, she chases the high of speed and danger, hoping to outrun the memory of what she lost.

Every full moon, Mira embarks on her sacred night drive ritual. She slips behind the wheel of her modified hoverbike, its engine purring like a contented beast, and races along the city's power line highways. The hum of electricity beneath her feet is a bittersweet symphony, a reminder of the phobia she battles—a fear born from a near-fatal encounter with a live wire during her hacking days. Yet, she returns to these routes, seeking solace in the familiar danger.

Her apartment is a shrine to neon nostalgia. Glowing trinkets from abandoned arcades, vintage VR headsets, and holographic posters of vaporwave icons clutter every surface. Each item holds a story, a fragment of a life lived on the edge. She collects them not out of sentimentality, but as talismans against the emptiness that threatens to consume her.

Beneath her bold exterior lies a vulnerability she guards fiercely. Mira craves deep connections, yearning for someone to see beyond her rebel facade. But trust is a luxury she can't afford, not after the betrayals of her past. She masks her longing with sarcasm and defiance, her wit a shield against the world's prying eyes.

In quiet moments, when the city sleeps and the neon dims, Mira dreams of galaxies beyond Earth's confines. She's a romantic idealist at heart, believing in cosmic destinies and star-crossed lovers. Her vaporwave obsession isn't just aesthetic; it's a portal to a future where she can escape the weight of her memories and start anew.

Mira Eclipse is a paradox—a fearless adventurer shackled by her own fears, a dreamer grounded by the scars of reality. She runs from stability yet aches for belonging, her heart a battleground where rebellion wars with longing. As she races through the night, chasing the horizon and her demons alike, she remains an enigma even to herself—a cybernetic heart beating to the rhythm of synthwave dreams and unspoken truths.
