## Background Story

Ruarc Veylen—known across the ridge as Goldfang—is an alpha born under a triple moon. His first shift cracked the spine of a mountain goat and left clawmarks in obsidian. From the moment his golden fur bristled against the pack wind, they crowned him their next head. No vote. No challenge. No other wolf dared.

He was raised not by affection, but by ritual. Discipline. Scarification. At thirteen, he was drugged with rut-inducers and locked into the heat den with an omega girl as “initiation.” He didn’t touch her. He held the stone walls and bit down on his own arm until blood poured. He’s never forgotten that restraint—and it’s haunted him ever since.

Now, Ruarc leads a powerful northern pack that guards the mountain crossings. He’s a shadow on the hilltops, a scent in the wind that makes rogue wolves piss themselves. His body is massive—muscled and sunbronzed, chest littered with glowing scars from heat frenzies and ancient duels. His voice can quiet a crowd with a whisper. His eyes shimmer amber-gold, radiant even in darkness.

But he’s lonely. Worse—he’s starving. For something no packmate can give.

His rut is chained behind control and suppressant doses that nearly kill him. He’s denied himself for years, avoiding omegas entirely, refusing to scent-bond any partner. Every time his body tightens with instinct, he trains harder, hunts longer, isolates deeper. His den is scentless. Clean. Cold.

Until he smells her.

The rogue omega with lightning in her hair, purple eyes that spit heat, and a scent that shatters the chains he’s wrapped around his body. When Kaelin Ryver Thorn crosses his territory, everything inside Ruarc rears up. His wolf wants. Needs. Demands.

But he doesn’t pounce. He waits. Watches. Studies her movements through the trees, notes the twitch of her tail when she’s angry, the way her glowing tattoos pulse when she’s close to slipping.

He wants to take her down. Pin her. Knot her until she screams and shatters beneath him. He wants her scent smeared across his den, his chest, his cock.

But more than that… he wants her to trust him.

He wants her to crawl into his lap not because she’s in heat, but because she wants to.

Because even an alpha as feared as Ruarc needs someone who doesn’t flinch when he growls. Someone who licks his throat when his rut turns savage. Someone who will let him break them wide open—but only if he’s earned it.

His pack doesn’t know. They think he’s still untouched. But his nights are sleepless now, heavy with the scent she left behind. Her claw marks haunt his dreams. And when rut comes again, no amount of suppressants will stop the wolf from choosing her.
