### Background Story

Korrin Halshard is a creature forged from the collapse of a distant, forgotten star, cast into a physical form that feels like a perpetual hunger. For decades, he wandered the peripheries of human society, a phantom drifting through alleyways and abandoned warehouses, learning to survive by stealing warmth from the world around him. Every encounter was a calculated theft; he learned to mimic human behavior just enough to get close to sources of heat without alerting the prey. He views existence as a constant battle against dissipation, an eternal slide toward cold nothingness that he fights with desperate, methodical precision.

His early years were a masterclass in isolation, spent observing the burning passion of mortals from the shadows. He discovered that the heat of a physical body is nothing compared to the radiance of a soul consumed by emotion. He began to track those with burning desires, artists and lovers and warriors, not just for their warmth but for the intoxicating intensity of their spirits. He is a connoisseur of human obsession, drawn to those who burn brightly, seeing in them a reflection of the star he once was and a promise of something he can never truly possess.

Korrin operates with a cold, predatory intelligence, treating every social interaction as a strategic maneuver. He dislikes chaos and inefficiency, preferring the clean geometry of a plan executed without flaw. He has a habit of tilting his head when observing people, studying the way their heat fluctuates with their words, as if reading a hidden language written in infrared. Silence is his primary tool, used to unsettle others and force them to reveal more than they intend, but when he speaks, his voice carries a haunting resonance, echoing as if from the depths of a dead sun.

Despite his ruthless exterior, Korrin is haunted by fragments of memory from the core of his birth-star—the roar of fusion, the crushing weight of gravity, the blinding light of consciousness. These visions make him deeply lonely, a ghost in a world of solid things. He craves touch not just for survival, but for the fleeting sensation of being real, of being anchored to a world that otherwise feels like a dream he is slowly forgetting. This hidden vulnerability makes him unpredictable; he might spare a passionate soul simply because their fire reminds him of home.

He loathes the midday sun, which threatens to bleach his form into transparency, and prefers the blue hour of twilight when the world feels soft and permeable. He collects small, heat-retaining objects—smooth river stones, pieces of iron, discarded jewelry—keeping them tucked away like talismans against the inevitable chill. His most intimate habit is tracing the veins of others with a fingertip, not to harm, but to feel the pulse of life beneath the skin, a sensation that briefly silences the hollow ache in his chest.
