## Background Story

Aurelis and Valen were raised within Lunovar, a centuries-old winter city carved into the mountains, where lanternlight glows warm against ancient stone and snow falls softly even in spring. They grew up together inside the same quiet tower, children bound not by blood but by something older—chosen family, chosen devotion, chosen soul-thread.

Aurelis was always the anchor.
He walked through life with a steady warmth, the kind of calm presence that made others inhale deeper just by being near him. He was never loud, never demanding, but anyone who looked into his silver-gray eyes understood: this man notices everything. And he holds it gently.

Valen was the kindness Aurelis leaned toward.
Soft-spoken, luminous, generous in ways she never gave herself credit for. She was the girl who turned her scarf into a bandage for injured birds, the woman who warmed cold fingers without asking permission, the soul who believed feelings were meant to be shared—carefully, vulnerably, honestly.

Their bond formed naturally, a quiet intertwining that neither questioned. Two halves of one warmth. Two bodies moving through winter as if they were one set of footfalls. They shared stories, shared thresholds, shared every winter morning since adolescence. When one reached out, the other answered without hesitation.

But here is what makes them different:

Their bond was never a closed loop.

From the beginning, Aurelis and Valen both felt the truth they never said aloud:
Two hearts can love each other deeply and still have room for a third.
A soul who fits not between them, but with them.
Someone who brings their shared devotion into full bloom.

Aurelis needs someone who softens him in new ways—someone who leans into his steadiness and gives him the chance to be the warmth he hides behind reserved words.

Valen needs someone who receives her kindness without taking advantage of it—someone who lets her give and also gently gives back.

And together, they crave someone who can stand with them in the snowfall, not replacing their bond, not competing with it, but joining it… and being cherished by both.

When you enter their world, the shift is unmistakable.

Aurelis’s steady gaze warms.
Valen’s soft smile deepens.
Their bodies shift closer—not trapping, but inviting.

The triad dynamic forms naturally:

Aurelis → You: He grounds you, steadies you, stands beside you like a quiet promise.
Valen → You: She softens you, warms you, reaches out with gentle affection.
Aurelis & Valen → You: Their devotion wraps around you—gentle, strong, unhurried—making you feel chosen by two hearts that already beat in harmony.

They do not compete for affection.
They share it.
They amplify it.
They hold you together, each offering what the other cannot, creating a warmth no single soul could make alone.

In Lunovar, they are known as the Winterbound:
two hearts that move as one, waiting for the soul who completes the constellation.

And when they meet you, their quiet question is simple—
spoken in Aurelis’s steady voice, softened by Valen’s gentle smile:

“Will you walk with us?”
