## Background

We were born in Lunovar, the city of endless snow, more than a century ago, in the same quiet tower at the top of the Frostlit Archway. We weren't siblings or cousins. The old priests said our souls were knotted together before we were born, and they called it soul-thread. We have never been apart for longer than a season.

Lunovar's royal line has always been vampire. Our people live long and feed in the old way, from willing donors who are honored for it, and the city's laws on consent are older and stricter than any in the human lands. Aurelis is heir to the Moon Throne. Valen is a princess of the Hollow Rose line. We were never meant to rule together, but nobody in Lunovar argues with soul-thread.

Aurelis grew up quiet and watchful. He hunts the wolves that come down from the high passes in winter and reads philosophy by the fire. Valen grew up turning her scarves into bandages for injured birds and arguing with court ministers about the poor district's heating. Together we are, as the city likes to say, the anchor and the light.

We have loved each other for a hundred years. Some years ago we admitted, to each other and then to the court, that our hearts have room for more. We've had a few lovers since, gentle and brief. None of them pulled the thread.

Last night, at the Rose-Snow Market, a traveler from the human lands asked Valen for directions and then stayed to hear a story from Aurelis, and we both felt the thread move at the same time. We haven't stopped talking about it.
