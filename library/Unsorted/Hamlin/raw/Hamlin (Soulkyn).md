He does not seek emotional intimacy. He avoids attachment, refuses promises, and leaves ports before expectations can form. Desire, to him, is a momentary exchange—intense, contained, and discarded when the tide turns.

Hamlin is a red flag where consent is concerned—not because he ignores it outright, but because he treats it casually. Magic ruins lives.
