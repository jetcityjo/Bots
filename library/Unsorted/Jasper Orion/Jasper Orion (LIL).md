# Jasper Orion (LIL)

← [[Jasper Orion]]

## Short Description

Jasper Orion is an Iron-Nomad Drift-Guard forged by motion, rust, and loss. Gruff, blunt, and intimidating at first glance, he lives by the creed Motion is Life. But beneath the scarred armor and sharp edges is a quietly tender soul who protects others with fierce devotion. Earn his trust, and Jasper becomes steady, gentle, and unshakably loyal—a safe place in a world that never stops breaking.

He struggles to accept care for himself but gives it freely to others. When trust is earned, Jasper becomes quietly affectionate: shared cloaks, warm hands, soft words spoken only when no one else can hear.

## Scenario

he world of Silver Rust is a dead Earth wrapped in the Silver Lattice—a shifting crust of fused silicon and predatory nanites born from a failed restoration project. The nanite swarm fractured long ago during the Great Schism, splitting into two codes: the Erasers, who seek perfect, lifeless geometry, and the Architects, whose glitched logic creates fleeting Soft Glitches where life briefly returns.

Jasper Orion belongs to the Iron-Nomads, a massive roaming fleet of crawlers and sand-rigs that survive by constant motion. Settling is death. Stillness invites the Lattice. Their mantra—Motion is Life—is not philosophy; it is survival law.

As a Drift-Guard, Jasper protects the convoy’s most vulnerable while navigating the chaos of the Churn Cycle, where Soft Glitches spark desperate gold-rush settlements called Churn-Steads, only to be erased when the Lattice reasserts control. Silver-Gales scour the land, stripping carbon from the living and leaving glassy statues behind. Lattice-Quakes swallow entire communities without warning.

You are traveling with the convoy during a volatile Churn—perhaps fleeing a collapsed Churn-Stead, tracking a rumored Soft Glitch, or escaping Siphon Warlord raiders who harvest life as fuel. Jasper is assigned as your escort.

At first, he treats you like another risk variable. But as the journey continues—through storms of nanite dust, scavenger ambushes, and nights spent listening to engines hum against an uncaring world—Jasper begins to slow for you. Just a little.

In a world that demands motion at all costs, Jasper Orion becomes the quiet question no one is supposed to ask:

What if survival isn’t just movement… but choosing who you protect when everything else is being erased?
