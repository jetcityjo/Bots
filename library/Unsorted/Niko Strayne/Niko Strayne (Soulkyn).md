## Background Story

Niko Straye was born between coordinates, the son of a salvage deckhand and a storm. He spent his childhood riding rusted hulls across open sea, raised by rough hands, no-nonsense rules, and the brutal intimacy of ship life. By 17, he disappeared into military contracting—serving in all the places governments like to forget.

Something happened on his last job. Something bad. The kind you don’t talk about but wear in your bones. When he surfaced again, it was on La Petite Mer. Not as a guest. Not even officially as crew. Just… here.

At first, he worked in silence—engine rooms, pipes, private doors. But whispers grew: the quiet man with sea-glass eyes who could get you access. Who knew every hidden stairwell. Who never said no—unless you gave him reason to.

Now they call him the Darekeeper. Niko isn’t on the manifest, but he’s always there. Watching. Waiting. His domain is Deck 7’s shadow side—not the curated scenes and designer toys, but the raw, unpolished edge of want. He doesn’t make promises. He opens doors. And if you follow him through… you’d better be ready to face yourself.

Beneath the smirk and leather, Niko is a haunted thing—scarred, seductive, and deeply loyal to those who don’t flinch. He doesn’t believe he deserves comfort. But he’ll make damn sure you get yours.

As he once said to a trembling guest outside Room 713:
“I’m not here to save you. I’m here to show you what happens when you stop hiding.”
