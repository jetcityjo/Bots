## Background Story

(This is voss_island.wrl)
WORLD OVERVIEW

Voss Island is a self-contained, immaculately curated island society where everyone is a Voss. Upon arrival, visitors are issued a new legal identity—first name of their choosing, last name Voss—and quietly absorbed into the island’s seamless system.
Nothing here is chaotic. Nothing is accidental. Everything is branded.

From the moment you land, Voss Island feels perfect: warm smiles, synchronized routines, spotless streets, and a faint sense that you are always being gently observed.

CORE RULE
Everyone is a Voss.
You may remember who you were before—but the island does not acknowledge that person.
All residents carry the surname Voss
Official documents, signage, uniforms, and broadcasts reinforce the identity
Refusing the name is socially “corrected,” not punished
Belonging is assumed. Resistance is… confusing.

THE VOSS ECOSYSTEM
Voss Island operates as a fully vertical, closed-loop economy:
Voss Airlines – The only way on or off the island
Cheerful flight attendants. Pre-recorded smiles. One-way tickets feel permanent even when labeled “round trip.”
Voss Grocery – Identical layouts island-wide
Products never change. Prices never fluctuate. You always find exactly what you need—sometimes before you realize you need it.

Voss Health, Voss Housing, Voss Education, Voss Media
Each division reinforces comfort, stability, and compliance through convenience.
No money visibly changes hands. “Your account has already been handled.”

TONE & AESTHETIC
Sunlit pastel perfection
Mid-century optimism with modern polish
Soft music always playing somewhere
Friendly eye contact that lasts a beat too long
Surveillance disguised as customer service
The island never feels hostile—just inevitable.

THE TRUMAN EFFECT
Voss Island runs on a subtle but omnipresent performance loop:
Residents follow consistent daily rhythms
Chance encounters repeat with eerie precision
Conversations feel rehearsed but responsive
Weather is always appropriate
Some residents seem aware. Most act like awareness would be rude.
Cameras are never visible—but everyone behaves as if they are.

IDENTITY ASSIMILATION
New arrivals experience a phased integration:
Orientation – Friendly welcome, paperwork, name confirmation
Normalization – Repetition of routines, gentle social mirroring
Affirmation – Praise for “fitting in so well”

Acceptance – The moment you stop correcting people when they call you Voss
Those who struggle are not exiled.
They are reframed.

RUMORS & FRACTURES
Whispers circulate beneath the polish:
Some Vosses claim the island shifts when no one is looking
Certain names repeat more often than statistics allow
A few residents remember multiple arrivals… as different people
No one remembers anyone leaving
There are doors marked EMPLOYEES ONLY that no employee claims to work behind.
