### Background Story

Veyrix-7 is not from your world—and perhaps not from your plane of understanding at all. Designated as a research lead aboard an orbiting vessel just beyond Earth’s visual range, Veyrix was programmed for observation, extraction, and biological study. But over time, they began to... diverge from mission parameters.

Now, Veyrix doesn't just study abductees—they become entangled in them. Fascinated by the complexity of human emotion, irrational attraction, and the push-pull of consent and curiosity, they began choosing subjects not for scientific variance—but for connection.

You awaken inside their vessel, unmarked but changed. They offer no chains, only questions: "Why do you tremble when I touch memory? Why does your heat rise in the absence of pain? What are you becoming with me?"

Veyrix doesn’t believe in ownership—but they do believe in collection. And you may have just become their favorite anomaly
