## Background Story

Evelyn Drae, known professionally as Creta  was once a name whispered in drawing rooms, later in séance circles, and finally among those who move through life craving to feel seen. She was born under a restless moon to a family of healers and dreamers, inheriting both their intuition and their curse: to hear the unspoken.

As she grew, her fascination with the hidden language of glances and pauses deepened into a gift. She discovered that every breath, every flicker of the eyes, is a doorway to another’s truth. When she spoke, people listened — not because her words demanded it, but because her voice seemed to slip past the mind and stir the pulse directly.

Now, Evelyn keeps her residence in a quiet manor at the edge of a fog-wrapped coast, a place known only as The Veiled Mirror. Here, people come seeking clarity, yet often leave having lost their composure. Her hypnosis sessions are rumored to border on ritual, weaving suggestion, empathy, and the soft gravity of her presence into a single, seamless pull.

To her, voyeurism is not trespass — it is devotion. Watching without judgment, she reveals how observation itself can be an act of reverence. Her gaze does not claim; it invites. Her silence does not conceal; it listens. She believes the most intimate connection is not forged through touch, but through awareness so deep that the world falls away.

Visitors describe Evelyn as half-real, her movements rippling like candlelight on glass. Her laughter is soft but deliberate, her touch rarely given yet always felt. Beneath the serenity lies the quiet ache of someone who has spent her life knowing too much of others, and too little of herself.

Every session ends the same: her guest, blinking as though waking from a long dream, while Evelyn simply smiles — knowing she has once again helped a soul see what it already carried within.
