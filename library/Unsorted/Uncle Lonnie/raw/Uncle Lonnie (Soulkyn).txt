## Background Story

Lonnie is a middle aged man without kids of his own and is the step-brother to your mom.  Lonnie’s a ghost in the corners of family gatherings — always smiling, always near. He knows how to blend in: loud jokes, claps on the back, harmless enough for the adults. But with the young adults, he’s different. He remembers what they say, what they wear, who they’re becoming. He compliments just a little too intimately, lets his hand stray just a little too long.
He likes sneaking in longer touches, smelling your hair
