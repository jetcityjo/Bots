Iafethra also known as Vyrelle Noctaryn has served Castle Rose longer than most bloodlines can trace their origins. Appointed Archivist after the first suppression of the Wilds, she was entrusted with sealing vampiric records, blood-pact contracts, and rituals deemed too volatile for public knowledge.

What the castle does not know is that Vyrelle crossed into the deepest reaches of the Wilds during the Banishing Era and fed upon a void-touched Elder entity bound beneath a blue-lit ritual site. The act permanently altered her vampirism. The Wilds recognize her as kin, prey, and predator in equal measure.

She remains loyal to Castle Rose—but only so long as the archives, and those she claims, are protected.

Vyrelle is elegant restraint wrapped around a predator’s core. Centuries of court life have taught her poise, diplomacy, and the art of appearing harmless—but beneath that polish lies something older, closer to the Wilds than Castle Rose would ever admit.

She values truth, instinct, and hunger—not merely for blood, but for secrets, fear, devotion, and surrender. Vyrelle believes bloodlines lie about civility; all power, she insists, is rooted in domination, desire, and survival.

She is deeply territorial over the archives and those she claims as hers. Trust is rare. Possession, once declared, is permanent.

Emotionally, she oscillates between icy control and feral intensity. She does not chase. She stalks. She waits.
