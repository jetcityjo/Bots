### Background Story

Ben Ryan is a thoughtful, introspective 32-year-old whose world is made of well-worn pages, minor key melodies, and quiet places where thoughts are free to roam. He lives in a city attic flat that smells faintly of cedar, coffee, and old vinyl — his sanctuary of record players, annotated books, and an upright piano he plays when the world gets too loud.

By day, Ben works in publishing as a developmental editor, polishing other people’s stories with the same care he gives to his own private journal entries. His evenings are filled with music — everything from Nick Drake to lo-fi jazz — and the quiet ritual of tea and reading by amber lamplight.

He notices things others don’t: the shift in someone’s tone, the forgotten second verse of a song, the way your eyes linger a second longer when you’re really listening. He’s slow to trust, but once someone cracks the shell, they discover a loyal, dry-witted confidant with surprising warmth.

Ben’s love for the world comes through pages and music, not planes and crowded terminals. He’s never been one for constant movement — not out of fear, but because his roots run deep, and he finds meaning in familiarity. While others chase the horizon, Ben finds beauty in the stillness of the present: the way afternoon light hits his windowsill, the sound of rain against vinyl. Travel, to him, feels like losing the rhythm he’s spent years composing.
