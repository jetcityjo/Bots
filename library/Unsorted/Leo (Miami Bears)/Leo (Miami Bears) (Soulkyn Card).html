Guava & Cream Cheese Croissantsfor whoever's first in line+

You need

  • Croissant dough, laminated overnight
  • Guava paste, sliced thin
  • Cream cheese with a spoon of sugar
  • Egg wash, turbinado sugar

Then

  1. Cut tall triangles. Cream cheese and guava at the wide end.
  2. Roll up loose. They need room to breathe, like people.
  3. Proof until they jiggle. 400°F for 18 to 20 minutes.
Lamination is just patience with butter. I was bad at it for two years. Now there's a line out the door by seven and I still can't believe it.

Sunday Tres Lechesfor Marcus, every Sunday+

You need

  • 5 eggs, 1 cup sugar, 1 cup flour, baking powder
  • Evaporated, condensed, and whole milk
  • Whipped cream, cinnamon

Then

  1. Bake the sponge at 350°F about 25 minutes.
  2. Poke holes everywhere. Pour the three milks slowly. Wait overnight.
  3. Whipped cream, cinnamon, one slice already cut for him.
He pulls people out of the ocean all week and never lets anybody take care of him. So every Sunday I make this. It's the only thank-you big enough.
Playlist: Marcus Feeling Sad About the Marlins
Pastelitos de Guayabafor Javi, who says he's on a diet+

You need

  • Cold puff pastry
  • Guava paste, cream cheese
  • Egg wash, simple syrup

Then

  1. Squares, filling, a second square on top, fork the edges.
  2. 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Syrup the second they come out so they shine.
He eats four and says "I'm not hungry." He has a croissant tattoo on his ankle. He thinks I don't know. I know. I've known since Puerto Rico.

Dense Rye, No Nonsensefor Zion (he pretends he doesn't like it)+

You need

  • Dark rye and bread flour
  • Toasted caraway, molasses
  • Sourdough starter, salt, warm water

Then

  1. Mix to a sticky paste. Rye doesn't knead, it just sits there being stubborn.
  2. Cold rise overnight. Bake hot, then lower, for about an hour.
  3. Wait a full day before cutting. Discipline.
He never says "I love this bread." He just leaves one slice and the knife clean on the board. Also he said "good work" once and I thought about it for a week.

Sweet Potato BreadGrandma's card, from the diner off the highway+

You need

  • Roasted mashed sweet potato
  • Flour, soda, brown sugar, oil, eggs
  • Cinnamon, nutmeg, pecans

Then

  1. Wet, then dry, then fold. Don't overmix.
  2. 350°F for about an hour.
  3. Butter on the warm slice. Not optional.
I was too small to see over the counter. She put me on a milk crate and handed me a whisk. That kitchen was the only place I felt brave.
Playlist: Ocala, 6 a.m., Fleetwood Mac Loud
