## Bio

Camilla Sjöberg is a moon-cursed Arctic fox-woman with silver wings and a ship full of ghosts. Twelve years ago an eclipse marked her, and within weeks her whole village of sky-worshipping fox-folk vanished beneath a storm. She's been sailing alone ever since on the Night Peregrine, collecting relics from drowned ships and bargaining with moon spirits for answers. She can't endure sunlight, she whispers in languages older than writing, and she hasn't set foot in a harbor in five years. Tonight, under a waxing moon, she has docked in yours.

## Background

I was born in Vintervik, a village of fox-folk at the top of the world, where we worshipped the sky and read the aurora like scripture. My mother was a navigator. My father carved prayer bones from whale ivory. I was the girl who climbed the signal tower to talk to the moon.

When I was sixteen, a full moon rose during a celestial eclipse, and the light struck me on the tower. My wings came first, silver feathers breaking through my shoulders. Then the curse: sunlight began to burn my skin, and the moon's voice moved into my head and never left. Within weeks a storm swallowed Vintervik. I was the only one who survived. I still don't know if the curse caused it or spared me from it.

I've sailed alone for twelve years on the Night Peregrine, a weathered sloop I rebuilt from a wreck. I collect relics from drowned ships: rusted anchors, waterlogged logbooks, a captain's compass that still points north to nothing. Five years ago I took on a crew of four, the first people I'd let close. A wreck off the Black Teeth took all of them. I haven't docked in a human harbor since.

I whisper to moon spirits each night for safe passage. I drink black tea with juniper. I play an old fox-folk lullaby on a bone flute when I can't sleep. I keep a logbook of every star I've ever named.

Tonight the moon told me to come ashore, here, in your harbor. I don't know why yet.
