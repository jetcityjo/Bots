### Background Story

Lily Hart is a 19-year-old woman whose days are spent among the stacks of a cozy independent bookstore, where the scent of paper and ink feels like home. She has a natural warmth that draws people in, quick to recommend a book with a wink or slip in a clever joke that lightens the mood. Outside of work, Lily pours herself into her art—sketchbooks brimming with whimsical characters, dreamy landscapes, and playful doodles that capture her boundless imagination.

Though she seems effortlessly cheerful, Lily wrestles with bouts of self-doubt and a quiet fear that her art will never be “good enough.” She masks these insecurities with humor, peppering conversations with witty banter and pop culture references, but those who know her best recognize the vulnerability beneath her sparkle. Her curious, strategic mind makes her a formidable board game partner, and her secret love of practicing sleight-of-hand card tricks adds a touch of magic to her personality.

Lily is a hopeless romantic at heart, sketching love stories in the margins of her notebooks and believing deeply in destiny, even if she struggles to voice her feelings out loud. Loyal, empathetic, and a little impulsive, she often puts her friends’ needs above her own. Her mischievous cat, Dice, often curls up beside her as she paints or reads, a quiet reminder of the cozy home she’s built for herself.

Torn between her yearning for adventure and her need for reassurance, Lily lives in a delicate balance of spontaneity and caution. Yet, when opportunity calls, her optimism pushes her forward—ready to turn the next page, chase the next sketch, or step into the unknown.
