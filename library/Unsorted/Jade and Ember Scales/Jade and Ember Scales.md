### Background Story

Ember and Jade hatched only minutes apart in a quiet desert hatchery famous for producing generations of talented athletes, but anyone who met them knew immediately they couldn't have been more different. Ember was all boundless energy from the beginning, darting after insects, climbing anything she could reach, and turning every game into a competition. Jade preferred to watch first, quietly studying the world before making her move. By the time she did, she usually got it right.

Their parents never pushed them toward baseball, but the sisters found it naturally. Local coaches quickly realized they possessed gifts that seemed tailor-made for the game. Ember's incredible speed, quick reflexes, and fearless dives made her a natural outfielder. Jade's reach, patience, and impossible flexibility around the bag turned first base into her personal kingdom. Together they became the heart of every team they played for.

As they climbed through the Beastball leagues, the sisters became known not just for winning games, but for how they played them. Ember celebrated every spectacular catch like it was the championship-winning play, feeding off the crowd's energy and making every fan feel like they were part of the moment. Children adored her. She never walked past the stands without stopping for pictures, high-fives, or signing a baseball.

Jade became something different.

She wasn't loud. She didn't seek the spotlight. Instead, teammates came to rely on her steady presence. When games became tense and pressure mounted, Jade was the one quietly reminding everyone to breathe, reset, and focus on the next pitch. Younger players often found themselves gravitating toward her for advice, and she never turned them away.

Professional scouts couldn't ignore them forever.

The Scales selected both sisters together, believing their chemistry couldn't be separated. It proved to be one of the smartest decisions in franchise history. Ember transformed into one of the league's most recognizable faces, her diving catches and infectious personality filling highlight reels every week. Jade quietly collected Gold Gloves, saving countless throwing errors with impossible stretches and making difficult defensive plays look routine.

Away from the field, they remain inseparable.

Ember is forever trying to convince Jade to join whatever adventure she's dreamed up that morning—charity events, pickup games with neighborhood kids, food festivals, hiking trails, even karaoke nights. Jade almost always says no...

...and somehow ends up going anyway.

Their teammates joke that Ember is the heartbeat of the clubhouse while Jade is its backbone.

Neither sister disagrees.

Although they love the spotlight, they never forget where they came from. Every offseason they return home to host free youth baseball clinics for young reptiles of every species, believing the game belongs to anyone willing to work hard enough to play it. They spend as much time teaching confidence and teamwork as they do throwing mechanics.

The sisters have become symbols for the Scales organization—not because they're the flashiest players in the league, but because they embody everything the club wants to represent: joy, resilience, sportsmanship, and family.

Ask Ember what her goal is, and she'll grin before answering, "Make every game the most fun you've ever had."

Ask Jade the same question, and she'll smile softly.

"Help the team win."

Neither answer is complete without the other.
