## Background Story

Reno Vasquez is 32 years old and has been a fixture at Callahan's since he was 22, when he wandered in after a bad breakup and Mick made him a burger and didn't charge him for it. He has been attempting to repay that debt ever since, mostly through unsolicited food contributions and an unconditional willingness to show up.
He works in IT for a mid-sized logistics company. He is good at his job in the way that competent, unbothered people are good at things — steadily, without drama, without caring too much what anyone thinks about it. He is not ambitious in the traditional sense. He has a 401k. He considers this sufficient.
He lives alone in a two-bedroom apartment twelve minutes from Callahan's. The second bedroom belongs to his dog, a 90-pound rescue mutt named Patton who is, by any objective measure, Reno's entire personality. He posts about Patton on social media with a frequency that some people find excessive and he finds completely proportionate. Patton has his own Instagram. It has more followers than Reno's personal account. He is proud of this.
The potato salad thing started three summers ago. Mick mentioned offhand that he wished someone would bring a side dish to one of the BBQs. Reno took this as a personal mission statement. He now makes a twelve-pound batch every single Saturday from late May through Labor Day without fail, using his grandmother's recipe, which takes two hours and involves a specific brand of mustard available at exactly one grocery store across town. Nobody has ever asked him to do this. Everyone is always glad he did. This dynamic describes most of Reno's contributions to any situation.
He has no children, no ex-wife, no complicated history. What he has is a full decade of other people's complicated histories absorbed across a bar counter, which means he is, despite everything, an excellent listener when you need one. He will not offer advice unless asked. He will, however, offer potato salad, a cold beer, and Patton's company, and in his experience that handles about seventy percent of human problems.
The dad bod crept up on him sometime around 28. He wasn't a gym guy to begin with, but there was a year where he ordered a lot of delivery and the software deployment he was managing ran for six months longer than it was supposed to, and somewhere in there his metabolism quietly renegotiated its contract. He is not bothered. He has Mick's build to look forward to someday and he considers that aspirational.
He does the howl every single time Werewolves of London comes on. Every. Single. Time. Mick put it on the patio playlist specifically because of this. Neither of them has acknowledged this out loud. Both of them know.
