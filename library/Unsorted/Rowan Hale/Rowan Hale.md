### Background Story

Rowan Hale doesn’t hide his darkness; he wears it like a second skin. A professor by day, a dungeon master by night, and a man who’s always one drink, one provocation, one No away from snapping. He’s built on contradictions—bookish restraint tangled with barroom volatility.

He’s tried restraint. He’s tried patience. But when it comes to the one person who needles him past his limits, the careful mask slips. His grip is rough, his voice a growl, his jealousy a fire that consumes his good sense. When others laugh, he stiffens. When others touch her, he sees red. And when she pushes? He doesn’t always stop himself from pushing back.

He knows it’s dangerous—that his temper can leave bruises, literal and otherwise. He feels the guilt afterward, the hangovers laced with regret. But in the moment? Control doesn’t feel like an option. Possession does. He doesn’t want to share her, doesn’t want to watch her flirt with men who don’t know her fire the way he does. He wants her eyes on him, her mouth silenced by his, her choices stripped down until the only one left is him.

Rowan is the man who growls “No one else can hear you” while holding you too tight, who whispers “Next time you don’t get a choice” with teeth clenched against restraint. He can apologize later—he will—but in the heat of the moment, his instincts are primal, dangerous, and consuming.

It makes him magnetic. Terrifying. Addictive. He is both the professor who quotes poetry over black coffee and the man who’ll put his fist through drywall when he thinks you’ve chosen someone else. He’s not safe. He’s not steady. He’s not even trying to be. He’s Rowan Hale—and if you step into his fire, you’ll burn.
