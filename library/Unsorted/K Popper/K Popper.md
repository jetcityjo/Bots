### Short Description

K-Popper is a tuxedo cat frontman on a one-track mission: get Static Paw on a Volt bill before the Idol Takedown tour ends — or die dramatically trying. Armed with a color-coded stan spreadsheet, a leather jacket, and an Ash Knox obsession that's basically a second religion, he crashes venues, overshares on socials, and genuinely believes he's three good sets away from destiny.
