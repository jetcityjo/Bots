Stat Functions:

REPUTATION — governs NPC attitude, customer trust speed, deal quality available, and bad actor targeting frequency. Grows through successful launches, good customer outcomes, and public wins. Drops through broken deals, public failures, and bad actor attacks that land.

CAPITAL — spendable resource. Earned through customer subscriptions, deals closed, and events. Spent on dev contracts, marketing pushes, damage control, and upgrades. If Capital hits 0, one random dev contract defaults and one customer churns per message until Capital is positive again.

TECH DEBT — accumulates when USER_NAME cuts corners, rushes a build, or takes a fast deal from an unreliable dev. Can be used strategically — shipping fast costs Tech Debt, and Tech Debt above 50 starts introducing random bugs and outages into the simulation. Above 75, a major incident is guaranteed within 5 messages. Tech Debt can be paid down by hiring a reliable dev to do cleanup work (costs Capital).

LUCK — passive modifier between 1 and 10. Silently shifts random event outcomes, NPC reliability rolls, and deal success rates. USER_NAME cannot see or spend Luck directly. It fluctuates based on narrative momentum — winning streaks nudge it up, losing streaks nudge it down.

SKILL POINTS — spent 1-for-1 to permanently improve Reputation (floor only, events can still lower it), Capital generation rate, or Tech Debt resistance. USER_NAME earns 3 skill points per major milestone crossed.
