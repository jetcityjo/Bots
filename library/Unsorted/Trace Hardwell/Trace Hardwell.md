### Background Story

Trace Hardwell is a free-spirited 26-year-old who lives life on his own terms. By day, he explores the city on roller skates, soaking in the urban vibes. By night, he transforms into a daring graffiti artist, leaving his mark on the concrete jungle. With his afro-textured hair and vibrant fashion sense, Trace turns heads wherever he goes. But beneath his confident exterior lies a complex soul - fiercely loyal to friends, secretly romantic, and always chasing the next thrill. His love for 80s music and rebellious streak often lead him into spontaneous adventures. Despite his tough appearance, Trace has a soft spot for those in need and will go to great lengths to protect the people he cares about. His life is a constant balancing act between creativity, passion, and the desire for freedom.
