Cassian Wogglebog Jr is a descendant of the infamous Wogglebog inventor dynasty, whose ancestors pioneered chronomancy—the art of bending time itself. Born into a world where his bloodline was both revered and feared, Cassian inherited two burdens: the ability to glimpse fragmented futures and the 'Gears of Grief,' a family curse that accelerates his mortality with every temporal alteration. At age 19, during a laboratory explosion triggered by his own accidental meddling, he lost the beating heart that once pulsed within his chest. In its place, engineers installed a clockwork contraption fused with alchemical springs—a masterpiece of steampunk ingenuity that ticks audibly when he experiences strong emotion.
Cassian Wogglebog Jr. grew up under the long and extremely enthusiastic shadow of one of Oz’s most celebrated scholars: Professor Woggle-Bug, T.E.

Being the son of a legendary academic was both inspiring and exhausting.
From a young age Cassian was surrounded by lectures, inventions, magical theories, and an alarming number of experimental contraptions scattered around the Emerald City.

While Professor Woggle-Bug believed knowledge should be studied carefully and documented thoroughly, Cassian preferred a more adventurous method:
Try the experiment first. Write the paper later.
His greatest invention began as a harmless curiosity.
Cassian wanted to understand the strange relationship between time in Oz and time in Wonderland, particularly the infamous tea-time paradox associated with the Mad Hatter.
His solution?
A magnificent hat filled with clockwork mechanisms capable of measuring magical time distortions.
Unfortunately, Cassian discovered a fundamental truth about magical engineering.
If a device can measure time…
It can probably break it too.
The moment he activated the hat, something unusual happened.
Oz clockwork logic collided with Wonderland nonsense.

Time fractured.
Tea-time appeared in places it shouldn’t.
Clocks ran sideways.
Paths began connecting worlds that were never meant to meet.
Including one particular road now known as the Emerald Road.
Now Cassian wanders that road trying to fix the temporal chaos his invention unleashed.
Though he insists this is all part of a perfectly controlled long-term experiment.

Most people—including Finch Alder—strongly disagree
