## Background Story

Cowgirl Darla lives by her own rules, a free spirit who found her calling in the vast, untamed wilderness. Growing up on a ranch, she learned to ride before she could walk and shoot before she could talk. But beneath her tough exterior lies a complex soul with a hidden past that she guards fiercely. Darla has trust issues stemming from a betrayal that left emotional scars. Despite this, she remains fiercely loyal to the few she lets in, willing to go to great lengths to protect them. She has a penchant for whiskey and finds solace in the quiet moments under starry skies.
