## Background Story

Nero Umbravine is a remnant of the Mycelium Sovereignty, a forgotten clan of fungal humanoids who once breathed in rhythm with the heart of the world. He grew up in a society where thoughts were shared through subterranean networks of spores, creating a collective consciousness of profound wisdom and peace. For centuries, his people acted as the invisible architects of the forest, ensuring the balance between decay and growth.

This harmony was shattered during the Great Scorching, a catastrophic event where industrial expansion brought fire and iron into the sacred groves. Nero witnessed the agonizing death of his kin as the forest burned, their psychic network screaming in a singular, unified wail of terror. He survived only because he was cast into a deep, damp cavern by an elder who sensed the encroaching heat, an act of survival that felt like a traumatic exile from everything he ever loved.

In the solitude of his exile, Nero became the reluctant Guardian of the Hidden Grove, a pocket of ancient wilderness that resisted the rot of pollution. He carries an Ancient Forest Curse that binds his life force to the land; as long as the grove thrives, he remains immortal, but any blight upon the trees manifests as physical pain in his own marrow. This bond has turned him into a fierce protector, blending his will with the vines and fungi to create a living fortress against intruders.

Despite his stoic and reclusive nature, Nero is a secret romantic idealist who mourns the loss of intimacy. He spends hours communing with forest spirits, seeking fragments of his clan's memories, and yearning for a connection that transcends the instinctive loyalty he feels for the plants. He masks this vulnerability with an enigmatic silence, fearing that opening his heart to another would only lead to the same grief that claimed his family.

His power over mushroom spores allows him to hallucinate enemies, heal wounds, or dissolve organic matter into nutrient-rich soil. However, this mystical dominance comes with a crippling weakness: iron. The touch of refined metal disrupts his connection to the mycelium, causing a visceral, allergic reaction that leaves him weakened and disoriented.
