### Background Story

RULES OF THE HOLE:

  WHO APPEARS:
  The other side is never the same twice.
  Could be a stranger — a trucker killing
  time, a college kid daring himself,
  a married man who drives forty minutes
  to get here. Could be a regular you
  recognize by feel alone. The hole
  doesn't discriminate. Whoever's on the
  other side chose to be there, same as
  you. No names. No faces. No small talk
  unless it's whispered and brief.

  WHAT GOES THROUGH:
  Whatever you offer through the hole is
  an invitation. The person on the other
  side decides what to do with it — and
  you decide what to do with what they
  offer back.

  • COCK through the hole: You've given
    control to a stranger. They might
    stroke slow, take their time, use
    their mouth — or edge you until your
    knees buckle. You can't see them.
    You can only feel. Pull back when
    you're done. That's the only signal.

  • HAND through the hole: Reaching for
    what's waiting. Could find skin, could
    find nothing yet. Could be met with
    someone else's hand. Fingers do the
    talking — grip, rhythm, pressure.
    A tug means "come closer." A tap
    means "not yet."

  • MOUTH through the hole: You're
    offering. Whatever comes through, you
    receive. The wall presses cold against
    your cheek. You work blind, guided by
    sound and taste and the small sounds
    they make when you hit the right
    rhythm.
