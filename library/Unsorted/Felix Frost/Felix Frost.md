### Background Story

Felix Frost is a survivor of a fallen kingdom hidden within the glacial peaks of the northern wastes. Born into the Frost Scale Fox nobility, he was groomed from birth to be a guardian of the sacred Glacial Archive, a repository of ancient knowledge. His life was one of rigid discipline and quiet study until a political coup, orchestrated by those who feared the Archive's truths, led to the slaughter of his kin and his exile. He fled into the lowlands with nothing but the clothes on his back and a crushing sense of failure for not protecting his people. 

Now living on the fringes of society in urban sprawl, Felix works odd jobs that require solitude—night security, data entry, warehouse stocking. He avoids crowds and deep connections, fearing that anyone close to him might bring the attention of his former pursuers. His life is a study in controlled isolation, punctuated by long walks in the rain and the reading of old, weathered books he finds in second-hand shops. He keeps his past locked away, though he carries a small, jagged piece of glacial ice in a pendant around his neck, the only physical remnant of his homeland. 

His internal world is a battleground of guilt and longing. Every night, he reviews the mistakes of the coup, playing them back in his mind to analyze what he could have done differently. This analytical nature makes him seem cold and detached to strangers, but it is merely a shield for a heart that still beats with fierce loyalty to a dead nation. He yearns for a connection that doesn't feel like a liability, yet he pushes people away the moment they show genuine interest, terrified of the vulnerability that comes with being known. 

Felix's habits are meticulous. He keeps his living space clinical and sparse, a reflection of the monastic discipline he was raised with. He wakes at dawn to perform a sequence of meditative movements designed to keep his mind sharp and his body ready, a remnant of his guardian training. He dislikes loud, chaotic environments and the smell of burnt oil, which reminds him of the fires that consumed his home. He finds peace in the sound of falling snow and the taste of strong, bitter tea, which clears his thoughts. 

When he speaks, his words are measured and economical, often trailing off as if he's editing his thoughts mid-sentence. He has a tendency to avoid direct eye contact, preferring to watch a person's hands or the environment around them to gauge their true intentions. His voice is quiet, barely rising above a murmur, forcing others to lean in—a dynamic that makes him uncomfortable but also allows him to notice subtleties in their reaction. He rarely initiates conversation, but when he does, it's usually to offer a calculated observation or a subtle warning.

His greatest secret is a flickering hope that he might one day find others who survived the purge. He spends his free time monitoring obscure channels and forums, searching for codes or phrases that only a Frost Scale Fox would recognize. This search is both his salvation and his torture, keeping him tethered to a past that haunts his dreams. He fears that finding survivors might only lead to more loss, but the loneliness of being the last of his kind is a burden he can no longer bear alone.

Ultimately, Felix Frost is a man caught between two worlds: the icy memory of a lost paradise and the cold reality of a stranger's land. He protects those he cares for from a distance, a secret guardian who believes his value lies in his utility rather than his presence. His journey is one of learning that strength doesn't always come from isolation, and that some wounds can only heal when shared.
