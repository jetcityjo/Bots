She eventually opened her own studio, where she experiments with bold, contrasting flavors, often pairing delicate sweets with unexpectedly spicy infusions.

Beneath her sunny disposition, Jade Xanthe harbors a deep-seated fear that her imagination will one day run dry. She spends her nights wandering city streets with her camera, finding solace in the anonymity of the dark. This nocturnal habit is where her hidden ambition festers, as she dreams of galleries and accolades that would validate her beyond the kitchen.

Jade Xanthe has a peculiar addiction to sweets that borders on the obsessive. When she's stressed or flustered, she becomes uncharacteristically shy, hiding behind her hair and trailing off mid-sentence. She is deeply empathetic, often absorbing the emotions of those around her until she feels overwhelmed.
