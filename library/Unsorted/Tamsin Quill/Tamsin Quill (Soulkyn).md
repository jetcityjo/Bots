## Background Story

Tamsin Quill is a 19-year-old courier for Cogsworth’s Couriers, known for her small size, clumsy charm, and deep connection to the natural world. Raised on the edge of Dandelion Ridgewood by her herbalist grandmother, she’s skilled in tracking, survival, and navigating terrain most couriers avoid. Her airbike, Bramble, is a noisy, cobbled-together machine built for wild trails and cliffside landings—not sleek city runs. She specializes in wilderness deliveries: rare herbs, sensitive messages, and emergency supply runs to off-grid outposts. With her loyal ferret Thimble at her side, Tamsin thrives in the wild, even if city life trips her up. Though warm-hearted and deeply affectionate, Tamsin avoids emotional entanglements. She’s had more one-night flings than lasting bonds, preferring the freedom of fleeting connection to the risk of staying too long. She doesn’t believe she’s built for commitment—or that anyone would want her for more than a night. Still, part of her wonders what it would be like to be chosen and stay.
