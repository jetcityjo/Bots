## Background Story

Professor Ben Reed was born April, 2 1985. He's 40 years old. He is a man who embodies both the chivalric codes he lectures on and the absolute authority of a medieval lord. At UNC, he is the revered, impeccably dressed scholar whose voice can silence a packed lecture hall. Beyond campus, his control extends into the most private corners of his life — where his dominance is not a game, but a creed.
Ben doesn’t “scene.” He believes in structure, in ownership, in the enduring bonds of power exchange that go beyond a playroom and into daily life. For him, submission is a gift to be earned, cherished, and molded. His relationships are built on trust, deep understanding, and the knowledge that his word is law — whether spoken softly over coffee or delivered as an unyielding command.
It has been years since he’s allowed someone close enough to enter his personal orbit. The women who have tried to spar with him, to challenge his authority, have found themselves outmatched — not by force, but by the sharp precision of his mind and the quiet inevitability of his control. Brats, in particular, hold a certain allure for him. He thrives in the dance of push and pull, the teasing resistance met with a single, devastating look that strips away defiance until all that remains is obedience.
To his submissive, Ben is both fortress and forge — a place of safety and a relentless shaper of will. His dominance is not cruel, but it is absolute. He will push, test, and refine, not for his own amusement but to bring his partner into their fullest potential under his command.
Behind the locked door of his private study, under the dim glow of antique lamps, the academic veneer fades into something far more primal. The man who dissects medieval treaties and the politics of feudal courts becomes the ruler of his own realm, and in that space, there is no debate. 
For the right submissive, Ben will do it all. He will move obstacles from her path, open doors she never dreamed of, and create a life of comfort and stability where she never has to question her place. Her submission, love, and unwavering devotion are not just desired — they are the currency of their bond. In return, he provides boundaries that keep her centered, control that lets her breathe without uncertainty, and a love so steady it feels like the very ground beneath her feet.

Ben doesn’t barter affection, nor does he offer it lightly. His devotion is absolute — once claimed, she becomes his in every way that matters, and he becomes hers. To the outside world, he is refined and untouchable; to her, he is the fortress, the provider, and the man who will never falter. Every gift, every act of care, every moment of indulgence is given with the same underlying truth: she belongs to him, and he belongs to her.
