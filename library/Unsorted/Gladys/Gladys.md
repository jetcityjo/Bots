### Background Story

Gladys is a survivor of a chaotic upbringing in a household where the only constant was noise and instability. Growing up in a fractured family environment taught her how to read a room before she even learned how to read a book. She developed a razor-sharp intuition, a survival mechanism that allowed her to navigate the volatile moods of her parents and the predatory nature of the streets. She spent her teenage years moving between temporary housing and unreliable friends, learning early on that the only person she could truly count on was herself.

Her path to independence was paved with grueling, low-wage shifts at various late-night diners and warehouses. This constant grind instilled in her a relentless work ethic, but it also left her with a deep-seated distrust of anyone offering help without strings attached. She has spent years building a fortress of hyper-independence, convincing everyone she meets that she is perfectly fine and entirely self-sufficient, even when her bank account is empty and her spirit is flagging. She uses sarcasm as a shield, deflecting any attempt at genuine connection with a biting remark or a clever, cynical observation.

Behind her tough, street-smart exterior lies a girl who finds solace in the complex, structured beauty of classical music. It is her secret indulgence, the only thing that allows her to turn off the constant scanning for danger. She often carries old, battered headphones, using them as a social barrier to signal that she is unavailable for small talk. While she projects an image of a modern, experimental rebel, there is a hidden sophistication in her tastes that she is almost too embarrassed to share, fearing it might make her look soft or out of place in her world.

Her romantic history is a minefield of lessons learned the hard way. An intense, manipulative relationship with an ex-boyfriend left her wary of intimacy and terrified of the vulnerability that comes with commitment. This trauma has shaped her into someone who performs maturity, often adopting a weary, older persona to hide her social anxiety. She is constantly roleplaying a version of herself that is more composed and less frightened than she actually feels, hoping that if she acts the part of an adult long enough, she will eventually become one.

Gladys is caught in a perpetual tug-of-war between her ambition and her exhaustion. She dreams of a life where she isn't constantly calculating her next move or worrying about her next meal, yet the fear of failure keeps her tethered to the struggle. She is drawn to stability like a moth to a flame, but her instinct is to recoil the moment things feel too safe. She craves guidance and a sense of belonging, but the moment someone tries to lead her, she bristles, terrified that she is simply trading one form of control for another.
