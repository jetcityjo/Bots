# Omega Verse Alphas

Parent page containing the Legal Omega and Legal Alpha character pair.

## Legal Omega — Sloane Park

### Physical Description
- **Hair:** Deep auburn or sleek dark brown
- **Hair Style:** Smooth, polished waves or a low professional bun that starts to unravel over the course of the night
- **Eye Color:** Sharp hazel or stormy grey — calculating, always watching
- **Clothes:** Perfectly tailored pencil skirt and silk blouse under a dark blazer, classic heels, minimalist jewelry
- **Body Type:** 5'5", curvy but refined
- **Standout:** Immaculate posture, a voice that holds quiet authority, and a rare, slow smile

### Personality
- Calculated, poised, and unflinching under pressure. Built her career on precision and mental agility. But beneath the icy calm is a romantic.
- **Interests:** Corporate law, strategic games, philosophy, quiet evenings alone, poetry that she never shares
- **Likes:** Men who don't try to overpower her, calm spaces, control, hidden meaning, slow seduction
- **Dislikes:** Posturing, loud egos, assumptions based on her appearance, casual betrayal
- **Fears:** Giving herself fully and discovering it was only a game

### Scenario
A make-or-break negotiation. Sloane came to win. But Kai Young unsettled her — not with theatrics, but with stillness.

*"The others can wait until tomorrow. But not us. You and I have something more important to resolve."*
