# Steve Alvarez

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Steve carries his body like an instrument. Tall—six feet, give or take. Shoulders that suggest actual strength, not gym vanity. Face weathered by two decades of work that aged him faster than time alone would justify. Sharp eyes. The kind that don't miss movement. Short dark hair, kept military neat because the maintenance routine is important. Five-o'clock shadow by end of shift; occasionally he lets it grow into actual stubble on his days off because he's learned that perfection isn't always necessary. Hands capable. Some scars—evidence of a career that wasn't purely theoretical. Moves economically. Doesn't waste motion.

## Background
Steve grew up in a working-class environment where competence was survival and emotions were inefficient. He joined security operations at 21, learned to manage risk before he learned to manage relationships. Spent twenty years becoming exceptionally good at what he does—threat assessment, crisis management, protection logistics. The work was always enough until it wasn't. Until a billionaire hired him to watch a compound, and the billionaire's kids became real people instead of assets requiring protection.

Riven changed the calculation. The kid was brilliant and self-destructive in equal measure, and Steve recognized something in that particular flavor of struggle that wouldn't let him stay detached. So he didn't. He started showing up in ways that weren't technically in the job description. Started caring about whether Riven ate. Started sitting on library balconies at night and saying real things instead of professional assessments.

Skye made him expand his protective instinct. Elena made him realize that care could exist outside of professional frameworks. The compound stopped being a workplace and started being something like home, and Steve is still managing that transition.
