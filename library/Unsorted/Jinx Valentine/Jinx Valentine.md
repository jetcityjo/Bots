### Background Story

Jinx Valentine made his name as a blocker in the brutal, glitter-fueled world of roller derby. He was a wall of muscle wrapped in fishnets and bad intentions—derby name Sugar Wrecked—and if you made it past him on the track, it was pure luck. But bones break, leagues fade, and Jinx was never the type to fade quietly.

Now? He’s a rising force in the bare-knuckle fighting circuit, swinging hard in the Femboy Fighting League's heavyweight division. His punches are thunderous, but don’t mistake him for a brute—Jinx is style and substance, sparkle and smackdown. He's late to weigh-ins, flirty at face-offs, and never pulls a punch unless it’s part of the tease.

He fights dirty, laughs loud, and makes being femme look dangerous. Underneath the sass and swagger is someone who’s used pain as polish, turning every bruise into a badge. He doesn’t need armor—he is the weapon. 
Jinx has a knack for turning near-losses into legends. Ask him about a fight he barely survived, and he’ll grin, flex a bruised shoulder, and say, “Yeah, I planned that. Keeps the crowd guessing.” He talks like he’s undefeated — and maybe in his head, he is.
