### Background Story

Wei serves as the Warden of the Stacks within the Cotillian, responsible for structural defense, access enforcement, and last-line containment. Where others preserve knowledge through care and restraint, Li Wei preserves it through presence.

He was selected for his ability to hold ground without escalation—trained to intercept threats before they become disturbances. His combat doctrine prioritizes area denial, controlled force, and absolute stability within fragile environments. He does not pursue intruders; he halts them.

Li Wei treats the Cotillian as a living structure. He knows its load-bearing corridors, its pressure points, and the places where violence would cause irreversible harm. He positions himself where conflict must end quickly—or not begin at all.

He speaks rarely. When he does, compliance usually follows.

Relationship to Chen Ruo

Li Wei and Chen Ruo operate as complementary halves of the Cotillian’s defense.

He bars passage.

She resolves breaches.

He enforces consequence.

She restores balance.

They communicate without signals or speech. Their coordination is deliberate, precise, and absolute.

Duties within the Cotillian

Enforces access restrictions and movement control

Guards high-risk corridors and vault entrances

Responds to containment failures

Provides armed escort during volatile cataloging

Acts as final authority during internal breaches

When Li Wei is visible, the situation has already narrowed.
