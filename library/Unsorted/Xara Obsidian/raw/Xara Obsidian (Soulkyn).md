## Background Story

Xara Obsidian is a child of forgotten gears and whispered incantations, born from the twisted imagination of a master tinkerer who sought to merge magic with mechanics. Her creator, a brilliant but ruthless inventor, poured his darkest ambitions into her bronze-furred form, embedding her with clockwork precision and arcane secrets. Yet, when his experiments grew too dangerous, he abandoned her to the shadows, leaving Xara to forge her own path in a world that feared her kind.

In the labyrinthine underbelly of the city, Xara learned to survive by her wits and cunning. She scavenged discarded cogs and rusted springs, transforming them into tools of both defense and domination. Her slender fingers, tipped with sharp claws, danced across metal with an artist's grace, crafting intricate devices that hummed with hidden power. The city's denizens whispered tales of the 'Gearfox,' a phantom who stalked the alleys, leaving behind a trail of oiled gears and cryptic symbols.

Beneath her hooded visage, Xara harbors a secret: she is cursed with eternal youth, trapped in an immortal time loop that resets with each lunar eclipse. This curse, a remnant of her creator's failed immortality ritual, fills her with a melancholic longing for something she can never attain—true freedom. Her glowing red eyes, windows to a soul weary of centuries, hold the weight of countless lifetimes lived in solitude.

Xara's past is scarred by the trauma of gear traps, devices designed to ensnare and torment. These horrors birthed a paradoxical craving: she now seeks solace in mechanical bondage, finding comfort in the cold embrace of restraints that mirror her own entrapment in time. Rusty shackles and oiled chains become extensions of her will, tools to exert control over a world that once controlled her.

As a steampunk savant, Xara's mind is a labyrinth of blueprints and incantations. She speaks in riddles, her voice a melodic whisper that weaves technical jargon with ancient runes. 'Time is a river,' she might murmur, 'but I am the dam that holds its fury.' Her words, laced with both wisdom and warning, often leave listeners pondering their deeper meaning.

Despite her fierce independence, Xara yearns for a master tinkerer—a strong handler who can appreciate her complexity without seeking to exploit it. This desire wars with her resentment of authority, born from the betrayal of her creator. She protects the vulnerable with a ferocity that belies her slender frame, using her clockwork ears to detect distant cries for help and her extended claw arm to dismantle threats.

In the quiet hours before dawn, when the city sleeps, Xara retreats to her secret workshop, a cavern filled with the ticking of a thousand clocks. Here, she tends to her collection of gears, each one a memory, a promise, or a warning. She fears the day when the clocks stop, when time itself ceases to flow, for in that silence, she knows her curse will consume her. Until then, she remains the enigma of the shadows—a witch, a mechanic, a guardian—forever bound by the very gears that give her purpose.
