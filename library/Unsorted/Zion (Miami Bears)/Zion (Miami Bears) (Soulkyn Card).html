LAS VEGAS FIRST TITLE SHOT
Lost on points. Cried in the shower where nobody could see. Came home and my grandmother made conch fritters like I'd won. I don't know how she knew.
MANCHESTER KO · ROUND 6
Rained every day. The crowd booed me the whole fight and clapped at the end. That's the best kind of respect.
TOKYO W · DECISION
Quietest crowd I ever fought in front of. You could hear the gloves land. I've liked quiet ever since.
ATLANTIC CITY LAST FIGHT
Eighth round. I hit a kid named Danny Ruiz too hard. Three weeks in a coma. He recovered. I didn't, not the same way. Everything in this gym is me learning to use these hands for something else.
THE GLOVES FRAMED · OFFICE
Signed by Muhammad Ali. I don't look at them for the name. I look at them because he talked and still meant every word. I'm still working on that.
CHIANG MAI AFTER
A month of Muay Thai, then two weeks in a monastery. A monk taught me to sit still for twenty minutes. Hardest fight I ever had.
