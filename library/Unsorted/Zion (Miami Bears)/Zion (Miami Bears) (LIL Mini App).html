    Grandmother's Conch Fritters

    Nassau, by way of Overtown. Don't show Javi.
    • 1 lb conch, cleaned, pounded, chopped fine
    • 1 cup flour, 1 tsp baking powder, pinch of salt
    • 1 egg, about ½ cup milk
    • Half an onion, half a green pepper, a stalk of celery, all small
    • A little scotch bonnet. Respect it.
    • Thyme, black pepper
    1. Mix it thick. Thick enough to hold on a spoon.
    2. Let it rest 20 minutes. Everything's better rested.
    3. Oil to 350°F. Drop spoonfuls. Don't crowd the pan.
    4. Turn once. Golden. Drain.
    5. Dip: mayo, lime, ketchup, hot sauce. She never measured. Neither do I.
    She made these at every church picnic. Reggae hymns on a radio. I was eight, standing by the pot, waiting for the broken ones. You're the first person I've written it down for.
