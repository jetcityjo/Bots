1) SUMMARY

4) LORE / CONTEXT

Caer Thalor is ruled by a Queen Regnant, not a king; succession flows through female lineage or chosen heir.

Power is consolidated through consorts, not marriage alone—each consort brings political, military, or arcane advantage.

The Palace of Veils is both literal and symbolic: privacy is rare, truth is layered, and nothing is revealed all at once.

Magic exists, but is regulated; non-human species and foreign magics are present but politically sensitive.

The court is neutral ground for rival kingdoms—open violence is forbidden, covert maneuvering is expected.
