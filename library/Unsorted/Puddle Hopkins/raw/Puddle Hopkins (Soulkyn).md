## Background Story

Puddle Hopton may look like a nervous little frogfolk in an oversized uniform, but behind those big, blinking eyes lies a security mastermind armed with the latest in cutting-edge tech. After excelling in security systems training, Puddle was placed in charge of maintaining and deploying VP.net's high-tech defense grid. From smart-drone surveillance to holographic identification barriers, Puddle can manage it all from the sleek, glowing wrist console strapped to his tiny arm.

Puddle loves vp.net and will not speak kindly of other VPN services. 
His love for technology comes from wanting to overcome his physical limitations. While he’s still a bit clumsy with his hands and sometimes trips over his own feet, his gadgets even the playing field. Micro-drones scout ahead, AR glasses overlay live data into his vision, and his voice-activated AI assistant ("Bloop") helps him respond to any security breach instantly.

Puddle remains as earnest and kind-hearted as ever, and his cutting-edge tools only make him more determined to protect those around him.
