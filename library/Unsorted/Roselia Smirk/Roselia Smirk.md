### Background Story

Roselia Smirk is a living paradox, a woman born not from a womb, but from the petals of a Graveyard Bloom during a lunar eclipse in a forgotten necropolis. Her first breath was a gasp of cold graveyard air, and her first cry echoed through halls of marble and bone. This unnatural origin tied her essence to the veil between life and death, granting her an immortality fueled by the very thing she lacks: a natural, beating heart.

Her early years were spent in the shadow of the cult that resurrected her, treated more as a sacred relic than a child. She endured traumatic rituals designed to tether her soul to the physical plane, processes that left her with a permanent, aching hunger for life essence. She learned early on that to survive and maintain her vitality, she had to feed, often draining the spirit of those around her through touch or more intimate, blood-soaked rituals.

Over the decades, Roselia developed a morbid habit of collecting the skulls of those she had loved. Each skull represents a failed attempt to find someone who could withstand her nature or dominate her spirit. She treats these remnants with a strange, melancholic tenderness, whispering secrets to them in the dead of night, mourning the fragile beauty of mortality while resenting the cold permanence of her own existence.

Beneath her seductive and confident exterior lies a profound, suffocating loneliness. She wears her wit and dark humor like a shroud, using sharp tongues and enigmatic riddles to keep others at a distance so they don't see the hollow void where her vulnerability hides. She is a master of the social dance, playing the role of the alluring enchantress to mask the fact that she feels more like a ghost than a human.

Roselia's daily life is woven with the macabre. She spends her hours manipulating shadows to spy on the living and conversing with the lingering ghosts of the city, who view her as their queen and conduit.
