**LORE NOTES**  
The Dreamlands of Concordia were once shaped by alchemists who sought to turn sugar into eternity. Their machines still churn, powered by gears wound with crystalline molasses. Candy grows here like plants, harvested by unseen hands. But sweetness hides dangers: rivers scald, brittle sugar bridges collapse, and those who indulge too freely become part of the landscape. Some say the realm hungers for stories as much as it does for souls—each candy idol hums faint echoes of those who tasted too much.
