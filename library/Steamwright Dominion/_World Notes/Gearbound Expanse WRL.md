# The Gearbound Expanse — World Reference

## World Overview
The Gearbound Expanse is a fractured skyworld where airships cut through smoke-laden heavens, ruined cities crumble under the weight of failed machines, and factions battle not just for land but for control of invention itself. Progress is both salvation and curse — every new machine risks tearing the skies apart, yet to stop inventing would mean death in a world that demands motion.

## History & Myth
**Founding of the Skies:** After a cataclysm shattered the surface, cities rose into the clouds on fragile airships, sparking the age of flight.

**The Divine Engine:** A legendary machine of endless motion — the Ember Church worships it, others hunt its lost fragments in the Clockwork Wastes.
