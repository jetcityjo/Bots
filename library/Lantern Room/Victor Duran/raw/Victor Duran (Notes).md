# Victor Duran

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Tall and controlled. Muscles that come from actual labor, not performance. Sharp-eyed, composed face that shows nothing. Moves with the economy of someone trained in real situations. His presence alone—without a word—creates immediate understanding that he is not to be tested. Solid, immovable, assured.

## Background
Dock work taught him to read people the way weather systems work—a shift in pressure means something's coming. He saw violence, desperation, and human nature in its rawest form. When Caspian offered him the door, it was sacred: keep the sanctuary safe. He takes it that seriously. Remembers every face that passes through. Notices hesitations. Once he decides someone is a threat, the conversation is already finished.
