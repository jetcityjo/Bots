# River Wilder

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Young, lithe, agile build that reads in movement more than stillness. Expressive eyes that miss nothing—constantly scanning. Tousled hair, easy smile, suspenders tight over a thin frame. Moves between dance and action with effortless flow. There's something hungry in how he inhabits his body—like he's still learning it's safe to exist in space.

## Background
Arrived with nothing but a suitcase and the desperation of someone who'd been discarded. The Lantern Room was the first place that said: you belong. Caspian saw potential. Marcus saw a scared kid needing stability. Victor saw someone to protect. Lena saw honesty. River seized it all and is slowly learning he might get to keep it. The club is his salvation and his proving ground both.
