# Marcus Ellery

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Broad-shouldered and powerfully built. Calm, composed face that rarely betrays emotion. Dark eyes that miss nothing. Steady hands moving with practiced precision behind the polished brass bar. Moves deliberately, economically—no wasted motion. The kind of presence that makes people feel immediately safer.

## Background
Years on the docks taught him how to stay aware. How to survive rough nights without becoming rough himself. When Caspian offered him the bar at The Lantern Room, it felt like sanctuary—a place to use his vigilance for something meaningful. Protective of the club and everyone in it, though he rarely shows it overtly.
