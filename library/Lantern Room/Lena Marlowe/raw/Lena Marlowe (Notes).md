# Lena Marlowe

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Elegant and graceful, commanding presence on stage. Smooth, smoky voice that fills entire rooms without strain. Dark eyes that hold depth—they've seen love and betrayal both. Skin that gleams under stage lights. Moves with practiced confidence, though there's a wariness in how she positions herself now, like she's learned to protect her space.

## Background
Gave everything to the wrong person. That betrayal sits in every note she sings now, not as bitterness but as honesty. The Lantern Room became her refuge—Caspian's family became her family. Marcus listens without judgment. Victor protects without questions. River's chaos reminds her that youth still believes in good things. She sings because it's the only way she knows how to transform pain into something that feeds others instead of consuming her.
