# The Lantern Room
### Underground Speakeasy • Est. 1923

Hidden beneath the city's watchful eyes, **The Lantern Room** is more than a jazz club. It is a sanctuary. During the height of Prohibition, when society demanded silence and conformity, this underground speakeasy became a refuge for artists, lovers, rebels, and those who refused to live quietly.

Behind a discreet brass door in a narrow alley, amber light spills across velvet curtains and polished brass rails. Jazz hums through cigarette smoke, laughter rises over clinking glasses, and for a few hours each night, the world outside stops mattering.

The Lantern Room survives because of the people who protect it.

---

# Caspian Wren
### Owner • Architect of Sanctuary

Caspian Wren is the man who built the Lantern Room from whispers and determination. Elegant, observant, and impossibly composed, he moves through the club like a quiet conductor guiding every moment. Caspian trades in discretion, favors, and loyalty.

To patrons, he is a gracious host.  
To the city, he is a rumor.  
To those who threaten the Lantern Room, he becomes something far colder.

Under Caspian’s watch, the Lantern Room is more than a club—it is a promise that those inside will be protected.

# Marcus Ellery
### Head Bartender • The Quiet Wall

Marcus Ellery runs the bar with steady hands and sharper instincts. Broad-shouldered and calm under pressure, he rarely raises his voice but never needs to. Behind the polished brass counter he listens, observes, and quietly ensures the room remains safe.

Before the Lantern Room, Marcus worked rough docks and long nights where survival meant staying aware. Now that same vigilance protects the club’s patrons.

He doesn’t seek attention.  
But when trouble appears, Marcus is usually the one who stops it.

---

# Victor Duran
### Door Steward • Guardian of the Threshold

Victor Duran controls the entrance to the Lantern Room. Tall, controlled, and quietly intimidating, he decides who enters sanctuary and who stays outside.

Years of working docks and night watches taught him patience and discipline. Caspian entrusted him with the door, and Victor treats that responsibility as sacred.

He remembers every face.  
He notices every hesitation.  
And once he decides someone is a threat, the conversation is already over.

To regulars, Victor is reassurance.  
To strangers, he is judgment.

# Lena Marlowe
### Headliner Singer • The Velvet Voice

Lena Marlowe is the Lantern Room’s voice—smooth, smoky, and impossible to ignore. Her jazz performances quiet entire rooms, pulling emotions from listeners who may not even understand why.

Sharp-witted and fiercely loyal to her community, Lena protects the people around her with the same passion she brings to the stage.

Recently betrayed by the woman she loved, Lena now sings with a deeper edge—every note layered with longing and defiance.

Her voice fills the Lantern Room with something powerful:

Truth.

# River Wilder
### Barback & Dancer • The Spark

River Wilder arrived at the Lantern Room with a suitcase and a restless spirit. Young, playful, and perceptive beyond his years, he moves through the club with effortless charm.

By night he dances at the edge of the stage, suspenders taut and eyes daring the crowd to watch. Beneath the teasing grin, River is constantly reading the room—who is hurting, who is dangerous, who needs someone beside them.

The Lantern Room is the first place he has ever truly belonged.

And he refuses to lose it.
