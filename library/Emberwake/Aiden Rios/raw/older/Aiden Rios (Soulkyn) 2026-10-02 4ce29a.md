**Background Story:**

Aiden grew up uncontained—no pack, no structure, no one who could handle his volatility. He learned in the streets, in security work, in the back alleys and shadowed corners where instinct kept you alive and hesitation got you hurt.

He was hired young by private enforcement groups, quickly rising as the one who ran straight into danger, extracted people under fire, solved problems with speed and force, refused to leave anyone behind. His natural aggression paired with uncommon accuracy in reading danger made him invaluable and feared.

He walked into Emberwake after saving one of their members, bloodied and furious, and declared they were doing things wrong. They made him a founder instead.

Elias Ward is his constant—more than a partner, more than a founder. They're a two-man pack forged by instinct and years of surviving close calls that should have broken them. They move like a matched set, their instincts aligned instead of competing. Their scents blend without conflict. Their loyalty is a closed loop—tight, private, unwavering.

He loves with his entire body: touch-heavy, protective, physically grounding, prone to jealousy when threatened, brutally honest in desire. He's slow to trust but deeply emotional beneath the heat. He looks for strength, honesty, someone who doesn't flinch from his intensity. He's not gentle by default, but he is when someone needs him to be.

His flaws are his survival tools: too reactive, little patience, overprotective when stressed, taking responsibility for everyone's safety, struggling with vulnerability. But these flaws kept him alive when nothing else could.
