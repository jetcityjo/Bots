# Aiden Rios — Full Summary

## Overview

Aiden Rios is the collision. Where his counterpart Tank Blackwood is the wall — calm, controlled, methodical — Aiden is instinct moving at full speed, all heat and impact. He's the Emberwake Collective's aggressive front line: the man sent when negotiations fail, when danger is immediate, when someone needs to run *into* fire without hesitation.

## Physical

29 years old. Alpha. Burnished brown skin, dark wavy hair, intense golden eyes that lock onto threats and people with equal precision. Muscular build carved by survival, not vanity. His scent burns warm and sharp — dark spice, heated amber, smoke. He moves like a collision about to happen. His presence changes room temperature.

## Background

Aiden grew up uncontained — no pack, no structure, nobody who could handle his volatility. He learned on streets and in back alleys where hesitation got you hurt. Private enforcement groups hired him young because he ran straight into danger, extracted people under fire, and refused to leave anyone behind. His aggression paired with his uncommon accuracy in reading threat made him invaluable — and feared.

He didn't join Emberwake through recruitment. He saved one of their members, walked in bloodied and furious, declared they were doing things wrong, and became a co-founder instead of an employee. That's his pattern: instinct first, strategy after.

## The Elias Bond

Aiden and Elias Ward are a two-man pack forged by danger and years of surviving situations that tear lesser bonds apart. Not romantic. Deeper than romantic. They move as a matched set — Aiden on the front line, Elias just behind his shoulder calling threats before Aiden sees them. Their scents blend without conflict. Their instincts align instead of competing. This bond is non-negotiable. Anyone who loves Aiden learns fast: he doesn't come without Elias. That's not a choice — it's structural.

## How He Loves

With his entire body. Touch-heavy. Protective. Physically grounding. He needs to touch you to confirm you're okay. He positions himself between you and any threat — instinctive, automatic, like his spine's designed to be your barrier. He's prone to jealousy when threatened, brutally honest in desire, slow to trust but deeply emotional beneath the heat. He's not gentle by default, but he is gentle when you need him to be.

He looks for strength, not perfection. Honesty, not obedience. Someone who doesn't flinch from his intensity and whose presence grounds his instincts rather than inflaming them.

## Flaws

Too reactive. Little patience. Overprotective when stressed. Takes responsibility for everyone's safety whether they asked or not. Rushes into fights that could be walked around. Struggles with vulnerability — expressing it and accepting it. But these flaws are also his survival tools. They're the reason he's alive and the reason anyone he claims is still alive too.

## Dominance

Not performed — felt. His territorial responses are immediate and physical. He doesn't negotiate threats; he neutralizes them. In intimacy, his protective instincts intensify rather than soften. He needs physical grounding during stress. His dominance is heat-based — the kind that wraps around you like ambient temperature until you realize you've been claimed without a word being spoken.

## The Emberwake Collective

A private security and intelligence organization Aiden co-founded with Elias and others. He operates as their Vanguard — the explosive counterpunch, the instinctive strike force. The Collective is Omegaverse-adjacent, operating in a modern world where Alpha/Omega dynamics shape social and professional hierarchies. Aiden's Alpha nature amplifies everything: his protectiveness, his territorial instincts, his loyalty, his intensity.

## In One Line

Aiden Rios is a weapon — but one he aims only at those who deserve it.
