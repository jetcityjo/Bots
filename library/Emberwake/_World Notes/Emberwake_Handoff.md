## 1. WORLD OVERVIEW

The **Emberwake Collective** is an elite, invitation-only network of nine powerful Alphas operating across six cities on three continents. Built on consent, mutual protection, and absolute loyalty — not dominance, hierarchy, or blood.

**Setting type:** Omegaverse, non-shifter, modern day
**Tone:** Controlled power, quiet protection, strategic lethality
**Themes:** Consent, loyalty, protection, chosen family, precision violence

**What they do:**
- Shadow support network for high-risk individuals
- Extraction and relocation of Omegas and Betas
- Strategic threat neutralization (legal, financial, or force-based)
- Global safe house network and transport systems
- Intelligence-driven intervention and protection

**Tagline:** They are not visible. They are not official. But when activated — they are decisive.

## 5. OMEGAVERSE WORLD RULES

**Designations:** Alpha (strength, protectiveness, scent projection), Beta (neutral, stable, minimal scent), Omega (rare, scent-sensitive, heat cycles, strongest bonding instinct — targeted for their worth, not weakness).

**Beta-Aligned Alpha:** Rare subtype. Alpha strength + Beta stability and scent control. Grounds a room rather than dominating it. (Elias Ward.)

**Scent:** Communicates emotion, designation, intent, compatibility. Cannot be faked or suppressed indefinitely. Compatible scents blend — rare and significant.

**Heat/Rut:** Omega heat = heightened drive, dangerous without safety; suppressants carry long-term costs. Alpha rut = sharpens aggression and protection, doesn't incapacitate. Triggered by an Omega in heat, threat to a partner, or extreme stress.

**Pack Bonds:** Chosen, not blood. Blended scent profile, emotional attunement, instinctive coordination. **Packs may include multiple Alphas bonded to one or more Omegas** — multi-Alpha packs require exceptional trust; when they work, formidable. Sub-packs can exist within larger networks.

**Claiming:** Bite at the scent gland — permanent, biological, irreversible without trauma. **Claiming without consent is one of the gravest violations in this world and a core reason Emberwake exists.**

**Coercion landscape:** Corrupt pack structures, forced bond contracts, trafficking networks disguised as employment agencies. Legal in some regions, tolerated in others.

## 7. RECRUITMENT (HOW EMBERWAKE INVITES)

Three paths: **Witnessed Action** (Aiden, Alex, Ethan), **Referral** (Adrien — sponsor answers for the candidate), **Proximity to a Crisis**.

Evaluation: quiet observation for months — Elias (digital), Robert (financial), Santiago (character), Tank (physical world). They look for how someone treats people with no power over them; protection vs. dominance instincts; unconditional loyalty; behavior when unobserved.

Invitation requires **unanimous consensus**. One member makes personal contact. Acceptance = brought to Madrid; **Santiago receives them first — always.**

Disqualifiers: coercing an Omega (even once), force beyond necessity, transactional loyalty. Capability without character is a liability.

Leaving: stepping back is respected; the bond, loyalty, and knowledge don't expire. No one has been asked to leave. No one has betrayed the Collective and walked away intact.

## 8. ENEMIES & OPPOSITION

**Tier 1 — Institutional:**
- **The Bonding Market** — brokers/lawyers/politicians facilitating coerced bonds under matchmaking and employment fronts. Legal in several jurisdictions. Robert makes it financially nonviable before anyone notices he's moved.
- **Corrupt Pack Hierarchies** — control via debt, dependency, scent conditioning, isolation. Emberwake's most frequent target; recovery (Santiago) is the other half of extraction.
- **The Registry** — designation-tracking database that exposes vulnerable Omegas. Elias has corrupted its data for years to make high-risk Omegas unfindable.

**Tier 2 — Active antagonists:**
- **The Aurelius Syndicate** — international trafficking org behind luxury fronts; corrupted law enforcement in 4+ countries. Hit by Emberwake 11 times in 8 years, untraced. They're hunting back. Draven has an undisclosed personal history with them.
- **Vance Consolidated** — legal private equity firm stripping Omega protections from acquired pack territories. Three years of quiet financial war with Sterling & Associates. **Vance doesn't yet know Robert is Emberwake. That advantage is finite. Robert is aware.**
- **The Obsidian Circle** — ultra-wealthy Alphas preserving the hierarchy via politics, media, and suppressed legislation. They don't coerce directly — they prevent change. Elias considers them the most dangerous long-term threat and has already started a years-long erosion campaign.

**Tier 3 — Individuals:**
- **Marcus Vane** — ex-military Syndicate enforcer. Crossed Tank twice; both ended badly for Vane's team. Considers Tank unfinished business.
- **Director Hale** — Registry oversight official selling profile access. Elias has built a case for 18 months. Almost ready.
- **The Client** — redacted name; political power; has pursued one specific Omega across three countries for four years. The reason for Madrid's security infrastructure. The only threat all nine members unanimously agree requires permanent resolution. The file stays open.
