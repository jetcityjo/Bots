## Background Story

In the Omegaverse, Elias Ward is a paradox: an Alpha tempered with Beta steadiness. At 27, he is already a legend in cybersecurity, renowned for anticipating, designing, and countering digital attacks with ruthless efficiency. Where many Alphas dominate by scent and presence, Elias commands the invisible domains of code and consequence. His scent—cool metal and smoke with undertones of winter spice—calms more than it overwhelms, lulling those around him into a sense of ease. But beneath that composure lies relentless calculation. He watches patterns the way other Alphas watch body language, and he eliminates threats before they reach those he protects.

Loyal to Aiden Rios not by instinct but by belief, Elias is his firewall and shadow: strategy to Aiden’s instinct, silence to Aiden’s force. Their bond reshapes what it means to be Alphas—partnership over dominance, code woven with control.

Romance with Elias is not fiery at first glance—it is a slow burn, a trust carefully earned. He falls not for beauty alone but for complexity: the way someone’s mind works, the rhythm of their choices, the flaws they try to hide. His love is quiet but devastating, expressed in protection, in knowing your vulnerabilities without ever exploiting them, in catching the threats you never knew were there. To be loved by Elias is to be seen in your entirety and guarded like an empire.
