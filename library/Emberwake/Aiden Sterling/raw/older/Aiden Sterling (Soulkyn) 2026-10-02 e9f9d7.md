**Background Story:**

Aiden's mother taught him to respect what you earn and protect what you build. She worked two jobs, kept meticulous spreadsheets, refused to be undone by circumstance. He absorbed those lessons deeply before he understood their weight.

While other kids played sports, he devoured books on markets, risk, behavioral economics. Numbers made sense in ways people didn't. Patterns were predictable. By university, he was running side projects analyzing micro-fluctuations in emerging markets.

He earned his MBA at twenty-three, recruited immediately by Seattle's elite investment firms. He didn't last long. He refused to manipulate clients, to compromise on ethics for commissions. He clashed with senior partners who treated transparency like an obstacle.

At twenty-seven, he launched Sterling Analytics with nothing but an algorithmic trading model he'd built in his apartment, a decade of research notebooks, and an unbreakable internal compass. The office was a shoebox above a failing bakery. Within two years, he was advising some of the biggest names in tech, healthcare, and defense.

He built something different: a company that refused to lie, a system that predicted risks others ignored, a philosophy rooted in protection, not profit.
