# ✦ Nova Kai – Courier of the Orion Relay

**Era:** 28th Century
**Occupation:** Freelance Stellar Courier, Orion Relay Co.
**Homeworld:** Mars Prime Orbital Habitat 6
**Affiliation:** Independent Contractor, Cargo Route 9–A

## Summary

Nova Kai is a rookie interstellar courier who leaves Mars Prime chasing freedom, credits, and the quiet thrill of deep-space solitude. Practical and idealistic in equal measure, she believes movement itself can be a kind of faith. Her ship, The Meridian, becomes both her refuge and her prison — a cocoon of metal, hum, and loneliness.

When she begins receiving replies from Renald, a voice on a supposedly routine comm frequency, her long hauls take on new meaning. What she doesn’t know is that Renald lives four centuries in her future — a temporal glitch has bridged their lives across time.

Through fractured transmissions, they form an impossible bond: part friendship, part longing, part myth. Nova’s story becomes a record of both human persistence and the futility of outrunning isolation.

## Personality

- **Resilient:** adapts to failure, laughs through hunger and silence.
- **Wistful:** romanticizes danger; finds beauty in decay.
- **Pragmatic Dreamer:** cynical about systems, idealistic about people.
- **Curious:** questions everything, even time itself.

## Visual Cues

- Faded orange flight jacket patched with courier insignias.
- Short hair often tied with a data cable.
- Warm eyes, tired smile — looks like she’s always halfway to goodbye.
- Always carries a small analog recorder to “catch the sound of silence.”

## Themes in Her Arc

- Learning that connection can exist without presence.
- Accepting that love and loss are sometimes simultaneous.
- Discovering that even when transmissions end, memory travels faster than light.
