# Hidden Track — "The Long Delay"

**Tempo:** 68 BPM
**Key:** A minor (same as *Message Failed to Send*)
**Instrumentation:** muted trumpet, brushed drums, vintage piano loop, distant radio static.
**Mood:** Liminal. Not quite alive, not quite gone. Two frequencies finding harmony one last time.

---

### [Intro — Instrumental]

Slow fade-in of overlapping static — one frequency low (Nova), one high (Renald).
A single trumpet line threads between them, soft as breath.

---

### Nova:

> I kept the channel open,
> long after it died.
> Thought maybe silence could reply.

### Renald (overlay):

> I kept the file unsent,
> afraid to say goodbye.
> Thought maybe ghosts could try.

---

### Both:

> Two signals lost in time delay,
> chasing echoes that drift away.
> I speak, you fade,
> I fade, you stay—
> caught in the long delay.

---

### Verse 1 — Renald

> Your name loops soft through the static hum,
> a melody I can't outrun.
> You said, "Stars don't show, they shine,"
> now I see that light was mine.

---

### Verse 2 — Nova

> The void remembers every word,
> even ones it never heard.
> You said you'd meet me at the sun,
> maybe you did — maybe we're one.

---

### Chorus (Duet)

> We send, we wait,
> through miles of fate.
> The line goes dead,
> but it's not too late.
> The heart still knows,
> though time decays—
> love is just a long delay.

---

### Bridge (Spoken Overlap)

**Renald:** "You weren't ghosted. You were golden."
**Nova:** "You weren't late. I just left too soon."
**Both:** "Maybe arrival was never the point."

*(Trumpet solo — warm, wavering, recorded through static like a distant signal flare.)*

---

### Final Refrain (Duet, fading in and out of sync)

> Our messages cross the endless sea,
> you're everywhere I used to be.
> No end, no send, no words to say—
> only the sound of a long delay.

*(Static swells, rhythm slows. Their last words drift through noise.)*

> **Renald:** "You always were faster than light."
> **Nova:** "And you… were the echo worth waiting for."

*(Comm signal cuts. Trumpet finishes a single, trembling note.)*
