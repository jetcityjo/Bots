# Message Failed to Send

*Track 8 — the ghosting / ending*

**Tempo:** 68 BPM | brushed drums, somber piano, muted trumpet in minor key
**Mood:** grief in quiet, resignation

### Verse 1

> Dock lights shimmer, relay hums,
> I wait where every signal comes.
> I've sent a dozen pings your way,
> each one vanishes into gray.

### Verse 2

> Bought a drink I couldn't taste,
> time drips slow, nothing to waste.
> Voices pass, they're not your tone,
> I check the logs — I'm still alone.

### Pre-Chorus

> Your promise replays,
> two days, two days.
> I count them out,
> they fade away.

### Chorus

> Message failed to send,
> no reply to amend.
> I waited till the stars went dim,
> still pretending I heard from him.

### Verse 3

> I play your voice through static hiss,
> a ghost in wires, a half-sent kiss.
> The system blinks—final receipt:
> "User offline. Connection complete."

### Bridge (spoken / breaking)

> "Renald… you there?
> You can say goodbye, you know.
> Or don't.
> I'll deliver the silence myself."

### Final Chorus

> Message failed to send,
> I'm out of routes to bend.
> The stars don't wait, they just begin,
> I fade, I float, I breathe you in.

### Outro (instrumental)

> Soft hum of the ship, faint trumpet reprise of "Dock 6, Bay 9."
> A final click, comm light dies.

*(Whispered line:)*

> "Courier Nova Kai — final delivery complete."

*Then nothing but static.*
