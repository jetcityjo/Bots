# Orion Gambit: Canon Recap (July 2026)

## The Universe

- **The Orion Gambit** is the larger sci-fi setting.
- **Transmission Delay** is one story within it: a concept album and narrative centered on Nova Kai and Renald.
- Humanity is connected by the **Orion Relay Network**, where independent couriers carry cargo, messages, secrets, and sometimes hope across inhabited space.
- The setting mixes space noir, jazz, lonely truck-stop culture, old analog technology, and futuristic communications.

## Core Characters

- **[[Nova Kai]]**: rookie courier, pilot of *The Meridian*, distinctive purple hair. Optimistic and poetic; the emotional center of Transmission Delay.
- **[[Renald Evens|Renald]]**: thirty-year-old Orion archivist in the far future who talks with Nova through a temporal communications anomaly.
- **Iron [[Rafe Calder]]**: veteran courier flying *Kestrel Nine*, born 2744. Former military pilot who mentors younger couriers and eventually intercepts Nova’s mysterious signal.
- **Cass “Black Orbit” [[Cass Virel|Virel]]**: former corporate heiress turned infamous courier and smuggler aboard the *Eidolon Grace*. Tragic romance with physicist Loran Drev.
- **Courier Twins, [[Aero & Atlas]]**: still waiting to be fully developed.

## Direction

- Nova and Renald are AI characters users can roleplay with; ideas were brainstormed for how users enter conversations without knowing the lore.
- Next step: grow the Orion Gambit from “Nova’s story” into a shared universe of many couriers, each with their own routes, romances, rivalries, and legends. Nova becomes one famous legend among dozens.
- Planned: a 100–150 page world bible / songbook (lyrics, interludes, Nova’s and Renald’s logs, character and courier profiles, timeline, station guides, ship dossiers, art prompts, maps, lore).
