# Return Burn

*Track 7 — hope, exhaustion, countdown to reunion*

**Tempo:** 90 BPM | soft propulsion beat, rhythmic bass pulse, hopeful but frayed tone
**Mood:** momentum with melancholy — determination through fatigue

### Verse 1

> Thrusters flare in silver lines,
> homebound, broke, out of time.
> The ship complains, my bones agree,
> space gets loud when it's just me.

### Verse 2

> I count the routes, the debts, the lies,
> the friends I left, the quiet goodbyes.
> Each beacon blinks a memory burned,
> each one whispers, *"no return."*

### Pre-Chorus

> Renald said "wait for me at Sol,"
> I set my course, I set my soul.

### Chorus

> Return burn, all systems ache,
> chasing ghosts for connection's sake.
> Return burn, my ship runs lean,
> carrying hope through the in-between.

### Verse 3

> Dock codes flicker, route unclean,
> my messages bounce off dead machines.
> One transmission loops his name,
> his voice distorted, never the same.

### Bridge (spoken / filtered)

> "Renald… if you're still out there…
> I'll be at the Sol relay. Two days. Don't be late."

*(low trumpet and heartbeat drums swell together)*

### Chorus (reprise)

> Return burn,
> stars blur into one long line.
> Return burn,
> I'll find you — or I'll lie.
