# Cold Cargo Run
*(the long, hungry haul)*

**Tempo:** 72 BPM | low, pulsing bass, icy synth pads, distant trumpet echoes
**Mood:** Isolation, fatigue, digital warmth in cold space

### Verse 1
> Frost on the dash, breath turns slow,
> heat core flickers, pale blue glow.
> The ship smells like metal and fear,
> I hum to fill the silent sphere.

### Verse 2
> Charts blur out, the nav drifts wide,
> hunger hums like static inside.
> The stars are beautiful, cruel, and dumb—
> they burn, they shine, they never come.

### Pre-Chorus
> Every ping a maybe, every crackle a sign,
> Renald's voice fades between the lines.

### Chorus
> Cold cargo run,
> half a tank and a setting sun.
> I dream of food that isn't paste,
> and warmth that isn't waste.
> Cold cargo run,
> I count the stars for one.

### Verse 3
> Docking logs say "Two more days,"
> I barter prayers for gamma rays.
> A flicker—his voice through static hum:
> "Nova, don't quit now… you're almost home."

### Bridge (spoken / layered static)
> "Renald… if you can hear me—
> tell me what color the sun is where you are."

### Final Chorus
> Cold cargo run,
> I chase ghosts through a dying sun.
> Cold cargo run,
> still pretending someone's on the other end.

**Outro:** instrumental – muted trumpet fading into ship hum
