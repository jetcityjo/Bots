# Echo Market Blues

*Track 3 — the hustler's orbit*

**Tempo:** 110 BPM, upright-bass riff, snare brushes, trumpet chatter
**Mood:** chaotic swing, sly humor

### Verse 1

> Neon tongues and counterfeit moons,
> traders bark in crooked tunes.
> I pawn my boots for docking rights,
> eat smoke and sound for nights on nights.

### Verse 2

> Bought a meal that never came,
> bartender charged me for the blame.
> A mimic laughed, then took my seat—
> said "you're learning, kid—repeat defeat."

### Chorus

> Sold my lunch for docking time,
> hunger's cheaper than a fine.
> Echo Market blues,
> and no one leaves on time.

### Verse 3

> A pirate preacher played the keys,
> blessing hustlers on their knees.
> I tipped him hope, he tipped me lies—
> "Girl, paradise is just supplies."

### Break / Trumpet Solo

### Bridge (spoken over bass groove)

> "Hey Riven—still alive. Barely.
> You'd hate this smell."

### Final Chorus

> Echo Market blues,
> I keep the change, I keep the bruise.
> And maybe someday when credits align,
> I'll buy back all that wasn't mine.
