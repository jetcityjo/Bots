# Dock 6, Bay 9

*Track 1 — the departure*

**Tempo:** 80 BPM, brushed drums, upright bass, muted trumpet
**Mood:** nervous hope, metallic romance

### Verse 1

> New badge, new route, breath held tight,
> ticket stamped for the endless night.
> The hangar hums, a thousand suns,
> blinking like they've just begun.

### Verse 2

> Ground crew waves in vapor mist,
> I kiss my luck, it doesn't kiss.
> Burn sequence sings, my hands still shake—
> the stars don't care what risks I take.

### Pre-Chorus

> They said it's easy once you leave,
> just drop, collect, don't dare believe.

### Chorus

> Dock 6, Bay 9,
> my pulse is a launch alarm.
> Goodbye gravity,
> hello empty charm.
> A message pings—soft, low, benign:
> "Hey rookie courier… don't get lost this time."

### Bridge (instrumental trumpet solo)

### Final Line (spoken)

> "Nova Kai—en route. Wish me luck."
