# Transmission Delay — Courier Log & Album Notes

A concept album following courier Nova Kai's journey through space, her growing connection with Renald across time, and the ultimate failure of their communication link.

## Page 1 — "Dock 6, Bay 9"
> They gave me a badge and a smile that didn't reach their eyes.
> Mars looks small from up here. So do promises.
> The ship hums like an old radio between stations.
> I'm scared. That feels honest.
> **Renald:** *"Don't get lost out there."*
> **Nova:** *"Define lost."*

## Page 2 — "Delivery to Luma-9"
> Luma-9 glows like a wound that learned to bloom.
> I keep your old message in a loop — the part where you said my voice sounded like *"dust before rain."*

"Still alive. You'd hate this smell." — Nova to Renald, delayed 14 hours.

## Page 3 — "Echo Market Blues"
> Bartered my boots for docking time. My feet are freezing but the ship's still breathing.

## Page 4 — "The Holo Diner at Titan Gate"
> The old courier told me, "The mail don't love your face."
> I watched Saturn's rings through the diner window and realized I've never actually seen home.

## Page 5 — "Cold Cargo Run"
> I count my breath between alarms.
> "Renald, if you can hear this… tell me what color the sun is where you are."
> *Transmission failed.*

## Page 6 — "The Smuggler's Port of Virel"
> The pay was real. The law wasn't.
> **Renald:** *"You sound… different."*
> **Nova:** *"You would too, if you'd been hungry this long."*

## Page 7 — "Night Transmission"
> **Renald:** "Still awake?"
> **Nova:** *"Define awake."*
> **Nova:** *"Don't promise what pixels can't keep."*
> *He didn't answer, but I could hear him breathe.*

## Page 8 — "Return Burn"
> Ship trembling. So am I.
> **Renald:** *"Two days. I'll be there."*
> I write it on my wrist with pen. So I can pretend it's a vow.

## Page 9 — "Message Failed to Send"
> Docked. Waited. Watched the sun.
> `MESSAGE FAILED TO SEND.`
> I whispered your name into the static just to see if it would echo. It didn't.

## Page 10 — Epilogue
> Rookie courier Nova Kai vanished from registry after Sol Relay Dock 9.
> Last logged activity: repeated ping to user *REN4LD.*
> *"Some messages never fail. They just arrive too late."*
