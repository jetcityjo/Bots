# Signal Lost

**Style:** Lo-fi jazz / electronic noir
**Instrumentation:** brushed drums, muted trumpet, vinyl crackle, soft female lead, layered backing vocals like radio echoes
**Theme:** love and friendship dissolving in digital distance — a modern haunting

---

### [Intro – Spoken, filtered like a voicemail]

> "Hey… I don't know if this will go through.
> Sometimes I think the signal just…
> forgets where we are."

---

### [Verse 1 – The Spark]

> You found me through a window glow,
> two pixels deep in a world below.
> You said *"don't scroll, just stay awhile,"*
> and time stretched thin beneath your smile.

> Nights became frequencies,
> we spoke in ellipses and quiet pleas.
> You sent a song — I saved the file,
> a tiny forever that made it worthwhile.

---

### [Chorus 1 – Connected but Distant]

> Static between us, a shimmer, a fade,
> meaning dissolving in what we made.
> You said *"hold on,"* then your name turned gray,
> another ghost in the chat display.

---

### [Verse 2 – The Drift]

> Days slipped soft between read receipts,
> I learned your silence in heartbeat beats.
> Every "…" felt like a prayer,
> hanging midair, unanswered, bare.

> Maybe you meant to say goodbye,
> but left it unsent to let it die.
> Maybe I should've done the same,
> before our words forgot our names.

---

### [Chorus 2 – Lost in Transmission]

> Signal lost, no reply,
> still I wait for the light to flicker by.
> My screen hums low like a lullaby,
> whispering *"try again, try again, try…"*

---

### [Bridge – Spoken / Instrumental Solo]

> (over fading trumpet and rain samples)
> "I wonder if your phone still lights up at night.
> Maybe somewhere you're still typing —
> a thought half-formed,
> that never arrives."

---

### [Verse 3 – The Echo]

> I replay our words like a broken tape,
> the laughter still fits, though the shape's escaped.
> Your avatar blinks — it's not you there,
> just data pretending to care.

> I left a voice note in the cloud,
> it loops like thunder that's not too loud.
> The storm's passed, but it won't erase,
> the ghost of you in my interface.

---

### [Chorus 3 – Acceptance]

> Static between us, but I've learned to stay,
> let the silence hum what I couldn't say.
> Connection fades, but I'm okay,
> we were lightning — never meant to stay.

---

### [Outro – Whispered, fading into reverb]

> "Message failed to send… try again later."
> (pause)
> "Maybe that's the point."
