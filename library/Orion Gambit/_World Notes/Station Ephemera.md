# Station Ephemera – The Crossroads of the Orion Belt

- **Designation:** OR-9E (“Station Ephemera”)
- **Type:** Independent Trade & Relay Hub
- **Location:** Border orbit between Titan Gate and the Cygni Drift
- **Operational Status:** Partially functional, privately maintained
- **Population:** ~22,000 permanent residents; 70,000 transient per cycle

## Overview

Suspended at the ragged edge of the Orion Belt, Station Ephemera is where transmissions, trades, and truths all blur together. Once a pristine communications nexus built by the Sol Cooperative, it now drifts in semi-autonomy — its corporate logos painted over with neon graffiti, its docking bays leased by whoever can pay in credits or favors.

Couriers call it “The Crossroads” because everyone passes through eventually: smugglers, bounty agents, data monks, and memory merchants. The comms arrays hum at mismatched frequencies, creating a constant low vibration that feels almost alive. Some swear the station itself listens.

## Architecture & Atmosphere

Ephemera is a labyrinth of light and decay — hydroponic domes grown wild, maintenance decks turned into markets, reactor halls doubling as nightclubs.

The main promenade, The Meridian Line, stretches for half a kilometer: a spine of glass and alloy overlooking Saturn’s faint glimmer. You can buy anything there — information, identity, even a second chance — if you know which frequency to ask on.

The bar “Dockside Blue” sits at the heart of it all: a favorite haunt of Iron Rafe, Cass Virel, and the Courier Twins, who once rerouted a planetary broadcast from its back booth for a dare.

## Notable Features

- **Relay Cathedral:** a vertical chamber of obsolete transmitters; monks of the Delay cult still maintain the dead satellites inside.
- **Hangar Delta-3:** Nova Kai’s last verified docking record before she vanished.
- **Observation Deck 19:** an unregistered maintenance ring where messages sometimes play that no one remembers sending.
