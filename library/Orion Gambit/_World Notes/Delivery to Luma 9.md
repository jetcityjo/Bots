# Delivery to Luma-9

*Track 2 — the first job goes wrong*

**Tempo:** 88 BPM, lazy swing with electric-piano shimmer
**Mood:** bright lights / bad day

### Verse 1

> Biolight drips from the swamp-sky trees,
> corporate labs hum, allergic to me.
> "Sign right here," the scientist sneers,
> his gloves worth more than my year's arrears.

### Verse 2

> My boots sink deep in the profit mud,
> cargo leaks a violet flood.
> He scans my tag, rejects my name,
> says, "rookie couriers are all the same."

### Chorus

> And I laugh through clenched design,
> "Guess rain don't count as fine."
> You can't refund time,
> on Luma-9.

### Verse 3

> The receipt won't post, the system's late,
> I hum old Earth tunes and curse the gate.
> A beetle-drone steals half my pay—
> must be Tuesday, light-years away.

### Breakdown (half-tempo)

> Fuel low, credits thin,
> I message him again—
> *"Still alive. You'd hate this smell."*

### Outro (chorus reprise)

> You can't refund time,
> on Luma-9.
