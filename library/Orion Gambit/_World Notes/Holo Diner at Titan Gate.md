# The Holo Diner at Titan Gate

*Track 4 — meeting the veteran*

**Tempo:** 76 BPM, steel-guitar synth, slow swing
**Mood:** melancholy wisdom, cosmic country-jazz

### Verse 1

> Neon syrup, coffee black,
> glass booths hum with data crack.
> I sit beside a freight-line ghost,
> steam and silence make the toast.

### Verse 2

> He says, "First run? You've got that shine.
> Give it a month—turn nickel to brine."
> His badge is older than the wars,
> his eyes still track departing doors.

### Chorus

> He stirred his coffee, black as space,
> said "the mail don't love your face."
> "Don't trust the quiet jobs," he sighed,
> "They'll eat your soul from the inside."

### Verse 3

> I asked his name; he grinned, "Call me None.
> Names burn up before they're done."
> He left a tip of cosmic dust—
> I left with caffeine and mistrust.

### Bridge (soft trumpet + slide synth)

> Somewhere Riven's message waits,
> I replay it between the plates.
> "Show me the stars," he'd said one night—
> guess this diner's close to light.

### Final Chorus

> He stirred his coffee, black as space,
> advice that time can't erase.
> I fly again with what he gave:
> a map of warnings, and a wave.

### Outro (spoken, comm-filter)

> "Courier Nova Kai departing Titan Gate… destination unknown."
