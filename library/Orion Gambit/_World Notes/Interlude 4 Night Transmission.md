# Interlude 4 — "Night Transmission"

*(between tracks 6 and 7)*

**Length:** ~45 seconds
**Mood:** half-asleep, quiet hum of a ship drifting; soft static like gentle rain
**SFX:** ship creaks, digital ping; Renald's voice comes through distorted.

> **Renald:** "Still awake?"
> **Nova:** "Define awake."
> *(soft laugh)*
> **Renald:** "You sound far."
> **Nova:** "I am far."
> *(pause)*
> **Renald:** "Two more runs, right?"
>
> **Nova:** "Yeah… two. Then I'm inbound."
> *(beat)*
> **Renald:** "Then I'll meet you. Promise."
> **Nova:** "…don't promise what pixels can't keep."

*The signal flickers, then cuts. Trumpet fades in like a lullaby over a heartbeat synth.*
