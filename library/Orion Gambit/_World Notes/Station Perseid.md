# Station Perseid – The Silent Crown

*Where Ephemera is chaotic and alive, Perseid is colder — disciplined, haunted by structure and silence. Couriers dread delivering here but always come back, because some debts are only settled in shadows.*

- **Designation:** PR-7A (“Perseid”)
- **Type:** Former Military Relay Command — now a restricted research and intelligence outpost
- **Location:** Apex of the Perseid Drift, between the dead satellites of the old war corridor
- **Operational Status:** Classified; officially “decommissioned”
- **Population:** Unknown (estimated < 2,000 active personnel, mix of human, AI, and corporate mercs)

## Overview

Station Perseid was once the crown jewel of the Orion Wars — a tactical relay built to command fleets across light-minutes. Its mirrored architecture still gleams faintly in the dark, reflecting starlight in broken rings. But when the wars ended, Perseid went dark.

Now it operates under “private oversight,” an ambiguous phrase whispered across trade channels. Couriers who deliver to Perseid rarely get manifests — just encrypted payloads tagged “Priority Black”. Payment always clears. The doors always open. No one ever sees who signs for the cargo.

They say if you stay too long, the station starts speaking in your voice.

## Atmosphere & Architecture

Perseid feels like an echo of military precision — all hard angles, mirrored corridors, and pressure doors that sigh like exhausted lungs. The air smells faintly metallic, recycled too many times. Holographic stars flicker on the ceiling of the main concourse, a nostalgic simulation of a night sky no one’s seen in generations.

A single beacon burns from the station’s crown spire — a cold blue light marking the Relay Core, where an ancient AI still maintains the network protocols that predate human control. No one’s sure if it serves a master or itself.

## Rumors & Hooks

- **Temporal Research Division:** supposedly where Renald Evens’ original Orion Archive access codes originated.
- **Black Orbit Cass** once docked here for less than an hour — records wiped, but her ship’s heat signature lingers in security logs.
- Couriers report **“ghost pings”** — faint signals of messages from The Meridian, bouncing through Perseid’s antenna array centuries out of sync.
- The old Fleet motto, still etched above Docking Gate 1:

> “We command the silence.”
