# Postcards from the Void

### Intro – Radio Static / Spoken
> "Route 9 to Vega Gate—signal clear.
> Cargo: correspondence, human emotion, assorted dreams.
> Destination: everywhere but home."

### Verse 1 – Departure
> The engines hum like an old love song,
> I trace the routes where I don't belong.
> A million names drift through my feed,
> I just deliver what they need.

> Packets of hope, wrapped tight in code,
> longing sent down a data road.
> Each pulse of light a whispered plea,
> *someone out there, remember me.*

### Chorus 1 – Wanderer's Call
> I'm a courier in the stars,
> ferrying hearts between the scars.
> I read their words, then let them go,
> but none of them ever write *hello*.

### Verse 2 – Transit
> Saturn sleeps in a haloed ring,
> I drop the mail and don't feel a thing.
> But sometimes I pause where the comets bloom,
> to feel the hum of an empty room.

### Chorus 2 – Interference
> I'm a courier in the stars,
> chasing pings through dying quasars.
> I send my name into the blue,
> waiting for the line to echo *you*.

### Bridge – Instrumental / Spoken
> "If you hear this, don't answer.
> Just think of me between transmissions.
> That's enough."

### Verse 3 – Ghost Frequency
> Once, a beacon blinked my name,
> three tones, a half-forgotten flame.
> I slowed the ship, I held my breath—
> it was only feedback, only death.

### Final Chorus – Resolution
> I'm a courier in the stars,
> trading routes for fading bars.
> The heart I send, the love I hide—
> both waiting on the same reply.

### Outro – Distorted / Fading
> "Courier 47… transmission complete."
