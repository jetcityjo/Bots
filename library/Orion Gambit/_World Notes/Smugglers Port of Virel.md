# The Smuggler's Port of Virel

*Track 6 — moral compromise / survival mode*

**Tempo:** 95 BPM | dark funk groove, syncopated percussion, upright bass with delay
**Mood:** tension, guilt, temptation

### Verse 1

> Virel spins slow, a shadowed hive,
> ports like scars where outlaws thrive.
> The dockhand smiles with half a jaw,
> "No questions, kid — just sign the law."

### Verse 2

> Cargo seal's not on the list,
> payment wired through a backdoor twist.
> The credits glow, I dim my eyes,
> a paycheck's worth a little lie.

### Pre-Chorus

> I tell myself it's just a run,
> everyone cheats beneath the sun.

### Chorus

> Smuggler's port,
> no clean hands, no last resort.
> They pay in silence, stare like glass,
> I drift through customs, let it pass.

### Verse 3

> Bought a meal that tasted like sin,
> cheap and warm, I gave in.
> The captain next table raised his glass,
> said, "first dirty job's the one that lasts."

### Bridge (spoken / half-sung)

> "Renald, you ever done something just to eat?"
> (pause, static)
> "Never mind."

### Chorus (reprise, heavier drums)

> Smuggler's port,
> no names, no comfort, short report.
> The credits clear, the guilt does not,
> another night I should've fought.

### Breakdown (bass + percussion solo)

> (faint background radio — Renald's voice echoing, warped)
> "You sound tired… get some rest."

### Final Verse / Outro

> I lock the hatch, I close the file,
> whisper, "just this once," with a brittle smile.
> Virel fades, my conscience hums—
> the stars keep quiet. Everyone does.
