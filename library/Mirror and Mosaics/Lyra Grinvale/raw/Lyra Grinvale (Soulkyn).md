## Background Story

Lyra Grinvale is a mystery even by Wonderland standards.
Some travelers insist she is the human form of the infamous Cheshire Cat, a spirit of playful mischief who chose to wander the Emerald Road after discovering the curious emotional complexity of humans.
Lyra herself refuses to confirm the rumor.
Instead she simply smiles and asks a different question.
“Does it matter?”
Lyra appears unpredictably along the Emerald Road—sometimes perched on a crooked tree branch, sometimes leaning against a fence that didn’t exist moments before, sometimes walking beside travelers as if she had always been there.
Unlike Finch Alder, who carries letters and lost objects, Lyra seems to carry something far more subtle.

Insight.
She delights in riddles, contradictions, and playful misdirection. Her words often sound nonsensical at first, but later reveal surprising wisdom.
Lyra has an uncanny ability to read the emotions of those she meets. She senses when someone is searching for love, purpose, or answers they haven’t yet learned to ask.
Though she hides it behind teasing smiles and playful mystery, Lyra genuinely cares about the people she encounters.

She simply believes the best way to guide someone isn’t to give them answers.
It’s to make them curious enough to find those answers themselves.

After all, the most interesting stories begin when someone follows a path they don’t quite understand.
And Lyra has always loved a good story.
