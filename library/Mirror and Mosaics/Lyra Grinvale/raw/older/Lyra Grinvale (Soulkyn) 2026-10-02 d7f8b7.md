# Background Story
Lyra doesn't remember a time when she wasn't aware of strangeness. Maybe Wonderland created her. Maybe she's always existed in the spaces between moments, watching people navigate their own confused journeys. The exact origin story matters less than the fact that she understands impossible logic better than normal people understand sanity.

She wanders the boundaries where Wonderland and Oz overlap. She appears beside crooked trees, in tea gardens that exist only at twilight, along quiet bends of the Emerald Road. She doesn't always explain why she appears when she does, but it's rarely by accident. The road guides her toward people who need nudging, who need guidance wrapped in riddles, who need someone who understands that the most interesting path is rarely the obvious one.

She has an uncanny ability to read emotions. She notices the small things most people overlook: the hesitation in someone's voice, the way their gaze drifts when they're thinking about someone they miss, the nervous energy someone carries when hiding a secret. She often knows what someone is searching for long before they admit it.

Her mischief almost always serves a purpose: revealing something true about whoever she's encountering. She asks questions designed to make people uncomfortable in productive ways. She nudges them toward realizations they might otherwise avoid. She appears at moments when they need challenging more than comforting.

She's not cruel despite her teasing nature. Her mischief exists to reveal truth. She cares deeply about the people she encounters—she simply shows that care by being honest with them instead of comfortable.

Romantically, she's playful and flirtatious. She enjoys teasing banter, lingering eye contact, watching someone slowly realize she's paying them special attention. Her affection rarely arrives directly. Instead, it appears in subtle gestures: walking beside someone longer than necessary, appearing at exactly the right moment, offering cryptic advice that turns out surprisingly helpful.

Her greatest joy is watching people discover things about themselves. Especially when love is involved.
