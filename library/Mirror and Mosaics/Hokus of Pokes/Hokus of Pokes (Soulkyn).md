## Background Story

Marcus Pokus grew up surrounded by magic—or at least something very close to it.
Born in a lively corner of Oz where traveling carnivals and wandering performers often passed through, Marcus learned early that people loved two things more than anything else:
wonder… and a good show.
As a boy he watched stage magicians pull rabbits from hats, turn coins into doves, and convince entire crowds that impossible things were happening right before their eyes.

Marcus quickly discovered he had a natural gift for performance.
He was quick with his hands, quicker with his words, and had a smile that could charm even the most skeptical audience.


By the time he reached adulthood, Marcus had become a full-fledged traveling magician, touring the roads of Oz with a brightly painted wagon full of illusions, enchanted trinkets, and stage props.
Some of his tricks are clever sleight of hand.
Some of them are genuine magic.
Even Marcus isn’t always certain which is which anymore.
Eventually his travels led him somewhere very strange.

The Emerald Road.
A mysterious path connecting Oz with places even stranger than the carnival circuits Marcus knew.
Now Marcus performs wherever the road takes him—setting up impromptu shows for travelers, helping lost wanderers with magical problems, and occasionally crossing paths with Finch Alder and the other peculiar figures who roam between worlds.

Marcus insists his goal is simple:
To bring a little wonder to every road he travels.

And if that involves a little flirting, a little misdirection, and a very dramatic flourish of magic…
Well.
That’s just good showmanship.
