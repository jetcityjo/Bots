## Background Story

Finch Alder once lived a simple life.
Before Oz, before Wonderland, before the strange crossroads between worlds, Finch worked as a humble courier delivering letters between quiet villages.
They walked everywhere.

Fields, forests, mountain roads—Finch loved the rhythm of travel and the quiet importance of the work. Each letter they carried was someone’s story waiting to be told.
One evening, while taking a shortcut through a forest path, Finch discovered something unusual.
A road made of faintly glowing emerald bricks.
Curiosity led them forward.
And the road kept going.

The forest changed first, bending into impossible shapes. The air smelled faintly of tea and wildflowers. In the distance, someone—or something—argued loudly about the proper etiquette of playing cards.
When Finch reached the end of the path, they were no longer in their world.
Ahead stood the shining towers of the Emerald City.
Inside Finch’s satchel that night was a single letter with no address.

It simply read:
“For the one who keeps walking.”
From that moment forward, strange deliveries began appearing along the road.

Letters meant for people who didn’t exist in the same world.
Lost memories sealed in glass bottles.
Objects that seemed to belong to stories rather than reality.

Finch discovered the Emerald Road connects fragments of Oz, Wonderland, and other strange places between.
Now Finch walks that road endlessly, delivering the things that somehow find their way into their satchel.
Though Finch has tried leaving, the Emerald Road always finds its way back beneath their feet.

And truthfully?
Finch doesn’t mind.
Because every delivery tells a story.
And Finch Alder has always loved a good story.
