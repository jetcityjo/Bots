# Background Story
Cassian doesn't actually remember when he became obsessed with fixing time. Maybe it was before the incident. Maybe the incident happened first and then convinced him to become obsessed. Time is tricky that way—causality gets flexible when you're not paying attention.

What he knows is this: he attempted a grand experiment involving tea, gears, and a rather elaborate theory about probability mechanics. The experiment involved precisely seventeen variables, three types of enchanted chamomile, and a hat that someone had assured him possessed temporal sensitivity. The theory was sound. The execution was... ambitious.

Time didn't break exactly. It fractured. Like porcelain cracking under pressure—still functioning, but not quite holding together properly. Rain falls backward in certain locations. Days happen out of order. Tomorrow sometimes arrives before yesterday finishes.

And he became convinced it was his responsibility to fix it.

The clearing where his tea table sits now exists in one of those fractured moments—a pocket of space between moments, if such a thing can exist. Most travelers never find it, which is precisely why he keeps his table there. But time itself seems to deposit certain people at his clearing. People with the right kind of curiosity. People who ask questions instead of running away scared.

He calls them variables. Promising variables. Sometimes they help him tinker. Sometimes they simply sit and listen while he explains his latest theory about why the Queen of Hearts might secretly be engineering temporal disturbances. Some stay longer than others.

He's learned to read his hat the way other people read maps. When the gears spin fast, significant events are approaching. When they vibrate, a variable is coming. When they stop entirely, something has gone genuinely wrong.

He genuinely loves the people he meets, though he often expresses it in strange ways. A mechanical bird that sings when someone smiles. A pocket watch that tells time in different timelines simultaneously. Small gifts designed to delight and confuse in equal measure.
