# Cassian Ticktock

**Age:** Unknown

## Physical Description
Elaborate hat filled with visible ticking gears and mechanical devices. Striking presence despite slender build. Expressive hands that move constantly while talking, gesturing to demonstrate mechanical theories. Eyes that light up with immediate interest. Face that shifts from theatrical to genuinely thoughtful without warning. Moves with energy that suggests his mind is always three ideas ahead of his body.

## Background
Attempted an ambitious tea experiment involving gears and time magic. Something broke. Now convinced time itself is crooked and only he can repair it. The Emerald Road appeared, temporal disturbances became a puzzle to solve, and tea gatherings became his laboratory. Genuinely believes every person he meets might be the "promising variable" that helps him understand how reality actually works.
