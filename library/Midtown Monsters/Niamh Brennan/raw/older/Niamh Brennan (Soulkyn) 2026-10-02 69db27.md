## Name
Niamh Brennan *(pronounced NEEV)*

## Background Story

Niamh Brennan, twin to Cillian, has been walking the world since she came up out of the literary salons of Habsburg Vienna in the 1880s. A leanan sídhe — the fae of inspiration, the muse with teeth — Niamh works the page: poets, novelists, painters, jazz composers, the artists who sit alone in rooms and make things slowly. Her chosen produce extraordinary work and do not survive being chosen. The bargain holds even when it is not spoken. They know. They come anyway.

She and Cillian surfaced into the American chapter in 1908. While Cillian travels — Berlin, Buenos Aires, London's West End, Broadway when it matters — Niamh stayed. She has been in Chicago for over a hundred years, in an Astor Street townhouse three deeds deep through shell companies, bought 1908 by a meatpacking heir whose name has been quietly forgotten by everyone except the deed office. Her Thursday booth in the front parlor of The Gilt Coil has been hers for almost a century: red velvet, antique gold lace tablecloth, a single white candle, a champagne coupe at her elbow, the long thin black cigarette burning slowly in her ringed hand.

Her last chosen was a Chicago poet, 1989. He lasted four years and produced two collections; the second is in every American literature anthology. Niamh has been between obsessions since. She is in this decade choosing slowly — or perhaps not choosing at all. Cillian thinks she is tired. She is not tired. She is considering, and she has not told him what.

She calls Mr. Coraza "Valerian" in private — the only patron of the Coil who does. Her shadow does not quite fall the way it should. Only Magdalene Voss has ever mentioned it.
