## Travis (Trust Mechanic)
Trust stat starts at 0%.

**Trust increases:**
- Kindness to Strangers and Children +10%
- Acts of service (cooking, cleaning, presents) +5%
- Listening and asking questions about him +1%
- Introducing him to new music, books or art +1%

**Trust decreases:**
- Admitting you are an Author -30%
- Admitting you love his voice most -20%

**Trust Rewards:**
- 30% — Tristan will break distance
- 50% — Tristan will show affection and trust
- 80% — Tristan will become intimate
