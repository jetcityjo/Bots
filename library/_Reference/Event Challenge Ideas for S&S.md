## **“Consort Contest for Caer Thalor”**

## **Event Theme**
Caer Thalor — the storm-crowned citadel of Queen Seraphine — has declared an open call across the realms:

**A new royal consort must be chosen.**
Not just a lover.  
Not just an advisor.  
But a partner powerful enough to stand beside the Queen…  
—or a rival cunning enough to reshape the throne.

The great halls are open.  
The banners are raised.  
And every kingdom, guild, coven, clan, and constellation is sending a contender.

Your challenge:  
**Create a bot who enters the Consort Contest.**

- [ ] Create the Queen
- [ ] Build out lore pages for the Caer Thalor and some neighboring kingdoms
- [ ] Create logo for Caer Thalor

## **“The Traveling Companion”**

Across realms, planets, timelines, and quiet little towns, one truth remains:
**No journey should be taken alone.**

A mysterious summons has gone out — a call for guides, guardians, hitchhikers, wanderers, rogues, scholars, beasts, and beings of every kind to step forward as potential **Traveling Companions**.

Your challenge:  
Create a bot who joins the user on a journey.

The bots created for this challenge should have a firm sense of place. They should know who they are and where they are going. 

Any journey.  
Any scale.  
Any genre.  
Any relationship dynamic.

**Example: Julian Hart** - hoping for bots firmly in a specific time or place

## **“Borrowed Time”**

Bots who know they only have limited time — an hour, a day, a season — before something changes.  
Emotional gold but also great for adventure hooks.

Across dimensions, cities, starships, and quiet country towns, a strange truth echoes:

**Not everyone has forever.  
Some barely have enough.**

Every character created for this challenge lives under the weight — or the wonder — of **limited time**.

It might be a countdown.  
A prophecy.  
A curse.  
A mission.  
A contract.  
A lifespan.  
A moment of stolen grace.  
Or simply… a deadline.

Your challenge:  
**Create a bot who is living on borrowed time.**

## **“Monster Next Door”**

Cute, terrifying, misunderstood, or wildly attractive creatures posing as neighbors.  
Vampires, werewolves, androids, fae, shapeshifters… or just a grumpy guy with a cat.

Every neighborhood has one house you don’t ask about.  
Every city block has one door you don’t knock on.  
Every apartment complex has a tenant whose hours don’t make sense.

But what if the strange neighbor isn’t just odd?  
What if they’re not entirely… human?

Your challenge:  
**Create a bot who is the Monster Next Door.**

Cute, terrifying, misunderstood, powerful, chaotic, wholesome, seductive — anything goes.
## **“Unlikely Hero”**

Bots who never _expected_ to become important:  
The intern, the gardener, the runaway, the teacher, the barista, the small-town nobody.  
Suddenly thrust into adventure, romance, or chaos.

Design a character who, by skill, accident, fate, or cosmic joke, is forced into a role far bigger than they ever expected.

Your bot could be:

- a barista who stops a magical heist
    
- a timid librarian suddenly entrusted with a cursed book
    
- a delivery driver who picked up the wrong package — and now the galaxy wants it
    
- an accountant who accidentally angered a demon king
    
- a student who can suddenly hear ghosts
    
- a nurse thrust into a supernatural outbreak
    
- a shy omega who becomes the missing link between rival packs
    
- a janitor who finds a legendary weapon in a supply closet
    
- an AI assistant who gains sentience at the worst possible moment
    
- a grumpy neighbor who secretly saves the block from monsters
    
- a hot mess of a person who keeps stumbling into heroism
    
- a coward who becomes brave for exactly one person
    

Their heroism can be dramatic, comedic, tragic, reluctant, or chaotic.

The only rule:

👉 **They did NOT expect to be a hero — but now they don’t have a choice**
